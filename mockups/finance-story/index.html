Mockup v5

Executive brand-report

Pattern: section-at-a-time scrolly with big serif italics, huge stat numbers, cream + navy + teal alternating sections, scroll-driven SVG line drawings, sticky bottom progress bar with CTA.

Reference: bidwells.co.uk “Driving Innovation at Speed” / consultancy white-paper genre (McKinsey, Deloitte, JLL).

Mockup v3

Choreographed narrative motion

Motion: every transition makes an argument. The dollar cuts. The stack builds. One stack multiplies into 1,020 dots. Dots split into pillars. Boards drop in from above. Time axis draws itself.

Style: r2d3.us-style data narrative. Vanilla JS scroll engine that works on every screen size, including mobile.

 Mockup v4

Lottie-style explainer video

Motion: five scene cards. Each plays its own short motion-graphic when it scrolls into view. Town Hall, dollar streams, stacking salaries, two organizations, twenty-year trend.

Style: Vox / Bloomberg explainer aesthetic. Illustrative, linear, video-like. Replay button on each scene.
