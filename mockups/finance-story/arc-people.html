Mockup B · Humanizing

About a thousand people work for Marblehead.

Start with the people. Walk back to the budget. Same material, opposite direction.

Brainstorming mockup. Numbers approximate.

Step 1 · The workforce

Each dot is one person on Marblehead's payroll.

Schools instructional, schools support, police, fire, DPW, town admin, library, recreation, council on aging. Different jobs, different titles, one tax base behind all of them.

Step 2 · Who's who

The school side is twice the size of the town side.

626 school-side. 394 town-side. Inside the school number, instructional staff (teachers, special-ed staff, related services) is the largest single group at about 460 FTE.

Inside the town number, public safety, DPW, town administration, and the library/recreation/COA cluster split the work.

Step 3 · What each one costs

An average $75K salary is closer to $100K of budget.

Every paid position carries a stack on top of the base salary: pension contribution to Essex Regional, the town's share of the GIC health premium, Medicare, workers' comp, life.

The stack is contractually and statutorily fixed. When GIC raises premiums, every line on the payroll rises.

Step 4 · The aggregate

About 80% of the budget is total compensation.

Multiply ~1,020 people times their loaded cost. That's the dominant line of the entire operating budget. Set bills (debt, OPEB, state assessments) make up most of the rest. Discretionary spending is small.

Step 5 · Who reports to whom

Two parallel pillars. They don't connect at the top.

Town side: Select Board hires Town Administrator who manages town departments. School side: School Committee hires Superintendent who manages schools. Voters elect both boards directly.

FinCom, appointed by the Town Moderator, reviews both sides before Town Meeting votes.

Step 6 · Above the payroll

Above every paid employee is an unpaid resident who decides.

Roughly sixty residents serve on the elected and appointed boards: Select Board, School Committee, FinCom, Planning, Conservation, ZBA, Board of Health, Recreation, Light Board, Library Trustees, Moderator.

They write the warrant. They set zoning. They recommend the budget. They hire the senior managers. They take no salary.

Step 7 · Over time

The total stayed flat. The mix moved.

Special-education staff grew with student needs. IT was built from scratch. Clerical compressed. Public safety and DPW held roughly steady. Cost per FTE grew faster than CPI in nearly every year.
