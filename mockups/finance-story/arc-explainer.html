Mockup v4 · Motion-graphic explainer

How a dollar moves through Marblehead.

Five short scenes. Each scene plays its own short animation once when you scroll past it, then settles on a final image. Linear, video-like, not scroll-coupled.

Brainstorming mockup. Numbers approximate.

Scene 1

A dollar arrives at Town Hall.

About 75¢ of Marblehead's annual operating revenue comes from the property-tax levy. The other 25¢ comes from state aid and local receipts. All of it lands here.

Scene 2

Town Hall splits it three ways.

About 80¢ ends up as compensation: salary plus the automatic stack of benefits on every employee. About 15¢ is set bills the town doesn't control: debt service, OPEB, state assessments. The remaining 5¢ is supplies, contractors, programs, capital.

Scene 3

Every salary carries an automatic stack.

Pension (~17% of pay) goes to Essex Regional Retirement. The town pays ~83% of the GIC health-insurance premium. Then Medicare. Then workers' comp and life. Multiply by ~1,020 paid positions.

Scene 4

Two organizations, one tax base.

The town side runs from the Select Board through the Town Administrator to the departments. The school side runs from the School Committee through the Superintendent to the schools. Voters elect both boards directly. Neither pillar supervises the other.

Scene 5

The budget grew 2.7×. Headcount didn't.

Twenty-one years. Operating budget went from $47M to $127M. Total FTE moved within a 200-position band the entire time. The growth came from cost per employee, mostly the GIC health premium.
