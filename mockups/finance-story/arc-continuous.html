Mockup A v2 · Continuous motion

Marblehead spends $127 million a year.
Where does it go?

The same arc as Mockup A, rebuilt so the visualization morphs continuously as you scroll instead of swapping between fixed frames. One thousand twenty dots persist the whole way through, taking different shapes as the story unfolds.

Brainstorming mockup. Numbers approximate.

Step 1 · A dollar

Start with a single dollar from your tax bill.

About 75¢ of every dollar Marblehead collects comes from the property-tax levy. The rest is state aid and local receipts. Same destination.

Keep scrolling to follow this dollar.

Step 2 · The three shapes

Almost every dollar pays a person.

The dollar split into three shapes. About 80¢ of the operating budget is total compensation: salary plus the automatic stack of benefits on top. About 15¢ is set bills the town doesn't control: debt service, state assessments, OPEB. The remaining 5¢ is supplies, contractors, programs, and capital.

Step 3 · The stack on every person

A $75K hire is closer to $100K to the budget.

Every salary line carries an automatic stack: pension contribution to Essex Regional (~17% of pay), the town's share of the GIC health premium, Medicare, workers' comp, life. About 1.33× the base salary, on average.

When the GIC raises premiums, every line on the payroll rises. That's why the budget can grow even with headcount flat.

Step 4 · How many people

About 1,020 people work for the town and schools.

FY26 figures. Roughly 626 on the school side, 394 on the town side. Counts have hovered between 1,020 and 1,210 for two decades.

Each dot on screen is one full-time-equivalent position.

Step 5 · The hierarchy

All of them report up to one of two elected boards.

The town side runs through the Select Board to the Town Administrator to the departments. The school side runs through the School Committee to the Superintendent to the schools. The two pillars share a tax base. Not much else.

Step 6 · The unpaid layer

The decision layer is unpaid.

About sixty residents serve on the elected and appointed boards around the paid workforce: Select Board, School Committee, FinCom, Planning, Conservation, ZBA, Board of Health, Recreation, Light Board, Library Trustees, Moderator.

None of them draws a salary for it.

Step 7 · Twenty years

The total stayed flat. The mix moved.

School support staff grew with special-education needs. IT was created from zero. Clerical compressed. Public safety and DPW stayed within their long-run ranges. Per-FTE loaded cost grew faster than CPI in nearly every year.
