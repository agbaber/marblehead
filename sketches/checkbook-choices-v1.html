A choice still to be made

Snow Removal is +$582K over its FY26 appropriation (654% overrun)

Town Meeting voted the original snow appropriation last spring; this winter cost more than planned. Under MA law, an overrun has to be closed by a supplemental appropriation at a Special Town Meeting. The vote isn't “should we have plowed” — the plowing already happened. It's about which other line gets reduced, or whether free cash absorbs it.

About this sketch

Mockup illustrating the “choices” reframe of /checkbook/. Bucket percentages are rough estimates from the Jun 9 ledger snapshot; specific line items inside each bucket are pulled from the actual fund totals but the article/vote tags (e.g. “ATM 2025, Article 33”) are placeholders — the real mapping table is not yet built. The “contractual qualifier” carve-out inside the Town Meeting bucket is real and worth surfacing properly; it's roughly GIC + PERAC + state assessments together. Snow Removal overrun number from the existing live page.

Things that would change vs. today: 15,364-row data table demoted to a collapse below the fold. “Top vendors” ranked-bar list dropped (top vendors are misleading without slicing — Commonwealth of MA and Berkshire Wind dominate as pass-through and ratepayer dollars, neither of which match a resident's mental model of “vendor we paid”). “Budget vs actual” with 5 toggles dropped — replaced by the single choice-bucketed stacked bar plus the Snow Removal overrun callout, which is the only meaningful budget-vs-actual story this year.
