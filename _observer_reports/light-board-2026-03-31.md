OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Name of Committee: Marblehead Municipal Light
Meeting Date: 3/31/26
LWVM Observer: Maggie Smist

Members in Attendance:
Jean Jacques Yarmoff, Michael Hull, Simon Frechette, Matt Harrington and
Adam Smith
John Blair: General Manager

Call to Order at 4:02 pm

Land acknowledgement read

There is one question from the public about an issue in Revere where there was
an accident at a sub-station. Chairman Yarmoff said they are aware of the
issue, and the GM’s in the area are learning and sharing information.

Board Meetings: Calendar of next meetings; Next meeting on April 28.

Prior GM Legal complaint defense organization, Vote At the March 9 meeting,
it was decided to hire an attorney. An attorney has been secured (Atty Eckard)
and next steps are to reply to complaint and there has been a request for an
extension of the deadline to reply. It is time consuming to gather the board
every time there is a request on this issue. The Chair asked for a vote for either
GM or Chair of Commission to respond to requests from the attorney so that the
entire board does not have to meet each time there is a response needed.
Vote passed. There was a request for actions to be reported to the full
Commission as responses are made by representative.

Voluntary payment to Town, Vote There was a calculation for kwh sold of
102,469 because there was a surplus in the account – this happens each year.
Approximately $368,88 will be given to the town after a vote was made to pass
this amount on to town.

There was a question as to why it goes back to general fund in town, and why it
can’t it be designated for a specific fund. Chairman Hull has asked and will
continue to pursue this question as the board feels they have not received a
satisfactory answer from the town.

Updated Public Charging Policy/rates, Vote

EV charging update: the Board discussed current rate structure and the
changes proposed at last meeting. There is a different rate for public and
municipal customers and there is a time of use charge.

Response to question from prior meeting:

   -    It would be a large administrative burden to have a different rate for out-
       of-town use.
   -   It is also difficult to have a tiered structure.
   -    No overstay fees for overnight charging will be implemented.

Question was raised about re-adjusting the policy at a future time if the motion
passes today and the answer is YES, it is possible to do that. Vote passed.

Go Green Rate review, Vote

Marblehead’s power portfolio is 75% non-carbon. The GM spoke about the Go
Green Now Rate rider and the new rates for the Go Green Rates in 2026. These
rates fluctuate quarterly. Motion proposed and passed on the new GG rate.

General Manager Goals and Objectives to September 2026: update based on
strategic plan

Old GM goals were carried forward for GM Blair and new goals have been
drafted and reviewed by GM but the Board has not had the chance to review --
it will be discussed at April meeting.
General Manager updates

Safety Minute was discussed around Dig Safe.

Utility corridor near Lead Mills down to Bessom Street needs paving and the
proposal and phases were discussed. This will affect the rail trail and the water
and sewer department. There will be ongoing coordination between the two
departments. No need for approval but GM wanted to bring it to the attention
of the board. There were questions about who is responsible for various parts of
the project like excavation and securing the tracks. The GM referenced a town
law and conversations he has had with the Water and Sewer department.
There will be further discussions amongst the various departments involved.

There is a parcel of land on Tioga Way which has been surveyed for use for
battery storage facility. Some of it is marshy and can’t be used but a Northern
area of parcel (100x300 feet) could be developed as a warehouse. GM asked
for approval to clear the area and start using it for auxiliary storage in the short
term. This would be an interim step so the MMLD can educate themselves on
how to use the space.

Further questions were proposed on how this would be done by the Board
specifically on working with the neighbors. Chairman Hull said the biggest issues
is just keeping the noise down. Next steps would be land clearing, and the GM
is in touch with the town about doing it this summer and a bid will also be
presented to the Board.

Aerial Thermal Insulation Survey – thermal imaging will be done by an insured,
licensed drone operator to identify poor insulation in various areas of Old Town.
The contractor to do this work was found thru Mass Municipal Wholesale Electric
Company (MMWEC). The survey will occur for about 2-3 weeks starting 4/6/26.
This is a better process then homeowners having to allow people into their
homes – no personal images will be collected. Hopefully, there will be a grant
received which will expand the program to other areas of Marblehead. Notices
will be sent out to homeowners in the Old Town area, and the police
department has been notified. If someone wants to opt-out the information will
be removed from the database.

Overdue customer bill collections – there are several hundred customers with
overdue amounts over 60 days and collections will commence. Up until now,
the MMLD has complied with state regulations to not shut off electricity during
the winter months. The MMLD is encouraging households to set up payment
plans or arrange for financial support.

MMLD has donated two Kill-A-Watt meters to the library – they can be borrowed
and used to track how much electricity is consumed by various devices.

There were also multiple other updates on Village 13, replacement of utility
poles, Union dissolved relationship with Local 8214, results due from survey in
April, working with Rec and Park on various projects and the MMLD is watching
political developments at the federal and state levels. Federal is the Energy
Regulation Commissions order to cut NE transmission owners Return on Equity
and the States is an order to have an Energy Supply Plan that drives affordability
and reliability.

Motion and vote to meet in Executive Session at 5:25 pm.
