Juneteenth and Jodi Smith

Jodi Smith, a member of the Marblehead Racial Justice Team, introduced Nicole
McClain, who spoke about the North Shore Juneteenth Association, founded in
2017.

Juneteenth is an American holiday that commemorates June 19, 1865, the date
that enslaved black people of Galveston, Texas learned that President Lincoln had
freed enslaved people in Confederate states two and a half years earlier.

Nicole has visited celebrations in Boston since she was a child and wanted to
bring Juneteenth events to North Shore communities to encourage positive
awareness of black freedom, contributions to American history and
accomplishments and black life

Two events are planned:

1) Hats and Heels Tea, this year called the Hats and Slippers Tea because it is
virtual, to be held on May 8. Carol Boyce Davies, a scholar in African American
Diaspora, will speak on Black Women and the Desire for a Transformed USA
2) A virtual or live celebration of Juneteenth with a 45 minute to one hour
presentation sometime in June to include:

   • a Juneteenth African American flag-raising overseen by someone from
     ROTC or experienced in the ceremony; cost of a flag would be about $250;
     the flag would fly for a week
   • playing of the Black National Anthem
   • speaker
   • festival activities
Nicole will present a proposal to the Board of Selectmen
