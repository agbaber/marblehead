OBSERVER REPORT

MARBLEHEAD DISABILITIES COMMISSION - March 12, 2025
LWVM Observer: Nancy Powell

Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell (Chair), Briggete Duffy, Amy Hirschkron
(remote), Gail Mack, Samantha Marino (remote), Andrea Mountain (remote)
Members Absent: Mara Dartly-Rocco
Guests Present: Kyle Wylie

Update MA Office on Disability Grant/Devereaux Beach
Ms. Blaisdell met with representatives from Parks and Rec Department to do a
walk-thru at Deveraux Beach and found that with the grant funding awarded, the
Town will be able to make the entire beach accessible including the parking lot,
the beach, the playground, the bathrooms, the pavilion and the restaurant. The
project is currently out for pricing.

Harbor Implementation Board Meeting Update
Ms. Blaisdell attended as a new committee member representing the MDC. The
Board expressed interest in sharing the work they’ve completed and planned thus
far.

Town Charter Invitation/Questions
The Town Charter Committee has extended an invitation to the MDC to meet on
March 25th to discuss key elements of charter creation and consider the
responsibilities, authority, structure of the MDC and draft language for Counsel to
eventually review.

Disabled Identification Registry
The MDC, in cooperation with the Marblehead Fire Department, keeps a list of
residents with disabilities who want to be identified as needing special assistance
in the event of an emergency. As there have been no recent sign-ups for the
registry, Ms. Marino will advertise this service on the Commission’s social media
channels to inform residents with disabilities about its existence and let them know
the information they provide will be kept in the Dispatch System so first responders
will have it available to them as needed.
139 Pleasant Street Hearing
The Boston-based Architectural Access Board will hold a hearing on Monday 4/7
at 2:00pm regarding 139 Pleasant Street updates. Some MDC members plan to
attend. Ms. Blaisdell will share the remote meeting link to interested MDC
members.

1 Atlantic Ave AAB Update
On February 11th the AAB received additional documents regarding the restaurant
that is about to open at this address. At their meeting on 2/24/25, the Board ruled
that the petitioner must provide further detail as to where trash will be stored and
picked up outside the building. Their ruling also noted that the pull side of the rear
door entrance lacks the appropriate floorspace clearance and this must be
addressed.

Hybrid Meetings Discussion
A reporter from the Current contacted Ms. Blaisdell for comment as the regulations
established during COVID-19 are about to expire. Ms. Blaisdell stressed the
importance of keeping public meetings accessible for all, including people with
disabilities. She noted that internet access is still one of the main hurdles to
overcome. Ms. Hirschkron confirmed that the end of the hybrid meeting law does
not affect the MDC’s meetings because State regulations permit disability
commissions to hold hybrid meetings provided the chairperson is physically
present in an agreed upon public meeting space while conducting the meeting.

Closed Captioning at Meetings Townwide
Members have noticed that during School Committee meetings, closed
captioning has not been enabled. A number of MDC members will attempt to log
into various meetings to see if closed captioning is available and working
properly.
