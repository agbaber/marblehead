BOARD OF HEALTH - 4-8-2025
Hybrid Meeting, Recorded

Hazlett

Sexual Assault Awareness Month - April 2025
Ms. Hazlett read the proclamation written and signed by the Select Board and asked the
BOH to endorse it. The motion was made and passed. Mr. McMahon wanted to note
that Megan Sweeney was the one who promoted and presented this to the Select
Board. After the vote the BOH took a moment to reflect on all those who have
experienced it.

Creating a Healthier Marblehead - CAHM
Dr. Massaro has continued to meet with various groups in Marblehead and all express a
genuine interest in the health assessment survey. Among the groups he has met with
are: Marblehead Pediatrics (who made some suggestions), The Female Humane
Society (for the second time), the MHD ministerial association. He is “in the queue” to
speak to the Select Board, whose current energy is for the Town Meeting. Other groups
that might be talked with include: the Chamber of Commerce, perhaps the banks,
Salem Hospital OB-GYN, etc. He was asked about the reception from MHD pediatrics
who were asked some good questions, e.g., what’s it like to be a kid and a caregiver
now for the ages 10-15. On a very positive note, the project has achieved 50% of the
needed funds.

Measles
       Dr. Massaro has received queries about how contagious measles is. He noted
that the MHD Current wrote a summary about measles. With any travel to the US
Southwest all infants age 1 year should have an MMR; it can be given as early as 6
months, but will have to repeated again at 1 year of age.
       He spoke further about one side effect of measles that is now getting more
attention - “immune amnesia”. A small percent of measles patients will LOSE immunity
against other diseases which will then have to be acquired again. He plans to do deeper
research into this topic and how this is an example of public health science versus
medical science. People need to understand more.

Mental Health Task Force update - the task force will soon be meeting with the MHD
Counseling Center to see their perspective of the merger of the two. He will report this
at the next meeting

Director’s Report
         Transfer Station - Mr. Petty reported the good news that the compactor as well
as the new chute are installed, and there is a new “tipping” floor. The only recent “glitch”
has been that the compactor when it is on the return cycle after pushing, an overload
circuit is tripped. The company has been out to investigate, made some adjustments,
and so far it seems to be working. Commercial business is back and they are filling two
trailers a day. He noted that more customers are coming from greater distances as it is
hard to find places that take trash.
         Recycling - the company Republic (that is part of GreenWorks) that does
recycling for MHD is shut down for a while (until December) to upgrade. The recycling is
now being handled by Waste Management and going to Billerica. This is at no extra
cost to MHD.
         Transfer Station Traffic Flow - with the transaction hut and the compactor hut
now in place, it is time that the traffic flow for residential and commercial customers to
change. On Monday 4/14/25 residential customers will enter via the Green Street
entrance. The gates will open at 7am, allowing customers to queue along the driveway,
before the TS opens at 7:30 am. Commercial customers will continue to enter via
Woodfin Terrace.
         As for the LPM (license plate readers) they will be installed in a few months. The
transaction hut will have cameras, allowing full view of the entire site. When the LPR is
installed, there will be enough time to spot scofflaws. There will also be parking at the
transaction hut, so traffic flow won’t be affected.
         Regarding facility stickers, 60% of the total have already been sold - the popular
times to buy stickers is in January, close to summer, and the fall. One still needs a
physical sticker to access Devereux Beach.
         Curbside Collection - Mr. Petty remarked that this is the last year of the town’s
very favorable contract. Republic will be approached, but the town is looking towards a
3, 5, or 10 year contract. At this point there is no limit on the amount that Marbleheaders
can recycle. This may have to change in the future, requiring new rules and regulations.
(No businesses are supposed to put out materials for curbside recycling). (Although not
mentioned specifically at this meeting, at past meetings Mr. Petty has said that the cost
for recycling will go up significantly.) The HD and BOH will have to have a talk with Fin
Comm.
         Other -
                Hazardous Waste Day is scheduled for 5/31/25 and will use the transfer
station as before. A new vendor will continue to recycle paint, gas, etc. but also will
recycle latex paint. This mixed latex paint is sold in bulk
                Curbside leave collection will occur on the weeks of 4/21, 5/12, and 6/2.
              Final drafts of the tobacco and body art regulations are “out” and a public
meeting to discuss them will be set up for 4/28/25
              The meeting schedule for the next 3 months is 4/28/25, 5/13/25 and
6/3/25.

The meeting was adjourned and the BOH went into executive session.
