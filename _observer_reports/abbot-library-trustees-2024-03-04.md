OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

ABBOT LIBRARY BOARD OF TRUSTEES – March 4, 2024
Hybrid meeting held in the interim library
LWVM Observer: Brigitte Lagoutte

Trustees in attendance: Gary Amberik (Chair), Kathy Barker, Jennifer Jewell, Deb
Payson, David Ross, John Williams, and Kim Grad (Library Director)

1. Minutes of February 5, 2024 were approved.

2. Director's report:
Approval by vote of having the library closing hour on Monday and Wednesday at 9pm
(instead of 8pm).
In preparation for the move to the renovated library, on March 15th, 2024 book pick up
will not be available at the library.
The Library is planning a closing date on April 22nd. but that date is not definite.

The Literary Festival will take place on July 4 to July 7. On the last day, Sunday July
7th, the library will be open. Many authors will be invited. Detail schedule is TBD.

A Legislative breakfast is also planned for later this year. This event is open to the
public. It emphasizes the importance of State financial aid for libraries, and the support
of innovative programs, and the partnership and collaboration among libraries.

Several issues are being discussed and worked on, such as a new meeting room policy,
as well as the fees that will be applied to individuals or groups interested in renting a
working space or organizing an event at the library.

3. Chair's report and Building Renovations updates:
    • The APLF (Abbot Public Library Fund) will have a meeting Tuesday March12th.
    • An invoice from the Architectural Firm Johnson Roberts of $9,915 was voted and
      approved by the Trustees.
    • The renovations are continuing at pace at the library; the new elevator will be
      installed soon.

4. Friends of the library updates:
- The Friends will hold an Annual General Meeting on March 13, 2024.
- A moving sale of books will take place at the Library from March 25th to March 30th.
(M-Tu.-W - F- Sat. 10am - 4 pm, and Th. 12 - 4pm)

Next meeting on April 1st, 2024 6pm
The meeting was adjourned at 7:15pm.
