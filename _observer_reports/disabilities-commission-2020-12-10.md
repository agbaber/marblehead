OBSERVER REPORT

MARBLEHEAD DISABILITY COMMISSION: 12/10/20 via Zoom
LWVM Observer: Nancy Powell
Board Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell (chair), Katie Farrell, Preston
Ford, Amy Hirschkron, Ed Lang, Cheryl LaLonde, Sue Harris, Samantha
Marino, Andrea Mountain, Guests: Tom Shotmeyer

Commission members discussed need to support passage of (H.4425) The
ACCESSIBLE Massachusetts Act which will come before the Legislature in
January and has the potential to be effective in creating accessible housing
and workplaces.
  • H.4425 would enhance the authority of the Massachusetts
     Architectural Board whose regulations are part of the State’s building
     code and enforced by local building inspectors.
  • H.4425 mandates among other things that any pre-1991 buildings
     (such as schools) being converted into housing units be required to
     make 95 percent of them adaptable in the event of disability thus
     expanding housing options for all. Laurie will reach out to H.4425
     Sponsors as well as Jeff Duggan (Mass Office on Disability) and
     Representative Lori Erlich and invite them to meet with the
     Commission.

There is a Warrant Article on Sidewalk Snow Removal being developed
by town resident, Jim Zisson, and Commission members would like to have
input on it. The stated purpose of the Article is to “create a common sense
and reasonable sidewalk snow removal by-law that provides safe passage
for pedestrians both young and old without being overly burdensome to the
Town’s property owners.” The by-law proposes that property owners be
required to remove snow from sidewalks (with certain waivers available)
within 24 hours after a snowstorm. Laurie indicated she’d like to see the
Town plow sidewalks in certain areas (i.e., Bell School) rather than to rely
on property owners to remove snow themselves as there’s the need to
consider people who use wheelchairs or walkers and not just those on foot.
The idea that the law might be difficult to enforce was also a concern.

Ed Lang reports Ft. Sewall restoration has turned out better than had been
expected and new pavement, gate, railings and accessible restrooms will
be completed in January.
