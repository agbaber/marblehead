OBSERVER REPORT

FINANCE COMMITTEE: 3/22/21 LWVM Observers: Katharine Redmond, Bonnie Grenier
Members Present: Ben Berman, Pat Franklin, Emily Belfbecker, Blair Nelson, Alec
Goolsby, Kevin Brodrick, Emma Puglisi, Clerk

Planning Board: $1675 level funded FY22 budget approved for town meeting vote

Finance Committee: $149, 760 level funded FY22 budget approved for vote at town
meeting

Abbot Library: Patti Rodgers, Nancy Arata
  • $1,172,202.12 FY22 budget approved for vote at town meeting
  • There will be additional step increases for salaries
  • Operating budget level funded
  • $9800 reserve fund transfer will likely be need for FY 2021 to cover HVAC repair
      this year
  • Overview presented of Article 35 on the Town Warrant to fund a major
      renovation to the Library:
          o $9,500,000 estimated project cost of which 54% is for infrastructure
             replacement
          o $1 million has been raised from private sources to put towards above
             stated costs
          o Aspire to raise an additional $2 million through private funding for a library
             Endowment
          o Project would take just over a year to complete (target start Sept, 2022)
          o Library services would relocate to a temporary local space during
             construction

Health Management: Andrew Petty
  • $291,150.52 FY22 budget approved for town meeting vote
  • $29,114 Increase in salaries to cover increase in hours from 30to 37.5/week for
      public health nurse and health inspector needed for fully operational health
      department
  • $965 to cover increased office expense related to MAVEN software necessary for
      remote internet access
Waste Department: Andrew Petty
  • $2,412,239 FY22 budget approved for vote at town meeting
  • Two part time people at Transfer Station to be replaced by one full time person
      to provide better coverage
  • JRM Waste Collection is losing money on the collection of recyclables in
      Marblehead.
  • Marblehead is in the 5th year of a 10-year contract with JRM. Costs are likely to
      go up considerably with a new contract. Future contracts are already being
      explored

Reserve Fund Transfer of $40,000 approved to cover labor counsel costs which are
higher this year due to continued negotiations with police, fire and municipal employee
unions

Covid 19 Relief Bill:
  • Marblehead could receive as much as $6 million, although the actual amount
      has yet to be determined and could be significantly less.
  • Half of the money would be received in 2021 and half in 2022
  • There are some general restrictions on how the money can be spent, but the
      town would have a fair amount of latitude in expenditures
  • There was general agreement that expenditures should not be put towards the
      operating budget but should be used for on-time expenses
