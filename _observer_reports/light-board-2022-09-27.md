OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Name of Committee: Marblehead Municipal Light
Meeting Date: 9/27/22
LWVM Observer: Maggie Smist

Members in Attendance:
Lisa Wolf, Jean Jacques Yarmoff, Michael Hull (Chair), Simon Frechette and
Adam Smith
Joe Kowalik General Manager

Meeting Summary:
Michael Hull called meeting to order at 4:30 pm.
August 30, September 8 and 14th meeting minutes approved.

Bill Bullock of EMWIC reported out on meter battery action project.

He reviewed process to date and said there were 9 bidders. 3 finalists for
project: Agilitas Energy, Convergent and Delorean Power.
Discussion of benefits and costs of the three finalists. Bill will be collected on saved
transmission and capacity charges. It is hard to project capacity but transmission
charges are expected to go up over 20-year life of battery. EMWIC will review the
invoices.
Delorean Power out of Albany NY was chosen. Service technicians are relatively close.
They have put together a team to start the contracting process. Delorean is willing to
add projects if Tioga Way and Village 13 works out. 50/50 split with savings with
Delorean. There was extensive discussion about next steps.

Bill updated MMLD on residential battery program

Considering rolling out an inventive for battery manufacturers since Inflation Reduction
Act will also be offering incentives for homeowners to install batteries. He talked about
current rebate program during the general discussion. Savings with residential program
is specific to each project and can be substantial with tax credits.
Fire Chief visited to discuss residential battery program

Chief asked for summary of why MMLD wants to add batteries. Lisa spoke about
savings with renewables being added and taking pressure off natural gas at peak
times. Long term plan is to reduce peak capacity by adding batteries.
Fire Chief asked for us to not have tunnel vision: we need to measure whether risks out
way the awards. Green is good but we need to be aware of downsides. Batteries
need to go in garages and not homes. Fire code, building code and electrical codes
will be overlapping.
Jean-Jacques mentioned there are revisions to codes going on now – he mentioned
there is a demand from residents so how do we work with everyone to make it happen.
He mentioned that other towns have it so we want to be able to offer it. It would be
good for homeowners to have process to get battery.
Fire Chief does agree everything has risks and we have to weigh risks. He said current
state parameters are not complete.
Mike Hull recommended putting together a task force from MMLD department, MMLD
Board, Fire Department and Building Department to work thru this issue. He said we had
a similar approach with solar panels.

General Updates by Joe Kowalik
 Tree trimming program is moving forward and will be done by October. Spoke about
Hurricane update in Florida. Marblehead sent one truck to Florida. Waiting for
Switchgear proposals. State Grant received from Coastal Management – it will take 4
parcels near MMLD down to Marblehead Trading and improve public access and
provide security for MMLD location. Money will also go towards raising seawalls. There
is a Solar Array dedication belonging to MMEWEC/MMLD.
MMLD Financial Update by Joe Kowalik
MMLD is having a bad year with expenses. We raised PPA twice this year and we have
to raise it again. Increase is due to prices in ‘day ahead’ market. New England is
constrained with bringing in natural gas this winter. Germany is being cut off from
getting gas from Russia and they stockpiling natural gas. Price going up 60%. PPA will
go up for average bill to go up $22. Joe asked for guidance on moving forward –
committee supported Joe’s data and decision.
There is a discussion about deferred capitol maintenance and revisiting this at a future
maintenance. Joe wants to also look at vendor to service/upgrade the equipment
that feeds meter information back to the MMLD. Currently, the MMLD has been
monitoring the meter feeds. They will come in with a proposal to remotely monitor our
fiber optic system.
Strategy working group report out by Jean Jacques Yarmoff
Long list of possible actions and what should be prioritized for coming year presented.
All 33 issues will impact financials etc. He reviewed the list and the impact via rates,
emissions etc. He is asking for guidance.
He spoke specifically on forecasting load with maximum electrification. He spoke
about not selling new gas cars by 2035. MMLD has to anticipate 2000 new Electric
Vehicles each year. MMLD has ten years to work out how it will affect the load. Per
Jean Jacque, ISO New England forecasting is not good. Joe asked who is best suited
to handle this problem? He doesn’t think the cities and towns should be dealing with it
and the state planning commissions should be looking at it.
Goal is to have the board continually bring back information to the meetings. Group
should meet off line with several commissioners and incorporate members of the
community be part of this group. They brainstormed people who can be part of this
team including engineering people or planning people.
Sustainability committee report out by Lisa Wolf and Simon Frechette
Fire Chief reported out and they spoke about hiring a Sustainability Officer. First role
would be developing a robust communication strategy. This role would pay for itself
through his/her work. Adam asked to see job description. Joe would be making the
decision and he asked if the committee has fully decided to hire this position. Mike Hull
said we need to backfill other positions.
Lisa asked about timing of department goal setting and asked for it to be on the next
agenda.
Jean Jacque asked about new General Glover project – Joe said one building will be
in Marblehead and we already have power going to the sight. There was further
discussion on this upcoming construction project.
Other Comments
Guest, Frank Cashner, commented that Ukraine war puts us all in competition for the
same power source. It has made the MMLD board and department job even more
challenging. He is encouraging more dialogue with the community.
Jim Zisson is on Brown School Committee and he asked about solar project at the
school being on an agenda.
Meeting adjourned at 7:00 pm
