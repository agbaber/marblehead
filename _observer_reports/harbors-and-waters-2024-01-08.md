OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

HARBORS AND WATERS BOARD - January 8, 2024
Meeting In Person, Not Recorded
LWVM Observer: Kathy Breslin

Members present: John Daub, Ken Breen, Clark Smith
Members absent: Gary Gregory, Jay Michaud
Alternates present: Peter Dragonis.
Alternates absent: Chris Hood, Rick Cuzner

FY25 Budget updates and status: no changes

Commercial Street Gangway Access repairs: The granite block connecting the
gangway cracked. Fifteen years ago, holes were drilled in and pins placed, and the
block cracked recently. Mark Souza, Harbormaster, met with the Town engineers to get
the block out and drill holes into granite and pour hydraulic into it. The labor was done
all in-house. The Operational Budget covered this expense.

Harbormaster report

The new boat project is in queue for production. It may be six months ahead of
schedule. $150-180 K is our share.

Storm 2018: We are awaiting $230K in reimbursement from FEMA for reimbursement
of sea wall repairs. Mark spoke to MEMA and the reimbursement should be signed off
in the next one-two months.
