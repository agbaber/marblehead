OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Marblehead Disabilities Commission
November 13, 2024
LWVM Observer: Nancy Powell

Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell, Mara Dartly-Rocco, Briggete Duffy,
Amy Hirschkron, Cheryl LaLonde, Gail Mack, Samantha Marino (remote)

Guests Present: Kyle Wylie

1. ADA Audit and Transition Plan

The Commission plans to invite the Town Administrator to a future meeting
to get his input on the town’s ADA Transition Plan that was completed by
the Collins Center for Public Management last spring. There are many non-
compliant areas in town that haven’t yet been addressed and they will seek
his advice on setting priorities. The Commission will propose that the
Collins ADA report be published on the town website and include a list of
the projects that have been completed and those that will be addressed
next.

Ms. Blaisdell noted that the ADA audit had omitted the new library as it was
under construction when municipal buildings were being evaluated for ADA
compliance in 2022. She plans to contact the Collins center to see if they
can come back and evaluate the building’s accessibility features. It appears
the “accessible” ramp now under construction at the library will still be out
of compliance as the slope ratio appears to be too large.

2. Website and Facebook Management

Ms. Mack has been making progress on the Commission’s website. The
Commission would like to become more active on social media platforms to
improve visibility of their work and services offered.
3. Future Plans

Ideas for potential future projects include ensuring that more public
meetings offer accessibility options and making Town Meeting more
accessible by generating written materials in large print and providing
personal amplification devices. (Ms. Marino will research to see what other
assistive technology might be available for increasing accessibility for Town
Meeting.) The Commission will also highlight businesses that focus on
being ADA compliant by providing them with stickers with the MDC logo for
display in their windows.

4. Gerry Playground

Ms. Hirschcron announced the formation of a planning committee (Friends
of the Park on Elm) that will ensure ADA compliance is a focus in the
redesign of the park currently located at the site of the former Gerry School.
Ms. Hirschcron will reach out to the planning committee to offer support as
needed.

Next meeting: December 11, 2024
