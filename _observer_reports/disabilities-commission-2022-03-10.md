OBSERVER REPORT

MARBLEHEAD DISABILITIES COMMISSION - March 10, 2022
LWVM Observer – Nancy Powell

Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell, Amy Hirschkron, Katie Farrell, Andrea Mountain,
Cheryl LaLonde, Samantha Marino, Sue Harris
Members Absent: Ed Lang
Guests Present: Joanne Miller, Ann Foley, Laney Dowling, Rich Ramos, Pamela Daly

Joanne Miller Presentation. Joanne Miller, member of the Board of Health and Chair
of the Marblehead Mental Health Task Force, gave an overview of priorities and
projects both groups have been focusing on over the last year, and expressed a
willingness to work with the Commission on projects of mutual interest. The Mental
Health Task Force was formed in the Spring of 2021 and includes representatives from
the Board of Health, the Board of Selectmen, the Council on Aging and the Marblehead
Counseling Center, Police Chief Dennis King, a local psychiatrist, and the individuals in
charge of mental health services at Boston U. and Salem State. Projects have included
creating an online list of local resources for those with mental health concerns
(marbleheadcares.org) and hosting panel discussions on mental health issues. The
group also is looking for ways to partner with other organizations in Town, including the
Disabilities Commission. The Commission and Joanne agreed to continue to
communicate on ways we might be helpful to the Task Force or otherwise work
together.

Family Fund. Amy Foley and Laney Dowling provided an update on the Family Fund’s
project to renovate Hobbs Field playground for use by children under 5 or 6. The group
has raised $240,000 to date, of which $62,000 is a grant to purchase specialized
accessible playground equipment. A total of $300,000 is needed to complete the
project. They plan to break ground in June.

139 Pleasant Street Update. Amy reported on the February 28 hearing before the
Architectural Access Board on the Warrens’ request for a waiver of ADA requirements
for this location. Their arguments were not accepted by the AAB, which has ordered
them to submit a feasibility plan to add an accessible lift or ramp at either the front or
back of the building. The plan will be reviewed at another hearing not yet scheduled.
Harbor Lift Update. After discussion with Mark Souza about the draft proposal for
installation of a lift at Tucker’s Wharf, Amy and Sue, with Laurie’s concurrence and input
from Rich Ramos, concluded that the only feasible location for the lift is State Street
Landing (which the Commission had approved as an alternative at its last
meeting). Amy and Sue submitted a revised proposal to Mark to give to Harbors and
Waters Board members, whose March 7 meeting subsequently was postponed until
March 14. Amy will present the proposal at the meeting. Once the proposal is
approved, the Commission will proceed with fundraising for the lift.

Town Map of Accessible Locations. Laurie and Sue distributed a list of Marblehead
locations to consider adding to the Commission’s brochure/map with a designation of
those that need to be researched and a spreadsheet for members to fill in with
information they learn about each site. The Commission discussed ideas for making
this information available to the public, including on social media, and will continue to
work to compile the necessary information.

Town Meeting Accessibility. Laurie reported on her conversation with Kyle Wylie
about the inclusion of a notice on the Town website about people’s right to request
special accommodations for Town Meeting. That conversation is ongoing. Meanwhile,
the Commission agreed to add that information to its Facebook page and to a new
Instagram account that Samantha agreed to set up.
