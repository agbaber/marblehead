OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Marblehead Municipal Light - 11.26.24
Hybrid Meeting
LWVM Observer: Maggie Smist

In Attendance:
Light Commissioners: Michael Hull, Jean Jacques Yarmoff, Simon
Frechette, Mike Hull, Lisa Wolf (chair). Jean Jacque Yarmoff (remote)
Light Department: Joe Kowalik (General Manager) Matt Barrett

-4:03 Meeting Called to Order
Land Acknowledgement was read. Minutes from 11/14 joint meeting
with charter commission, 11/21 public session and 11/21 executive
session moved and approved.
Public Comment
- Outstanding items and requests from previous meetings
1. Employee survey – the MMLD submitted an edited survey last week
and they are waiting to hear back from POLCO that survey is
appropriate. The employee survey will go out electronically once
approved. POLCO will make themselves available to employees for
questions.
2. Juicebox EV Charger customer – Joe followed up and the company
making them is going out of business. The software will be terminated
so everyone will be moved off this platform. MEMIC has reached out to
current customers and there are 7 customers where there is no solution
at this time. Joe is continuing to work for solutions
3. MEAM (Municipal Electric Association of MA) legislative actions -
VOTE REQUESTED Background -On Thursday October 17, Chairs of a
MLP Legislative Caucus reached out to legislators (Jenny Armini) with
MLPs in their district to request sign on to two letters of comment for
DEP (Dept of Environmental Protection) and DOER (Dept of Energy
Resources) on draft regulations. These letters outlined concerns with
proposals related to the Clean Heat Standard and Large Building Energy
Reporting and were drafted with MEAM. The letters requested sign-on
by legislators by mid-day Tuesday October 22. The GM and MEAM did
not share these letters with MMLD Commissioners in advance of the
request for legislative sign on or solicit MMLD Commissioner input on
these comments. The timing and urgency are also unclear.
Proposed Motion to: Direct our GM to ask MEAM to share all letters
related to draft regulations and legislation with Commissioners, prior to
sending to legislators.
Discussion followed about the need for the motions. They discussed
the difference between legislation and regulation.
Motion was proposed, seconded and approved.
General Manager Updates
1.Village 13 Update – construction bid went out and lead contractor has
to be electrical contractor. There are six good vendors who we hope
will bid. Bid package is 250 pages, and deadline for bids is 12/11.
2.MA Coastal Zone Management grant – reconciled with town that
additional funds were approved. Department will receive $161,000.
3.Preliminary 2025 Operating Budget – this year tracking against plan
has been very positive. Barring any extreme weather, the department is
in good shape to end the year.
4.Preliminary 2025 Capital Budget – with projects for 2025,
department is showing an increase in expense budget. Projected rates
for 2025 show there will be no change in rates at this point.
In 2025, there will be more money spent on capital plans – the Light
Department hopes to finish the Village 13 project. Federal CEC Grant
applied for – it is for $2 million. If we get that grant in December, we
need to spend money in next two years. We would need to come up
with $600k to apply to this project. If we don’t get grant, we will still
need to do a portion of the work covered by this grant.
$2 million is also needed for other equipment expenditures (vehicles,
switchgear, general upgrades). General discussion followed on what is
needed and type of vehicle the department is exploring.
5.Ocean Ave utility easement requests – the Light Department
attorney has been involved and there are a few remaining items to be
done. The easement has the MMLD ‘owning’ this easement. There
will be future easements to give MMLD the right to be in the front yard
of some properties.
6. Title dispute on Atlantic Ave entrance to Clifton Substation – issue
with definition of the parcel listed above. The Town Planning board
during their October, 2024 meeting gave some of this parcel back to
landowners on Atlantic Avenue, but MMLD was not made aware of the
transaction. There is a dispute within the town. MMLD will continue to
work on how to proceed.
7.IT upgrades -the department has updated comcast internet and
working with town on future IT upgrades.
Meeting went into Executive Session at 5:10 pm
