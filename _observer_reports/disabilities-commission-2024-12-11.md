OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

MARBLEHEAD DISABILITIES COMMISSION - December 11, 2024
LWVM Observer – Nancy Powell

Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell, Mara Dartly-Rocco, Briggete Duffy, Amy Hirschcron, Gail
Mack, Samantha Marino
Members Absent: Cheryl LaLonde, Adreea Mountain
Guests Present: Don Morgan-Marblehead Conservancy

1. Eagle Scout Project
Don Morgan of the Marblehead Conservancy joined tonight’s meeting. He gave an overview of
a planned Eagle Scout project that involves building a four-foot-wide stoned dust pathway
leading to Joseph Brown Pond from Barry Road and a viewing platform at the Pond’s edge. He
is seeking feedback from the Commission regarding ADA compliance. Ms. Hirschcron cited
regulations from Chapter 10 of the Outdoor Developed Area section of the US-ADA which she
emailed to Mr. Morgan.

2. Update Mass Office of Disability Grant
The Commission has officially been awarded a $92,028 MoD grant that corresponds with the
Devereaux Beach ADA upgrades the MDC had applied for. The Town Administrator will follow
up with the Commission regarding next steps once it has been finalized by vote. Ms. Hirschcron
will request a copy of the grant application in order to review what renovations are included.

3. Variance Application- 1 Atlantic Avenue
The owner of this property is requesting a variance that would allow construction of an
accessible entrance at the back of the restaurant. The building is located directly on the
sidewalk/street, making it difficult to build a ramp at the main entrance. The Commission has
concerns regarding the narrow width of the alley that would lead to the back entrance, noting
that the State AAB (Architectural Access Board) which develops and enforces regulations
designed to make public buildings accessible and safe for use by people with disabilities will
decide whether or not to grant a variance in this case.

4. Townwide Meeting Accessibility
Ms. Blaisdell spoke with the town’s Facilities Director who explained that the new computer
projection system used at Town Meeting has numerous font sizes available. In prior years, the
text on slides was small and difficult to read. This will be corrected for Town Meeting 2025.
Ms. Hirschcron noted that, by law, a public meeting attended by 50+ people requires an
amplification system to be in place. Of the four options typically used, she believes an FM
system would be the most appropriate for the town’s needs. And, since 25% of FM receivers at
Town Meeting must be compatible with hearing aids, she roughly estimates the town needing
approximately 27 receivers at a total cost upwards of $2500. Ms. Mack will connect with the
individuals responsible for the sound system used during town meeting to determine what
specific system is used so compatible receivers can be researched.
Ms. Mack drafted a letter for the Chair to send to the School Committee to ensure that
accessibility (closed captioning) is integrated into their meetings.
When the 2025 Town Meeting notice is sent out, Commission members agree that local
newspapers should be asked to include a notice indicating that accommodations will be made
available and the town ADA coordinator should be provided prior notice.
Ms Hirschcron noted ADA law requires that the town ensure effective communication (closed
captioning) is available for all public meetings as the lack of it could be considered obstruction.

5. Website and Facebook
Ms. Mack continues to work on the Commission’s website. She reviewed the recommendations
from the town’s ADA report and plans to connect with the town’s website manager to determine
if the Commission is allowed to place a link on the town’s website directing users to the
Commission’s new page (potentially via Google Builder).

6. 139 Pleasant Street follow-up
Ms. Blaisdell reached out to the AAB asking for an update on this property but has not received
a response.

7. Year in Review
Ms. Blaisdell offered a review of the happenings of the Commission over the past year including
new members joining, some members retiring, a review of the ADA Collins Center report,
working to make accessibility improvements at several commercial establishments, an
accessible Harbormaster’s ramp installation, a MOD grant awarded for Devereaux Beach
upgrades, guidance in making Abbot Library’s ramp ADA compliant.
