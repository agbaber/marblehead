OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

BOARD OF HEALTH – April 28, 2025
Hybrid Meeting, Recorded
LWVM Observer: Tom Krueger

Members in Attendance: Andrew Petty, Tom McMahon, Tom Massaro, Helaine Hazlett

Public Hearing - Regulations Prohibiting the Manufacture, Sale, or Distribution of
Synthetic Cannaboinoids

The detailed regulations were available at the hearing. Mr. Petty noted that these are
prohibited at the state level; it is important to have regulations at the local level: this
allows the tobacco control officer to look for their presence in local stores and then
engage enforcement. He further said that these compounds are changing all the time,
sold under different names, and we don’t what they do in the long term. These items
are often sold in convenience stores. In MHD currently no stores carry these products.
Mr. McMahon said these regulations were both proactive and important. Dr. Massaro
said that we have no idea what downsides might be and it is important to educate the
community. There were no comments from the public. The regulations were passed
unanimously.

Public Hearing - Regulations for Body Art Establishments and Practitioners

These detailed regulations were available at the hearing. The last set of regulations was
made in 2008 and needed to be made current. Although MHD does not have any body
art establishments currently, neighboring communities (e.g. Salem) do. Much of the
discussion centered around fees - permit $500, application $750, plan application $400,
practitioner PMU (permanent make up) $350, guest practitioner PMU $200. There were
no comments from the public. The regulations were passed unanimously.

Bills - Mr. McMahon read the vendor’s names and bills since the last meeting.

Board of Health Powers and Duties

This discussion was about what the above powers and duties are. Legal counsel had
given their view on these, but Dr. Massaro averred that these should go beyond these
minimums. He wants these to be seen as “interesting” and “exciting”. Some discussion
followed and Dr. Massaro offered to rework the document with more emphasis on the
positive and to include the waste department.

Director’s Report

Mr. Petty said that Mr. McMahon will present at Town Meeting all the work that has
been done in the waste department and what is planned for next year. Of note, the
currently favorable trash collection contract will soon expire, and the next contract will
be much more expensive and have a huge impact. Mr. Petty will be working with Fin
Com.

There may have to be changes in curbside pickup for trash and recycling. Currently the
limit is 65 gallons per household of trash, and no limit on recycling. Trash collection is
done for single-family up to four apartment units. This may all have to be revisited with
new bylaws in the future.

On a separate subject, when the prospect for an assistant health director was
considered, it was deemed necessary for another truck F150, as the health director and
assistant both have additional duties, including at times curbside trash pickup. The loss
of this position will mean that the department will often be short staffed - lean staffing,
illness, etc. as main causes. There was subsequent discussion about funding the
assistant HD from the waste revolving fund (commercial users pay for disposal of their
waste.) Mr. Petty will track all the costs from curbside, recycling, etc., to see this
possibility. The addition of an assistant health director was to manage the waste side of
the health department, a task that consumes much Mr. Petty’s time, and it would allow
him to concentrate more on the health side.

(Some of the board members felt “blindsided” by the loss of the assistant health
director. The position had been in the budget that was first formulated in September and
subsequently voted on by the BOH in March. Then two weeks ago the position was
dropped. One board member did not think that this position was just “hypothetical”; he
further thought that this should have been an agenda item with much more discussion.
Another board member felt that the addition of such a position was something the board
should approve. Mr. Petty noted that no other MHD department operated that
way. Regardless of whose decision it should be, the position would have to be
approved at Town Meeting.)

Public Comment: One resident spoke very favorably about the new traffic pattern for the
residential and commercial traffic. Mr. Petty added that new signs about this pattern will
soon be posted.
