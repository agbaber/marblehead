OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Marblehead Municipal Light Commission: 3/25/25
LWVM Observer: Maggie Smist

In Attendance:
Light Commissioners: Michael Hull, Jean Jacques Yarmoff, Simon
Frechette, Adam Smith, Mike Hull, Lisa Wolf (chair).
Light Department: Joe Kowalik (General Manager)

4:03 Meeting Called to Order
Land Acknowledgement was read. 5 documents were sent around for
approval of a number of minutes for meetings held in March. Motion
moved to approve all documents.
1. Employee survey – Survey is ready to go and employees can do by
phone, on paper or on departmental computers. It will remain open for
two weeks. Joe stated he has had meetings with employees to explain
the survey and the importance of doing the survey.
2. 2025 Department Goals – Joe distributed the goals to the
commissioners and there was discussion of the mission statement, the
training section, engineering goals and the current chain of command.
There was also a discussion of the target goals around carbon emissions
and electrification grants. There was a discussion of the difficulty in
getting grants with the current administration – resiliency grants may
survive. The commissioners want to keep goals as part of the plan and
wait for the dust to settle.
Goals were moved and approved.
3. General Manager Updates
-Village 13 Update (Substation Construction Contract) – The site work
is on schedule and contractors are very good communicators.
Switchgear manufacturing delivery is being delayed from late April into
May and this equipment is important to get asap. There are some
concerns about sewer main pipe near Swampscott and new options are
being considered. Additional engineering and planning will be required.
-Utility Scale Battery - There is a signed contract for doing base line
noise study. Joe has also come up with six ways to do other site
evaluations. These were discussed and included environmental impact,
location of batteries around town, cost and impact to residential areas.
There was extensive discussion with the Commissioners on the use of
batteries in Marblehead.
- Value of BESS (Battery Energy Storage System) in Marblehead – It will
lower wholesale power costs in Marblehead, and the calculations were
presented by Joe. It will also allow for resiliency during unplanned
outage, and it may allow us to defer some capital investments.
-Wilkins Plant Upgrade – receiving functional grants
-Open Employee Positions
AGM Engineering Project Manager – expanding the search
Energy Efficiency Project Manager – interviews are ongoing
Department Mechanic – one offer declined and another interview
planned
Substation Specialist – job posted with possible internal candidate
Working Foreman – position is posted
2ND Lineman – new employee started 3/23/25
-APPA Excellence in Reliability – MMLD was a 2024 Certificate Winner
Meeting went into Executive Session at 5:15 pm
