OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

MARBLEHEAD HOUSING COMMITTEE - 8/19/25
Hybrid Meeting- Recorded
LWVM Observer- Julie Matuschak

Attendees: Kurt James, Alex Eitler, Debra Larkin, Deacon John Whipple, Nisha Austin,
Ramon Garcia, Mimi Hollister, Brendan Callahan.

The meeting began at 3:31pm

The first order of business was to appoint a Chair of the Committee. Brendan Callahan
was appointed chair by unanimous vote.

Minutes from the meeting of 6/17/25 were approved.

Discussion of outcome of vote on Section 3A of the state’s zoning laws:

On behalf of the Select Board, Dan Fox and Thatcher Kezer submitted a letter to the
Governor and the EOHLC, requesting an exemption from 3A zoning requirements. That
letter is still circulating, and a response is anticipated.

The town is now non-compliant and has until December 30 to become compliant. The
town is now ineligible for discretionary grants. The grant previously awarded for Rev
250 has been rescinded. Prior to becoming non-compliant, three grants were under
contract with the state, however, there has been no award or announcement of status
on these grants. The town had previously applied for ten grants which are on the
ineligible list. Decisions on these grants are due in October.

A question was asked as to whether there was any movement for the town to become
compliant. Chair Callahan replied that decisions moving forward would be made after
receiving notice from the state regarding exemption.

In response to a question about another special election, Alex Eitler replied that the
process could be used again but timing would be an issue.
Deacon Joe Whipple inquired whether a zoning amendment could be redrafted by the
Planning Board by the end of the year, but the strategy the town will choose to take
won’t be decided until the outcome of the exemption request. Redrafting a zoning
amendment would require obtaining data which would take time. Kurt James wondered
if a new plan might be met with less resistance, stating that the Pleasant Street zone
might be the main concern.

Chair Callahan stated that if the exemption is not granted, the Community Development
Department would seek to ask for assistance from the state.

Chair Callahan tabled further discussion about this issue until a response was received
from the state.

New business:

Kurt James asked if there was any additional information on the Coffin School property.
The Select Board is hoping to come up with future use options within the next few
months. Options available include housing of various kinds or municipal reuse. A
potential grant for consulting would most likely not be awarded, so internal discussion
by the Select Board will continue.

Chair Callahan reported that there was $39,000 of Home Consortium Funding available.
These funds have not been spent mainly due to strict federal regulations. There are also
funds in the Affordable Housing Trust. It was suggested that inviting representatives
from other towns or cities to discuss how they are using these funds might be helpful.

Next meeting will be on September 9 at 3:30pm.
