RECREATION & PARKS COMMISSION -January 29, 2026
LWVM Observer: Brigitte Lagoutte

Members in attendance: Jaime Bloch, Larry Simpson, Karin Ersnst, Shelly
Bedrosian, Chris Kennedy, Peter James. Rosanna Ferrante (Chair) was
absent.

The minutes of the January 12 meeting were approved.

There was a recognition for Peter James, Superintendent of the Park
Commission, for his service of 39 years to the Town. Peter is going to retire
at the end of January. He was thanked profusely for his work and
dedication.

Support for Town’s Parks:

Kathy Bradford, Charlene Tyler, Paul Green, Brigitte Lagoutte were present
to introduce the Commission to the newly founded group Friends of the
Parks of Marblehead led by Larry Simpson. The group would like to
improve the quality and the environment of the parks by adding a variety of
native plants and shrubs in order to encourage biodiversity (biological
diversity), the interconnection of life on earth between all living organisms,
insects, animals and plants. Consequently, this will reduce maintenance as
the grass areas will be reduced while leaving intact the current spaces
allocated for recreation. Native plants generally don’t need to be watered
as some non-native plants do. The group will also do some fundraising:
garden tours, native plants sale and private donations. They want to
engage the community and to have an educational role as well. The
Commission was positively receptive to the group and will support their
projects. The group needs time to design and organize the new areas and
will meet again with the Commission at a future date when they are ready
with their plans to become a non-profit organization and design the areas of
planting. Their first target will be the Glabicky Park at the entrance of
Marblehead on Route 114.

This is the beginning of a nice and productive partnership which will
improve the quality of our parks.

Department projects:
  • The ramp access at Stramski Park is almost done.
  • The Riverhead beach leases will be released soon to allow the rental
    businesses to function during the warm season.
  • The Rec. Commission is also starting to work on their programming
    of activities for the coming season

Memorial Policy Discussion:
The Memorial Policy booklet is still being reviewed. The person honored
must have been a Marblehead resident for at least 25 years. Other aspects
of the policy are being revised.

Recreation and Parks update:
  • Jaime is underway with programming for the spring season.
  • On January 23, there was a gathering at the Marblehead Community
     Center for the retirement celebration of Peter James who has kept
     our parks running for 39 years!

Correspondence:
There was a request for a wedding ceremony taking place at Chandler
Hovey Park for July 11. It was approved.

The meeting was adjourned at 6:25pm.
