Observer Report
Marblehead Disabilities Commission – June 9, 2022
LWVM Observer: Nancy Powell

Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell (Chair), Sue Harris, Amy Hirschkron, Cheryl
LaLonde, Ed Lang, Samantha Marino, Andrea Mountain, Rich Ramos

  1. Harbor Matters Update

     Harbor Lift: The Commission has been working with the Harbor and Waters
     Board on plans to open up Marblehead Harbor to those with mobility challenges
     including by making boating available to all regardless of physical ability. After
     getting valuable input from Harbormaster Mark Souza, a manual lift will soon be
     installed at State Street Dock. The Commission approved plans for a Dedication
     Ceremony to be held at the site on June 25th at 10:00am. Invitees will include
     Town officials and local news media, and will be open to all. At the event, Donna
     Rice, who is helping with publicity for the lift, will distribute 100 buttons with a logo
     design gifted to the Commission by a friend of Amy’s.

     Harbor Working Group: Laurie attended the May 16th public meeting of this
     group, the purpose of which was to seek comment on priorities for the harbor.
     Previously, at the Working Group Consultant’s request, Amy developed a list of
     questions relevant to the interests/concerns of the disabilities community. The
     meeting was not available remotely making it less likely that people with mobility
     challenges would be able to attend. Amy will check whether a ZOOM public
     meeting will be held as was previously agreed to by the Working Group’s
     Consultant.

  2. Town Map of Accessible Locations
     The Commission has been working with the Marblehead Chamber of Commerce
     to create a brochure that assesses points of interest in Marblehead for wheelchair
     accessibility. Sue reported that the brochure is almost complete and thanked
     members for their feedback. A thousand copies of the brochure will be printed and
   distributed by the Chamber of Commerce at its office and Information Booth. Sam
   suggested making the brochure available on the Commission website and
   perhaps creating a QR Code link to the site that will also be posted at the
   Chamber locations.

3. Disabilities Commission Future meetings
   Laurie reminded the Commission that the Open Meeting Law exception that
   allows fully remote meetings expires this year in July. Although the law allows
   Disabilities Commissions in Massachusetts to vote to make all meetings virtual, it
   requires that the meeting Chair be present at a physical location so that members
   of the public who wish to can attend in person. Laurie agreed to pursue finding a
   location that will allow future meetings to be available in a hybrid format.

4. Disabilities Commission Logo
   Sam reached out to some local school contacts about having students design a
   logo, but has had limited success to date. Amy brought a sample logo designed
   by a friend who is willing to give the Commission permission to use it in any
   manner it chooses. Commission members approved use of this logo.

5. Village Plaza Accessibility
   Village Plaza has a wheelchair ramp that leads to the second floor but it is located
   behind a door with no signage to indicate an accessible entrance. Laurie sent a
   letter to the owners of Village Plaza making three requests: that they install a sign
   on the door to the ramp to the upper level indicating that it is an accessible
   entrance, that they install a notice of the location of this ramp at the base of all
   stairs leading to the second level, and that they notify all Plaza tenants of the
   availability and location of the ramp. She has yet to receive a response and will
   follow up.

6. New Business
   Devereux Beach Playground: Rich received an email, copied to the Park & Rec
   Director among others, regarding the need for an accessible swing at the
   playground. Laurie indicated there is already an accessible swing on site. The
   Commission will leave this concern for the Park & Rec Director to handle for now.

   Fort Sewall Opening: Ed said that the renovated Fort Sewall official opening will
   be on June 18th from 12-2pm. Laurie encouraged Commission members to
   attend.
