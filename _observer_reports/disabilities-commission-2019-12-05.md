MARBLEHEAD LEAGUE OF WOMEN VOTERS
                                   Observer Corp Report Form
League Observer:              Nancy Powell
Commission:                   Marblehead Disability Commission 12/5/19
Members in Attendance:        Laurie Blaisdell (Chair), Andrea Mountain, Katie Farrell,
                              Preston Ford, Cheryl LaLonde, Sam Marino
Meeting was posted at least 48 hours in advance
Meeting was held in handicapped accessible location.
The public was allowed to participate.
The minutes of last meeting were not posted.
Issues on the agenda that were relevant to League Positions: LWV is an organization fully
committed to diversity, equity and inclusion in principle and in practice. The Marblehead
Disability Commission works to insure that people with disabilities are given equal access to
community resources such as sidewalks, crosswalks, parking and entrances to public buildings.

Laurie Blaisdell would like to explore having a Resident Outreach Coordinator position created
for Marblehead similar to one that Salem has instituted. This would ease and hasten the
process of getting needed repairs at public housing developments. She mentioned the excellent
“Salem for All Ages” website that could serve as a model for Marblehead.
The Commission is considering proposing a Town Warrant Article aimed at correcting
inaccessible sidewalks throughout town. Among the problems are lack of curb cuts, tree roots
lifting sidewalks, potholes, crosswalks with malfunctioning or inaccessible stop light buttons. The
problem is widespread and the fix will be expensive ($5-$15k for a curb cut). Town Planner has
applied for a grant to study ADA compliance in Marblehead. It was suggested that prospective
new GIS tree maps could include sidewalks to help get arms around the scope of the problem.
Some funding will come from Complete Streets Grant but not until 2022 as repairs to Rail Trail
has been given first priority.
Laurie hopes to recruit volunteers to attend new school meeting next week to address concerns
about accessibility including: that front door drop off area will accommodate wheelchair users
being dropped off, that wheelchair users will be able to enter the school with their peers and not
have a separate entrance at the back of the building near the dumpsters, that the door buzzer
will be placed at an accessible height, that there will be a dedicated accessible bathroom in the
building and enough wheelchair accessible parking spaces close to the entrance.
Goals for the coming year include: invite a disabilities attorney to present on ADA compliance,
invite Rep Lori Ehrlich, and continue to build a hungry and powerful disability community in
Marblehead.
