OBSERVER REPORT

PLANNING BOARD: December 8, 2020 LWVM Observer: Judith Black
Members in Attendance: Rebecca Curran, Bob Schaeffne,r Andrew Christianson, Roseanna
Ferrante, Edward Nilson, Barton Hyte, Steve Leverone (non-voting member)

4 Foster Project, Walther Jacot (Architect) Applying for Dawn and Noel Henrich
Want to add to accessory building for storage of boating equipment. Demolish pool house
building and add second story on garage as a pool house with deck off of 2nd floor of
garage/pool house-full bathroom there for guests. House from 70’s and the addition is being
created to compliment it. Reducing driveway to get rid of some concrete. Staying within
existing footprint. No change in house. 850 sq. ft. in new space above garage.
       Public Comments
       1 letter in support 6 Foster in favor
       Unanimous in favor

50C Cloutmans Lane:Veronica Hobson (Architect) for Barry and Julie Kelly
House built 1929. It’s in FEMA flood zone. Want to destroy structure and move it out of
VE (Flood Elevation) zone, according to FEMA guideline. Shorelines expanded single
dwelling.Similar size footprint but a bit taller. Losing some driveway Same grading, so
potential flooding still an issue Moving 13 ft. back and to the right a little. Not invading anyone
else’s sight lines.Planning to use materials from New England. Building will be elevated on
piers to put in compliance with the FEMA requirement. Outside, natural cedar Trim, composite
fiberPV panels on 2 of the roofs that won’t be visible from water way. Conservation
Commission looked at trees. The few trees that will need to be removed will be replanted (a
few evergreens) Have asked all neighbors for OK, and those except for two are in writing. The
remaining 2 gave a verbal OK

Public Comment:

Unanimous in favor
