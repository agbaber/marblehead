OBSERVER REPORT
Task Force Against Discrimination - January 10, 2022
LWVM Observer: Shari Pressman

Members in attendance via Zoom: Helaine Hazlett(co-chair), Anne Marie Hunter
(co-chair), Chief Dennis King (ex-officio member), Deacon John Whipple, Scott
Marcus, Alexis Earp (Student Representative), Reece Dahlberg, Diane Gora

Louis Meyi and Jay Morrison (liaisons to Marblehead Racial Justice Team)

Police Chief Report: Chief King reported that towns are working together to
investigate incidents; extra protection will be in place at Jewish holidays;
Upcoming Housing panel discussion with very strong panelists to discuss current
situation and historical perspective in Massachusetts at a date TBA late in
February; Fair Housing’s input will be sought

Land Acknowledgement: Statement read acknowledging that Marblehead was
built on land stewarded by the Naumkeag tribe; discussion ensued about final
draft of statement and how frequently and at the beginning of which Town
meetings, Boards and Committees TFAD would seek permission for it to be read;
the Task Force can sponsor the article as a group; discussion ensued about how
and whether to acknowledge the sub-committee that wrote the statement;
Unanimous vote to go forward with a statement

Update on Marblehead Schools’ Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Program:
Communitywide presentation scheduled for Thursday evening, February 10. DEI’s
mission statement will be read, teachers’ presentations about how DEI is taught
at each education level (elementary, middle, high school), multimedia
presentations by students
Fundraising: Helaine noted the need for the Task Force to raise funds; it is a
town-appointed committee with a fund for donations; she suggested a letter-
writing campaign or a town mailing

Marblehead Racial Justice Team: Louis reported working with Lauren
McCormack, Executive director of the Marblehead Museum and others on the DEI
event scheduled for February 10.

He also noted the ongoing “Conversations on Race” at Abbot Library; the
originator of the program, Mary Gardner, has left because of health issues;
Pamela Bartlett has assumed leadership. .

On Monday evening, January 24, Reverend Seth Carrier-Ladd, the Unitarian
Universalist Church minister, will lead a discussion titled “Calling Out Racism with
Love”. See Abbot Library’s website for more information on upcoming Monday
evening topics.

Next Meeting: Thursday, March 3, 2022
