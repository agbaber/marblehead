SUMMARY OF AGENDA ITEMS DISCUSSED:

Vegetation Management, Joe Kowalik, MMLD. The Village 13 substation will see an
upgrade. Stable road needed to support 50-ton loads. Last Oct. we were forced to
shut down. This is the first time we had to cope with major line issues. The first
week of Aug. a study will start looking at trees along line. We need right of ways
upgraded in October. The challenge is to make repairs while providing service. In
the past we used Pleasant Street. Bessom Street and West Shore drive have less
impact on existing infrastructure than Pleasant Street.

Hooper Goodwin: 5 Beacon Street. Shanty town at little harbor. The land is owned
by five people, called the first harbor associates. Asking for summer seasonal
service. Just want water, but no sewage. It may require excavation. Water is
available in the area. Superintendent of water department wants conservation
commission approval first. Approval pending presentation of a plan.
