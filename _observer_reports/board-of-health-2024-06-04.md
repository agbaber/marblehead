Marblehead HS Survey of Substance Use and Related Risk Factors
       Gina Hart, CSW, at MHS and Julia Ferrer, assistant superintendent, presented
the results from the survey of MGH Substance Abuse and Risk Factors (SURF) as well
as additional questions developed by MHS. This has been done each year for the past
three years so provides longitudinal comparisons. The survey was administered on
10/11/23 to 14-18 years olds for a total 743 students with 85% response rate. (There is
a provision for students to opt out of taking the survey.) Supplemental questions were
completed by 677 students. The results have been shared with faculty, school
committee, students, etc. As noted by the presenters one can’t be sure of 100%
honesty, but the survey is anonymous and can be opted out of. Numerous questions
were asked: connection with others; self-harm and suicide ideation or attempt; body
image; experience of discrimination by gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability,
social economic status; use of nicotine, cannabis, alcohol; age of first use; impaired
driving; texting, etc. In addition to the quantitative data there were focus groups to get
more qualitative data.
       The result showed often stable patterns with very little change over the past few
years. Some of the finding were as follows (2022 to 2023); anxiety 34->24%, depression
27->15%; suicidal thought 17->16%; discrimination experience same with sexual
orientation 7.9%, religion 7.5%; at risk for depression - 64% GLBQ, 73% transgender,
30% Asian, 23% Haitian, Black and 48% seniors; lifetime use alcohol 40%, cannabis
23%,, nicotine 34->38%; age of first use 29->32% 15 year olds, 24% 16 year olds; 62%
planned to decrease or quit nicotine.
       After the presentation of the data Gina and Julia discussed interventions and
programs that are ongoing or planned to address these risk factors.
Mental Health Task Force
       Ms. Miller presented the most recent news from the MHTF. Mark Lebon
previously the co-chair is now the chair. On 6/3 the NAN project (a project to promote
mental health awareness and suicide prevention which uses a peer-to-peer model) was
presented. The project involves a peer who shares their story of mental health issues
and who has recovered. This is very profound, moving, and gives hope to
recovery. There are a number of storytellers who can go into the schools to talk. So far
75 schools in Massachusetts have taken part in the NAN project. This project is FREE
to schools. The speakers are diverse - old/young, different languages, etc.
       Other programs that are planned are ones involving speakers to talk to students,
parents, and families. One would be in the fall and the other spring.
       On 6/22/24 MHD Counseling Center is to have field day 1-4 pm at Hobbs Field.
There will be a number of tables, games, etc. The MHTF’s Marblehead Cares will have
a table there.
       The next meeting in on 8/5/24 and Ms. Miller will be stepping down as co-chair
and will continue on in administrative role.

Review of Mission Statement and Role of Board of Health and Department of
Health
        The Board and HD reviewed the mission statement for each entity so that the
public knows what each does as well as the role of each. Mr. Petty read the current
mission statement from the Annual Report and had suggested additions that included
wording regarding regulating and enforcement. After much discussion including
comments from the next BOH member that the HD is the “operative” arm and the BOH
is the policy arm - “the HD shouldn’t be telling the BOH of what to do”, the mission
statement for now will remain unchanged.

Website Development
       Changes to the HD website are for the most part on hold as the town of MHD is
going to be revamping the entire system. Mr. Petty hopes that there will be a general
calendar of all the recurring meetings and events. Hopefully there will be a separation of
health from the waste issues. The new website is a high priority for the town manager
and will be done by an outside consultant.

Substance Abuse Talks/Speakers
      The plan is to perhaps have joint programs with the school. The high school
space offers a more “intimate” one for speakers.

Transfer Station Updates
        Mr. Petty reported sending 22 letters to contractors regarding the project and has
gotten 5 letters back suggesting interest. Each contractor was provided with all the
architectural, etc. plans. A consulting engineer is reviewing the bid document to see if
there are ways to save and speed construction. Installing the new scale and pit will be
the biggest delay. As far as the kiosk a 10x10 booth could be rented and its use could
be quickly implemented. Given all the steps ahead the project is likely to go out to bid in
the fall and then construction will depend on the contractor. Fortunately, much of the
material is on site (e.g. compactor).
