OBSERVER REPORT

Recreation and Parks Commission meeting
Tuesday, June 28, 2022 7 pm Community Center Conference Room
LWVM Observer - Kay Rieper

The meeting was called to order by the Chairman, Derek Norcross. All members
present: Linda Rice Collins, Matt Martin, Karin Ernst, Rosanna Ferrante, Peter
James, and Jaime Bloch.

The minutes from the last meeting were approved.

Derek was elected chairman, and Linda was elected vice-chairman. Peter
James was re-appointed Superintendent, and Jaime Bloch was re-appointed
Recreation Director.

Project Updates

Peter reported on many repair and replacement projects underway including
Hobbs playground, the shed at Gatchell’s, the Seaside tennis court re-surfacing,
(The pickle ball players are requesting a “strap” in the middle of the net, and that
the “kitchen” is painted a different color from the rest of the court.) Floats need
to be removed from the Devereux beach parking lots by the July 4 weekend, and
boat trailers at Riverhead Beach need to be removed after 24 hours. The infield
at Seaside should be worked on July 18 and 19. The water line to the Stramski
house is waiting for wetlands filing. Both Stramski’s parking lots need grading.
Town planner Becky Cutting needs to approve the change orders for the Nature
Center roof, and draw up contracts for having two vendors at the beach to work
on floats. The electrical power at the Nature Center is turned off until a problem
is repaired. The railing at the Crocker Park float has fallen into the water, but it
was recovered, and will be repaired. The Robies have submitted a plan for
native plantings behind their house abutting Chandler Hovery Park which they
will pay for.
A memorandum of understanding between the Commission and Bike Mor has
been drawn up and will be presented to the Bike Park sponsors after it has been
finalized and the insurance is approved.

Recreation

Jaime reported that Playground programs started on Monday. Unfortunately, it
rained, but her staff is great, and they got through it. The programs are full with
waitlists. She is starting fall planning. July 24 will be “Christmas in July” with
Fire Chief Santa.
A sign will be posted at the Gatchell’s parking lot allowing parking only for
vehicles using the park.

Correspondence

A request to install a bronze duck sculpture at Chandler Hovey was approved
with the condition that the size is reasonable.
The Eastern Yacht Club’s request for use of the Usher parking lot for boat
storage for a regatta from Sept. 7 to Sept. 28 was approved. The Board will ask
for a donation of $150.
A request for a wedding at Crocker Park on Sept 9 from 1-4 for 50 people was
approved.

There was a discussion of the dedicated website, whether the cost was worth the
benefit. There will be further discussion at a future meeting.

The next meeting will be July13 at 5:30, and the Sports Committee will meet
afterward.
