OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Marblehead Disabilities Commission
February 12, 2025
LWVM Observer: Nancy Powell

Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell (Chair), Mara Dartly-Rocco (remote), Briggete Duffy, Amy
Hirschcron (remote), Samantha Marino, (remote), Andrea Mountain (remote)
Members Absent: Gail Mack
Guests Present: Logan Casey, Donna Cotterell, Joanna Rose

Update Mass Office of Disability Grant

The Commission has been awarded a $92,028 grant earmarked for Devereaux Beach ADA
upgrades. Town Sustainability Coordinator, Logan Casey, provided an update. The grant will
cover items such as: building a new ramp and replacing wood decking to improve restaurant
access, installing heavy duty portable beach access mats to enable easy access to the water
and the restrooms for wheelchair users and strollers, adjusting restroom amenities to meet ADA
guidelines, improving access to the playground, installing signage for handicap parking,
purchasing a new floating beach wheelchair. Mr. Casey noted the grant requires all updates be
completed by June 30th. A town resident who was present remotely, suggested it would be
helpful to ensure that doors to the bathrooms have push-down handles which require less fine
motor dexterity turn-knobs.

Disabled Parking at Magic Hat

Ms. Blaisdell accompanied Todd Bloodgood (MPS Facilities Director) on a walk-thru of the
Magic Hat Thrift Shop at the Veterans Middle School to determine the feasibility of creating an
accessible entrance. They concluded the upgrade would require the elimination of three parking
spaces in order to make room for a ramp and van turnaround. The doors leading to the shop
would need to have electronic door openers. Further, the shop interior presents a challenge for
wheelchair users as its narrow aisles would make managing corners, turning around, and
general maneuvering difficult.

139 Pleasant Street

This project has continued to be out of compliance with commercial building accessibility
requirements. Ms. Blaisdell was informed in writing by the State Architectural Access Board that
the owners have submitted a revised plan and the AAB will conduct a hearing on the matter on
4/7/25. The Disability Commission will send a representative to the hearing.
Website/Facebook Management

Some members have noticed an uptick in traffic to the Disabilities Commission FB page. Ms.
Blaisdell suggested one way to increase awareness would be to regularly post the monthly
meeting date/time/agenda.

New Business

Just prior to today’s meeting, Ms. Blaisdell was informed that new owners of 1 Atlantic Ave
(formerly Le Bistro Café) have been asked to submit architectural drawings of proposed
renovations noting that the entrance might not be compliant with accessibility regulations. The
building is located directly on the sidewalk making it difficult to accommodate a ramp at the main
entrance. The owner has requested a variance to allow construction of a separate accessible
entrance at the back of the restaurant. The Commission is concerned about the narrow width of
the alley that would lead to the back entrance. The AAB, which develops and enforces
regulations designed to make public buildings accessible and safe for use by people with
disabilities, will decide whether or not to grant a variance in this case.

Resignation

Cheryl LaLonde has resigned from the Commission. Members expressed gratitude for her
service and the time she has spent dedicated to its mission.
