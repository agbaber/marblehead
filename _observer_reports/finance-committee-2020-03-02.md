MARBLEHEAD LEAGUE OF WOMEN VOTERS
                        OBSERVER CORP REPORT FORM

League Observer: Katharine Redmond
Finance Committee: 3/2/2020
Members in Attendance: Ben Berman, Emily Belfbecker, Kevin Broderick, Zur
Muhlen, and Alec Goolsby. Yeimi Colon, Jason Silva, and Alison Nieto were also in
attendance.

Meeting posted in advance in handicapped accessible location

Minutes: Minutes from the January 27, 2020 meeting were voted on and approved.

Liaison Meetings: Finance Committee members formed smaller liaison groups to
work with department heads, managers, administrators, and elected officials to
review their respective budgets with the goal of limiting increases to contractual
obligations for a “level service” budget. Results of the following liaison meetings
were presented as follows:

   •   Town Clerk/Election and Registration: It is unclear if early voting in 2020
       will put pressure on the election budget. The FY 21 budget request of
       $207,856 was approved.
   •   Cemetery: Plot prices and fees have remained level since 2018. The average
       single grave lot cost is $1,500, and 242 lots from all locations are available.
       This is estimated to be 5 years worth of space. A FY 21 budget request of
       $417,055 was approved.
   •   Retirement: The Board added new members to the retirement rolls. They
       also limited part-time school employees from joining until they have been in
       service for approximately 2 years. In order to fully fund its retirement
       account, The Town is required by the Public Employees Retirement
       Administration Commission (PERAC) to pay an increased annual assessment
       of 8% until 2038. The FY 21 budget request of $3,513,768 was approved.
   •   Library: The FY 21 “level service” budget request of $1,188,856 was
       approved. (This does not include funding for the “Abbott Public Library
       Renovation Campaign.”)
   •   Planning Board: The FY 21 budget request of $1,675 was approved.
   •   Finance Committee: The FY 21 budget request of $149,904 was approved.
   •   Assessors: The FY 21 budget request of $293,627 was approved.

Upcoming Meetings:

   •   March 30: Parks and Recreation, Selectmen, and Schools. Health and Waste,
       and Water and Sewer are also tentatively scheduled to be on the agenda for
       March 30.
   •   April 6: Warrant Hearing
