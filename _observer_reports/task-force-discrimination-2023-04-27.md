OBSERVER REPORT

Task Force Against Discrimination-4-27-23 Zoom
LWVM Observer: Shari Pressman

Members in Attendance-Diane Gora(co-chair), Deacon John (Joe) Whipple, Chris
Bruell, Candice Sliney, Reece Dahlberg, Kim Gubelman, Chief of Police Dennis
King (ex-officio)
Also present: Jay Morrison, Marblehead Racial Justice rep. to TFAD

  1. Welcome and Introductions; New Member
     Diane Gora opened the meeting by having each member introduce
     themselves to new member Kim Gubelman; Kim teaches in the Computer
     Sciences Dept. at Northeastern and has two young children.
  2. Update on Police Department News
     Chief King reported no new incidents. The Village School incident report is
     nearing conclusion and will be made public at that time. He also spoke
     about the meeting for police chiefs at the Holocaust Museum in
     Washington,D.C. last week to learn about the role of police during the
     Holocaust and the role of police in present time. King described it as an
     “unbelievable” experience.
  3. Report on Marblehead Public Schools
     Candice reported that both Student Representatives, who were not present
     at the meeting, attended the New England Youth Congress concerning
     making schools safer.
  4. Update on Juneteenth Celebration—Wednesday, June 14 beginning at
     4:30
     The celebration will begin with drumming and storytelling with Greg Coles.
     A Select Board member will offer opening remarks, followed by reflections
     from the Reverend Andre Bennett, Pastor of Youth and Young Adults at
   Zion Baptist Church in Lynn, and Nicole Mclain, founder of the North Shore
   Juneteenth Celebration.
5. Update-Marblehead Pride Day- Reece
   Flag Raising on Saturday, June 3 at 3:00pm (rain date on June 10) on the
   Abbot Hall lawn. A celebration at the Beacon, 123 Pleasant St., is planned.
   See thebeaconmarblehead.com for details and reservations.
6. Update on Hispanic Heritage Month, September 15-October 15 was
   postponed until the next meeting.
7. Racial Justice Team-
   The annual exchange of portraits of John Lewis and Harriet Tubman is
   scheduled for Friday, May 5 at 8:30 am at Glover School. The entire 1st
   grade will sing. Jay noted Conversations on Race continues Monday
   evenings from 7:00-8:30 in person at the Marblehead Museum and via
   Zoom.
8. Future Agenda Items
   a.) A motion to form a Subcommittee for a TFAD booth at the Festival of
       Arts this summer was unanimously approved. Chris, Reece, and Kim will
       explore activities for a TFAD booth and continue conversations with
       Jodi-Tatiana Charles, president of the 2023 MFofA.
   b.) Diane will register TFAD for the Marblehead Chamber of Commerce and
       Discover Marblehead Spring Celebration on Saturday, May 13 at the
       National Grand Bank parking lot from 12-3.
   c.) Jack Attridge, Town Moderator, will read the Town of Marblehead Land
       Acknowledgement at the opening of Town Meeting
9. Next meeting date: Either May 18 or May 25
