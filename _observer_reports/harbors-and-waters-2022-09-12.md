Observer Report

HARBORS AND WATERS BOARD - September 12, 2022
LWVM Observer: Kathy Breslin

Location: Harbormaster’s quarters, 9 Ferry Lane (in person)

Members present: John Daub, Ken Breen, Jay Michaud, Clarke Smith, Peter Dragonis, Rick
Cuzner. Mark Souza, Harbormaster

Meeting Summary

Harbor Plan Presentation by Emily Innes: Emily Innes, principal at Innes Associates,
presented to the board a summary of the Draft Recommendations of the Marblehead Harbor Plan
they have been working on with Tighe and Bond, engineers. The next step will be to prioritize
the recommendations from the Woods Hole Group report done in 2020. A strategic plan will
need to be developed. There will be a stakeholder meeting on September 14, 2022, including the
Harbors and Waters Board for more input as well as a hybrid public forum on September 21,
2022, at Abbot Hall. The public will eventually be able to interact online with the GIS document
that Innes Associates and Tighe and Bond will prepare in the next month. As new data is
collected via this interactive online site, recommendations and priorities will evolve. A rough
draft for recommended actions will be formulated at the end of the month. Then it will be sent to
Becky Curran, Town Planner, for review over several weeks. Once the implementation process
begins, along with prioritization of projects, Innes Associates will identify potential funding
sources in order that grants can be applied for to at least partially finance the Harbor Plan. The
National Sea Grant Program has funded this first stage of the three-stage Harbor Plan.

Parkers Boatyard update: There is a new boat lift/hoist up and running. The use of the hoist
has requirements approved by Town Counsel. Operators are required to have a 3A permit. This
includes trained town employees and certain commercial fishermen and commercial services.
There is an annual fee to use the hoist. Lift use requires that the purpose will be “useful for the
harbor.” If small boats were to use the hoist they would need a town employee to operate it and
have stamped (certified) lifting straps on the boat.
Harbormaster Report: A new pump station has been installed. Crocker’s float is out of the
water. We are waiting to hear from Dave Haley on the rest of the float removal. Appreciation
was given by the Board to Mark Souza on helping with the successful running of the Star Worlds
Regatta. There have been no complaints about outside dining at the Landing. It was stated that
the State may not permit outside dining next year. The Chamber of Commerce has announced
that the recipients for this year’s Harbor Award will be the Harbors and Waters Board and the
Disabilities Commission, considering the installation of the first Hoyer lift which is at State
Street Landing. A Hoyer lift at Stramski’s for next year was suggested to help with ADA
compliance in the West Harbor.
