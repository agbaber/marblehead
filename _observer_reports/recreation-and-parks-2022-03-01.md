OBSERVER REPORT

Recreation and Parks Commission
Tuesday, March 1, 2022 6 p.m. zoom
LWVM observer - Kay Rieper

The meeting was called to order by Chairman, Derek Norcross.      All members were
present. The minutes from the last meeting were approved.

Appearances

Julie Portier Brown appeared on behalf of the Farm Direct Co-op requesting the use of
Stramski’s upper parking lot Tuesdays and Thursdays from 1 till 7:30 starting in June.
The request was approved with the suggestion that they look for another site, possibly
Eveleth School, for 2023 as there will be more activity with the Stramski house open.

Representatives of the Marblehead Family Fund reported on their progress for the re-do
of Hobbs Playground. With $10,000 from two events, $113,000 in individual donations,
and a grant from the A.D.A. of $65,000, they have raised $265,000 of the $305,000 they
need. In two weeks, they will put down a 50% deposit, and equipment will be delivered
June 15 after tee ball is over. The Commission has no more money in the Shattuck
fund for this year, but they are willing to possibly buy a piece of equipment not covered
by the grant.

Ongoing Projects

Peter James reported that the Rainbow Zone is out for bid, the cost to be shared with
Marblehead Youth Baseball, repairs have been made at Gatchell’s, the infield at
Seaside will be fixed later in the spring as well as the tennis courts. The handicapped
entrance to the Stramski’s has run into a couple problems that have extended it beyond
what is stated in the contract which is a concern.
Members asked for a spreadsheet on how the revolving fund is being used.

Recreation

Jaime Bloch reported that, sadly, the half day ski trip will not happen because the ski
areas have closed during the week to save their snow for the weekends. She plans a
bouncy house/mini golf event on March 30. She currently has 3 after school programs
going on at once in the Community Center. Summer programs are in place, and she is
already working on fall. She is working on a summer reading program in partnership
with the Library. Her main staffing concern is for the sailing program.

Other Business

Karin has gotten a positive response from a reference for the website producer.

Correspondence

A request from Laurie and Ben Willard to perform dock services at Riverhead Beach will
referred to the town planner, Becky Curran.

A request for a wedding at Chandler Hovey on May 28 was approved pending
insurance and that it’s only for the ceremony because it is Memorial Day weekend.

Linda Rice Collins suggested that the Commission up the fee for weddings from the
current $100,

The next meeting will be on March 15, hopefully in person. The meeting was
adjourned.
