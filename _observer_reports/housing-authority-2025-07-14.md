OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

MARBLEHEAD HOUSING AUTHORITY – July 14, 2025
LWVM Observer – Cathy Marie Michael

Board members present: Jenn Schaeffner, Bill Kuker, Terri Tauro,
Absent Kristin DuBay Horton, Jean Eldridge
Maintenance: Gary Dean
Executive Director: Cathy Hoog (CH)- Present

Public Comment

Tenant 1 - Thanked Maintenance for help with trash during strike. Need more lights,
pitch black in parking. Gary responded that lights will be installed

Tenant 2 - Farrell Court - She does not feel listened to or respected regarding the locks,
feels the money could have been better used for other purposes. CH responded that
they did meet with people to get comments

Tenant 3 - Janet - Farrell Court
Wondering why there always seems to be many vacancies while at the same time
there’s a huge waiting list.CH responded that there’s always an ebb and flow, also
sometimes people go through the entire application process, then visit the unit and turn
it down.
How does one get a transfer when being harassed, and why shouldn’t the harasser be
moved instead of the one being harassed? CH responded that we take harassment very
seriously

Tenant 4- Elaine - Roads School
Maintenance did a great job with the railing behind the building
Work Orders (WO) - Light is broken, need a WO, not safe for a disabled person,
wondering why it is taking so long to fix it. - Gary response - lights are scheduled to be
fixed

Bill - Our energy bill is extremely high, it seems as if no attention is being paid to
conservation, for example on the 3rd floor AC is often left on at 60 degrees, even if no
one is there. AC ran 24/7 during that heatwave. Jenn response - AC in common areas
and 4th floor are supposed to be left on auto but tenants change them. Terri and Jen
response - We are focusing on heating units because those are failing at the Roads
School
Tenant Elaine response - it would be helpful if the MHA posts guidelines so the ACs are
not always left on.
