OBSERVER REPORT

MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION: April 27, 2021 4:30PM via GoToMeeting
LWVM Observers: Jody Howard and Nancy Powell

Members Present: General Manager-Joseph Kowalik, Commissioners: Nathaniel Burke,
Michael Hull, Karl Johnson, Lisa Wolf

The Marblehead Municipal Light Department met with MMWEC (MA Municipal
Wholesale Electricity Company) to discuss Project 2015A, the Peabody gas-powered
peaking plant that was proposed in 2015 and voted on in 2017. It will be located in the
rear of the Pulaski Industrial Park, next to the Waters River Substation.

MMWEC says the plant will only be operating for 2.27% of the year during high energy
usage, with reduced carbon emissions. According to MMWEC’s Attorney Nick Scobbo,
the land and infrastructure on the site are ideal, giving the munis a chance to join
together to create a capacity asset and resource. At present Marblehead’s electricity is
37% carbon free, being supplied energy from wind, solar, and hydro.

Sustainable Marblehead made a presentation of objections to the plant, asking to put a
hold on the decision. The energy picture is quite different than it was in 2015. Not
intending to delay the project, Sustainable Marblehead is looking to generate energy
locally. They would like to see a comparison of the cost and storage capacity of a
Battery Energy System for peak demand against that of the proposed duel-fuel peaker
facility. Sustainable Marblehead asked if the Battery Energy Storage system would be
less costly to purchase without incurring a penalty for the delay in redesign.
