LWVM Observer – Shari Pressman

Present: Co-Chairs, Helaine Hazlett and Anne Marie Hunter, John Whipple, Scott
Marcus, Esther Mulroy, Alexis Earp, Candice Sliney, Reece Dahlberg, Chief Dennis
King, Diane Gora Guests: Deandra Williams, Marblehead METCO Director; Lauren

Report on Trans Day of Remembrance
Memorial on November 20 effective in remembering those trans whose lives were lost;
TFAD donated in their honor as did the JCC and Shubie’s

Update about Marblehead Schools’ Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee—
Reece Dahlberg
-Subcommittee of Planning for Success met to discuss goals for 2022
-One goal is to hold Parent/Community Engagement events in collaboration with the
Marblehead Racial Justice Team, the Task Force Against Discrimination, and
Marblehead Public Schools discussing how to be an antiracist. Panel sessions would
involve inviting speakers to talk about their experiences and work to be inclusive

Recent Incidents and Updates—Chief King
   -Antisemitic and racist graffiti found at the Transfer Station being investigated;
perpetrator(s) not identified yet
    -Chief spoke with school officials about tiny swastikas found scratched into desk at
the Middle School; propose conversation with students; will speak with other chiefs;
vandalism is considered a felony

Report on Community Read and Connection to Marblehead Public Schools—
postponed until next meeting

   -Meets 3rd Monday evening of the month in the library

Historical Commission website
  -next film will explore unmarked Black and Native American burying spaces
