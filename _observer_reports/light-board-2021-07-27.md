OBSERVER REPORT

MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT BOARD: July 27, 2021
LWVM Observer: Jody Howard

Michael Vigeant of Great Blue Research presented a summary of what his
company does: working with other companies, to include research being
developed from surveys given to residential customers and employees. There are
40-50 questions in a survey, which will determine customer satisfaction, and
employee satisfaction in trusting the teams they’re working with to improve
relationships.

Next, Kowalik and the Board had a discussion about Land in Ludlow to be used for
solar panels, totaling 6.9 megawatts, or possibly nuclear storage.

What to do with customers in arrears (60 days overdue)? There are currently 109
accounts overdue ($367k). No one wants to pull a meter.

Lisa Wolf presented the Marblehead Municipal Light Department’s Mission
Statement:
“The mission of MMLD is to provide reliable power as we work towards a goal of a
carbon free portfolio, while maintaining competitive rates in a safe working
environment for MMLD employees and its customers.” And below is Lisa’s
Working Draft **Means priority

*Upgrade the transformers and switchgear at the Village 13 Substation.
