OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Abbot Public Library Board of Trustees-Tuesday February 5, 2024
Hybrid Meeting/ Meeting held in the interim library
LWVM Observer: Brigitte Lagoutte

Trustees in attendance: Gary Amberik (Chair), John Williams, David Ross, Deb Payson,
and Kim Grad (Library Director). Kathy Barker and Jennifer Jewell (via zoom).

1. Minutes of January 8 meeting were approved.

2. Director's report:

The Library will be closed on February 19th (President's Day Holiday)

The Library hired Wesley Sueker as an Adult Services Librarian.
The Interim Library building still has problem with inconsistencies in the heating system,
and some lavatories. All problems are being looked at.

Several projects regarding the library renovations will be addressed during the month of
February:
      - Self-checkout equipment and digital signs will be ordered. (funded by the
        Second Century Fund) as well as several makerspace items. (funded by the
        Killam Fund)
      - Delivery of the book drop bins. (funded by Shattuck Fund)
      - Purchase of some audiovisual equipment will be made. (Funded by the Killam
         Fund and the APLF and the override)
      - Selection and purchase of the memorial benches.
      - On February 16, the MHTV will do a tour of the renovated library.
      - Work with the Driftwood Garden Club on plan and budget for the
        rehabilitation of the library grounds on 235 Pleasant. (Funded in part by the
        Donation Funds)

Several items will need to be purchased later on: new refrigerator, new book trucks,
shelving, and a People counting sensor.
The Interim Library will be closing on April 13, 2024 to start the transition to the new
library.

A Town Report is being prepared which will be presented to the Fin Com. The budget
goals are going level funded for the rest of the year.

The Library programs remain very active. The meeting rooms are completely booked for
the month of March. The Library membership is increasing, and the Cultural Counsel
Program has given $450 for STEM programs, and $900 for a 6-week genealogy course.

Another Library Festival is being planned for July 2024.
The New Library reopening is planned for late Spring/Early Summer.

3.Chair's and renovations reports:

The Building Renovations Committee will meet on February 21, and the next APLF
(Abbot Pub. Lib. Fund) meeting will be on March 12.
The library renovations are on target and progressing well.

There will be a Gala at the new library on Friday June 14, and a walkathon on June 15
to celebrate the reopening.

3 invoices were voted upon and approved:
- Wakefield Moving and Storage
- LD Russo construction
- CHA engineering consultants

4. Friends of the Library updates:

The Friends membership is growing.
The next meeting of the Friends will be on March 13 when current officers will be
presented.

The meeting was adjourned at 7:15pm.
Next meeting on March 4, 2024 at 6pm.
