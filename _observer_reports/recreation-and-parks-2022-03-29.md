OBSERVER REPORT

Recreation and Parks Commission
Tuesday, March 29, 2022.
Community Center Conference Room
LWVM Observer - Kay Rieper

The meeting was called to order by Chairman, Derek Norcross. All members were
present. The minutes from the last meeting were approved.

Three members of the “volleyball community” appeared before the commission
requesting that their Thursday night-playing time, which was discontinued during the
pandemic, be reinstated. They will meet with Jaime to see what can be done.

There was a discussion about the grooming of the baseball and softball fields to make
sure that softball gets equal treatment. Some issues on the school fields such as the
broken gates at Vets (caused by plowing) should be taken up the with the school A.D.
Rec. and Parks has repaired the backstop at the softball field and grooms the fields on
game days.

Park Update - Peter James
   • The tennis court resurfacing at Seaside will start after the high school tennis
      season is over and should be finished before the summer tennis programs begin.
   • The Rainbow Zone “carpeting” should be started about April 11 and will take a
      couple days.
   • The major work on the Seaside infield won’t be done until summer.
   • Bids for the roofing at the Nature Center are due Thursday.
   • A change order was approved to add $1800 to the handicap ramp construction at
      the Stramski house.
   • The tarps have been removed, and a thanks to the Fire Department who helped
      remove them without causing damage
   • The lower Village field has been lined for a men’s soccer game this weekend.
   • The fields will be ready for youth sports on April 1, much earlier than usual.
   • Peter has bought and installed new trough planters at Stramski’s.
Recreation - Jaime Bloch
  • March 30th is the Spring Fun Fest at Hobbs in conjunction with early dismissal.
  • Thursday evening there will be a program to raise awareness of LGBTQ issues
      for coaches and all interested people. It will be held in the high school
      auditorium from 6 - 8 pm.
  • Jaime is trying to schedule cleanups by school groups and Sustainable
      Marblehead so they don’t interfere with summer playground programs.
  • She is meeting with the Library about the summer reading programs. She hopes
      to have some books available at the playground venues.
  • Winter II programs have been filled to capacity. Spring programs will start after
      April vacation, and summer program planning is well underway. She has a need
      for sailing instructors.

Correspondence
   • The Commission approved a request to hang a white mesh banner at the
      Seaside Grandstand in honor of James Galante. It will be the same size as the
      one at Gatchell’s.
   • The Commission approved a request to use the Devereux beach parking lot on
      October 15 from 10 to 11 for the 5K Run for Miles for Mary.
   • It was approved for the Democratic Town Committee to use the Community
      Center parking lot on April 9 from 11 - 3 pm to gather signature for candidates
      pending the approval of the Town Clerk.
   • A request for holding a Sunrise Service on Easter at Devereux was approved.
