OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

FAIR HOUSING COMMITTEE (FHC) & HOUSING PRODUCTION PLAN
IMPLEMENTATION COMMITTEE (HPPIC)-6/17/25
Hybrid Meeting- Recorded
LWVM Observers- Julie Matuschak, Bonnie Grenier

Attendees from FHC: Kurt James, Alex Eitler, Dan Fox, Thatcher Kezer, Debra Larkin,
Deacon John Whipple
Attendees from HPPIC- Kurt James, Alex Eitler, Dan Fox, Thatcher Kezer, Deacon
John Whipple, Nisha Austin

Minutes from the meeting of 5/13/25 were approved.

Merger of the FHC and HPPIC committees was the agenda item for this meeting. There
is significant overlap in the responsibilities of these two committees. At the last meeting,
a motion was made and passed to merge the two committees, develop a new mission
statement, and suggest a membership roster for the merged committees.

There was discussion about the differences in the committees, for instance, the FHC
has a focus on minority/ low-income housing, whereas HPPIC does not. Dan Fox
suggested that the new mission statement preserve the purposes of both groups.

“The Marblehead Housing Committee” was agreed to as the proposed name for the
new committee.

Nisha Austin shared a proposed mission statement, which is as follows:

The Marblehead Housing Committee works to expand access to fair and affordable
housing for all residents through education, advocacy, and strategic implementation.
We help the public and town officials understand housing challenges and opportunities
while developing and implementing programs that expand, enhance, and create diverse
housing options in Marblehead.
It was voted on and approved that the following be forwarded to the Select Board;
       Merger of the two committees
       The recommended proposed name
       A proposed slate of committee members

There was discussion about the proposed slate of members for the new committee.
Thatcher Kezer felt his presence on the committee as Town Administrator was not
necessary, as he works closely with the Town Planner. Dan Fox stated he thought the
Select Board could send a liaison to the meetings rather than have a member of the
Board sit on the committee.

The number of at-large members to be included on the committee was discussed at
length. It was suggested that too many at large members could create a quorum issue.
After discussion, it was agreed that there be 9 members and 2 alternates on the
committee as follows:

Housing Authority Director
Town Planner
Task Force representative
Representative from the Disability Commission
Director of Community Development
Four at-large members
2 alternates

Both committees voted unanimously to forward to the Select Board the recommendation
to merge the two committees; adopt the proposed mission statement; and accept the
proposed slate of members for the committee.

The next meeting will be held on Jul 15, 2025
