OBSERVER REPORT

Marblehead Municipal Light Board: August 25, 2020. LWVM Observer: Jody Howard
Members in Attendance: Michael Hull, Lisa Wolf, Michael Maccario, and Karl Johnson.

Lisa Wolf, who ran for commissioner on the platform of changing the mission statement and
goals to include "clean energy," wanted the commissioners to vote on it at the meeting, but
Chairman Hull said all five commissioners should be there to discuss it and to vote on it. Karl
Johnson wanted to see the statement in writing. It was suggested that nuclear power should
not be precluded since 1/4 of our supply is nuclear. "Clean" complies with the language of
legislation in the Senate.

It was decided to discuss the change with all the commissioners and vote on it at the
September meeting.

The meeting adjourned to executive session.
