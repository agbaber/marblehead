OBSERVER REPORT

MARBLEHEAD DISABILITIES COMMISSION            October 14, 2021
LWVM Observer – Nancy Powell

Members Present: Laurie Blaisdell (Chair), Katie Farrell, Sue Harris, Amy
Hirschkron, Andrea Mountain
Members Absent: Cheryl LaLonde, Ed Lang
Guests: James Ashton, Jocelyn Cook, Richard Ramos

139 Pleasant Street Laurie gave an update on this property. On June 15th 2021
the Zoning Board granted the owners’ new request to rezone the property as
mixed-use (commercial 1st floor/residential 2nd and 3rd floor). When the Disability
Commission was notified of this, Sue contacted the Mass Office on Disability to
clarify the status of a temporary variance granted to the owners in February 2019
which they later withdrew. If granted, the variance would have allowed the
owners to avoid being required to make the first-floor commercial unit
wheelchair accessible. Commissioners reported the situation to the Mass Access
Architectural Board (MAAB). Laurie recently received a copy of a Stipulated Order
issued by the MAAB to the owners that grants them 60 days to submit a request
for a variance of 521 CMR 25.1 that requires an accessible entrance to a
commercial space. The Commission decided to revisit once the notice of
compliance or non-compliance is issued.

Harbor Access The Commission has been advocating for improvements that
would make the public docks, piers, and gangways ADA compliant and give
people with mobility impairments equal access to the harbor. Members reviewed
a report prepared by Sue and Amy who made site visits to Village, Stramski, and
Tucker’s Wharf to assess and make recommendations for accessibility
improvements. It was agreed that Sue, Amy, and Laurie will arrange a meeting
with Harbormaster, Mark Souza, to discuss their findings and suggest as a first
step installing a Hoyer lift at Tucker’s Wharf and thereby have a blueprint for
including another lift at one of the two piers/docks on the Salem Harbor side of
Marblehead. Laurie indicated the Harbormaster is amenable to making sure the
waterfront is ADA compliant and accessible to all.

Guest, James Ashton, reported that the town has hired a consultant to create a
Harbor Improvement Plan. The Board of Selectmen will announce a request for
letters of interest from parties who wish to participate and work with the
consultant to formulate the plan. Amy has been in touch with Town
Administrator, Jason Silva, about this issue and will contact him again expressing
the Commission’s strong desire to be included on this committee. Guest, Jocelyn
Cook, mentioned that, based on meetings of the Municipal Boot Camp being
conducted by Silva, both the police chief and the fire chief have expressed
interest in having the town purchase a Hoyer lift to be used in facilitating water
rescues.

Brown School Laurie, Katie, and Cheryl recently toured the Brown School with
Jeff Dougan and Ashley Santana of the Mass Office on Disability (MOD). Also
attending were Owner/Project Manager, Brian Johnson; Gilbane Construction’s
John Roche; Marblehead Schools Facilities Director, Todd Bloodgood; and
Associate Director of Student Services, Paula Donnelly. The ensuing MOD report
found relatively few violations of the Architectural Access Board guidelines,
among them the need to reconstruct a ramp leading to the stage in the
cafeteria/auditorium. Gilbane Construction has agreed to correct all violations.

Sidewalk Repairs Laurie shared that the Town has received a $25,000 grant to
hire a consultant to develop an ADA Transition Plan. The Commission had been
considering sponsoring an override to fund town-wide sidewalk repairs in order to
remove barriers, but have now decided to wait until the Transition Plan has been
developed. Laurie will reach out to Jason Silva to reinforce the importance of
having input from the Disabilities Commission in creating the plan.

Other Business Amy raised concerns about the lack of sign language interpreters
at Town Meeting. Commission members will try to be a presence at BOS meetings
to provide updates on access issues and request remedies as needed.
