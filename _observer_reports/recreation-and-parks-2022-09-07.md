OBSERVER REPORT

RECREATION AND PARKS - Wednesday, Sept. 7, 2022 7:30 pm
Community Center Conference Room
LWVM Observer - Steven Levy

Members present: Derek Norcross, Linda Rice-Collins, Karin Ernst, Matthew
Martin, Ferrante, Ballard

   • On a site near Green Street, a number of people are
     congregating informally with bikes. This is an annoyance to
     the neighborhood. Communications will be improved in the hopes of
     finding a solution.
   • The memorandum of Understanding Bike Mor/Nemba has some insurance
     issues.
   • A million dollars was donated to improve Reynolds Playground. It has not
     been spent. Some of it could be used for planning.
   • This year's weather was better than 2021. Receipts and expenses were
     both higher.
   • A field at the new Brown School was landscaped improperly. The
     landscaper will pay for part of the cost. The rest will come from the
     construction contingency fund. R&P is recommending sod not seed
     because it would be ready sooner.
   • Wedding and event requests were reviewed and approved after discussion.
   • The next meeting will be on Sept. 20.
