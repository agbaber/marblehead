OBSERVER REPORT

MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT BOARD: 2-1-22
LWVM Observer – Maggie Smist

Board: Michael Hull (Chair), Walter Homan, Karl Johnson, Lisa Wolf, Simon
Frechette. They were present in-person or on zoom
GM: Joseph Kowalik, Mgr. (in – person) Guests: Jean-Jacques Yarmoff,
Eileen Mathieu, Mathew Eid, Scott Kearns

November and January minutes were approved pending one small change
on a date.

Discussion for 2050 Power Portfolio Roadmap strategy was tabled to future
month.

Discussion of Go Green Now opt in rate for residential customer

A letter was read from a Central Street customer which addresses the Go-
Green Now initiative – the homeowner requested an opt-in rate for 100%
renewable energy to be voted on by the MMLB.

Scott Kearns discussed his wanting to go 100% renewable and also
discussed his electric car and savings of gas costs.

Joe discussed with Matt Barrett on how this opt-in rate would be
implemented - a dummy bill was reviewed and single line in the bill to
show the extra cost for the opt-in. Joe showed a spreadsheet showing
calculations based on average electric use in Marblehead. He also
presented going 75% carbon-free which gives another option. Customers
need to call in to opt-in.   There was a discussion of how state minimums
for 100% carbon free increases each year, and this allows Marblehead
customers to understand the power portfolio and participate in a positive
way.

Lisa asked about outreach to the community to sell the plan, and Joe
discussed using the website, social media and a re-start to the newsletter.
Joe wants to follow up with HS Green Committee and Sustainable
Marblehead. Simon floated the idea of moving to opt-out instead of opt-in,
and Joe mentioned the tone of the messaging being very different.

Lisa spoke about the change in the rate study going on at the same time.
She wants us to re-visit this program next year and Joe agree that it should
be recalibrated regularly at six and twelve months. Eileen stated her
household has already done a lot to go-green so we want to make sure we
don’t hit people twice. Scott was asked if you can opt-in and then opt-out if
you can’t make it in your budget, and this is an option.

Future plans include MMWEC allowing customers to finance equipment
such as heat pumps and pay through your bill. There was a discussion of
the difference between carbon free and renewable energy and how it is an
educational opportunity for our customers. The Board discussed using
term carbon-free instead of go-green.

A motion was put forth for opt-in to 100 percent renewable rate with
quarterly reviews. Passed unanimously.

Joe will talk with Matt Barrett and the MMLB plans to implement over the
next month.

Eileen had forwarded 2040 document for becoming net carbon free – Joe
will send on to the board.
Board reviewed town warrants back to 2018 which involved climate vision
documents and net zero planning process. Both the Green Committee and
the MMLB need to normalize relations and make sure both groups are on
the same page – this will be part of strategic planning.

MMWEC Project 2022A Announcement –

There was discussion of a new project which when rolled out includes a
battery electric storage facility. MMLB participation interest is now being
assessed. The facility will be located in Ludlow MA on MMWEC campus.
The MMLB is going to go into executive session to discuss interest in this
project. This project is similar to project 2015a.

The survey of Village 13 has been completed. The goal of the analysis is to
determine how to flatten electrical use peaks and determine how much
battery capacity we need in Marblehead.
