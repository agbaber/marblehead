OBSERVER CORPS REPORT

Task Force Against Discrimination—8/21/25—7:00pm
Zoom and Recorded
LWV Marblehead Observer—Shari Pressman

Members in Attendance—Helaine Hazlett, Diane Gora, Deacon Joe Whipple,
Reece Dahlberg, Jean Guastaferri

Update on Marblehead Police Dept- Chief King couldn’t be present

Update on Marblehead Public Schools—PCO president Reece reported
meeting with the newly appointed principal of Veterans’ Middle School,
Matthew LeVangie, in mid-July and earlier this week. She described the
meetings as cordial and productive.
The NEA (National Education Association) overturned the decision to boycott
ADL materials so ADL will still be present with programs. The MTA
(Massachusetts Teachers Association) also had materials online, but they
were never used. The MEA (Marblehead Teachers Association) and the School
Committee denounced the MTA antisemitic materials.

Farmer’s Market, August 23, 8:45-12:00 Jean, Reece and Joe will represent
TFAD at the booth

Reminder—All members present had been sworn in by the Select Board and
acknowledged the Sexual Harassment Policy/Ethics Training required by the
state for municipal employees

Update--Marblehead Racial Justice Team—Representatives not present

Next meeting is scheduled for September 25. Meeting adjourned at 8:00.
