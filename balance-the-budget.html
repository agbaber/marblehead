Balance the FY27 budget

Marblehead's FY27 budget proposal cuts $1.5M from schools and $4.89M from departments and transfers. An override (Tier 1, 2, or 3), if voters approve, raises levy capacity to fund part or all of the department side. Schools cuts and restorations are not affected by override outcome in FY27 (tier restorations for schools begin FY28).

Every department cut and skipped transfer below starts pre-checked — that's the town's no-override plan. Click a tier to see how much override revenue would let you restore. Uncheck items to use that revenue, or use the insurance and one-time-funds levers to test alternatives.

Schools (fixed for FY27)

$1.5M cut from schools at every tier this year. The override does not restore school funding until FY28; the FY27 schools line is $47.6M either way. DESE's FY27 Net School Spending floor for Marblehead is $34.4M, so the cut is well above the legal floor. This page focuses on the department side, where the override does affect FY27.
