How does Marblehead pay for its roads?

For about 25 years, Marblehead paid for its roads almost entirely with state Chapter 90 aid, which held around $449,000 a year through FY2025. The town's own guide puts the amount it needs at roughly $3 million a year, and says surface patches masked deeper structural problems during that stretch. Local tax dollars for road capital did not enter until 2022, when Town Meeting approved Article 11.
