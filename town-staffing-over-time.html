How Marblehead's workforce has changed

The org chart shows who runs Marblehead today. This is how that workforce got here: 19 years of town payroll, by department.

The 19-year arc

Total municipal payroll headcount rose from 999 in FY2008 to a peak of 1,212 in FY2019, then settled to 1,020 in FY2026. Schools are the larger share; the town side has stayed near 400 to 480 across the period.

Town and school payroll headcount, FY2008 to FY2026. Headcount, not FTE: a 0.1 FTE substitute counts as one. The FY2021 dip tracks the pandemic year. School staffing detail, including a FY2023 reporting change, is covered on the school staffing page.

Then versus now, by function

Two functions on the town side did not exist in FY2008: Human Resources (first appears as its own payroll line in FY2022) and Community Development and Planning (FY2018). Several line items stopped appearing as separate departments. Engineering, Drains, and Tree are now divisions of the Department of Public Works. Others continued in a different form after their separate payroll line ended: the Animal Inspector remains a Board of Health appointment, Town Counsel a stipend position, and the grant-funded tobacco-control work stayed under the Board of Health. Most departments that ran through the whole period moved by only a few positions.

In each row below, the open dot is the FY2008 count and the arrow points to the FY2026 count; the bold number is the change. Departments that were added or retired partway through the period are not shown here; they appear in the timeline that follows. Counts are headcount, not FTE.

The school side

Schools are the larger share of the municipal payroll, shown as the middle line in the chart at the top. The school workforce has its own story, including a FY2023 reporting change that briefly overstated education staff. That is covered on the school staffing page and the enrollment versus staffing chart, so this page keeps its focus on the town side.
