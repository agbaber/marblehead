Marblehead Town Checkbook

Every vendor payment in FY26 plus the budget the town adopted to fund them. Sourced directly from the town's spending and budget portals.

Two different "totals" appear on this page. The town runs two portals: a budget portal that tracks the appropriated Revised Budget ($206.06M for FY26) and the spending portal's Actual rollup ($140.45M booked through May 29), and a checkbook portal that lists vendor-payment transactions only ($98.07M of the $98.49M source, after redactions noted below). The gap between Actual and Checkbook is mostly payroll and inter-fund transfers, which don't flow through accounts payable.
What was redacted before publishing. The source checkbook export labels individual police/fire employees by surname under 111F INJURY LEAVE FUND (e.g. "BURT INJURY" paid to Spaulding Rehab) and student initials under school out-of-district tuition lines (e.g. "Out of district tuition B. Dob..." paid to Northshore Education Consortium). The Description column has been dropped from the published dataset, and 560 medical-claim rows ($413,167.81) across the 111F Injury Leave Fund (133 rows / $17,021.82) and Workers Compensation Fund (427 rows / $396,145.99) have been excluded entirely. The source portal still exposes this; aggregate totals for the excluded funds remain in the budget-vs-actual chart above so nothing is hidden, just de-identified.

About this data. The published checkbook table lists 15,001 individual vendor payments totaling $98,074,838.23 between July 1, 2025 and May 29, 2026, after redacting 560 medical-claim rows ($413,167.81) and the Description column. The source export also excludes payroll (paid through a separate payroll-system run that doesn't post to AP), inter-fund transfers, and intergovernmental remittances posted through the general ledger – that's why the spending portal's Actual ($140.4M) is higher than the checkbook total. About 1% of rows are credit/refund entries with negative amounts.

Source files in the repo. checkbook_FY26_2026-06-02.csv – redacted FY26 checkbook (Description column dropped; 111F + Workers Comp rows removed). checkbook_redaction_disclosure.json – what was removed and why. budget_actual_FY26.json – budget vs. actual rollups by Fund, Department, Category, Division, and Object, built from the budget portal's API and CSV exports. checkbook_performance.json – pre-rolled variance, vendor-concentration, multi-fund-vendor, and monthly-cadence aggregates used by the performance panels above.
