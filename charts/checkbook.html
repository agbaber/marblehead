Marblehead Town Checkbook

Every vendor payment in FY26 plus the budget the town adopted to fund them. Sourced directly from the town's spending and budget portals.

Two different "totals" appear on this page. The town runs two portals: a budget portal that tracks the appropriated Revised Budget ($206.06M for FY26) and the spending portal's Actual rollup ($140.45M booked through May 29), and a checkbook portal that lists vendor-payment transactions only ($98.49M through May 29). The gap between Actual and Checkbook is mostly payroll and inter-fund transfers, which don't flow through accounts payable. Numbers here are pulled directly from those portals on 2026-06-01.

Why some bars over-run: The Light Department ($13.2M actual) runs as a self-supporting enterprise with no appropriation, so its actuals show against a $0 budget under the Electric fund and Electricity object. Snow removal ($687K actual vs. $105K budget) is intentionally under-budgeted by policy and topped up with year-end transfers. Capital-article rows whose Original Budget is $0 received mid-year appropriations and are at or near 100% of Revised. None of these is a runaway expense.

About this data. The checkbook table lists 15,561 individual vendor payments totaling $98,488,006.04 between July 1, 2025 and May 29, 2026. It excludes payroll (paid through a separate payroll-system run that doesn't post to AP), inter-fund transfers, and intergovernmental remittances posted through the general ledger. That's why the spending portal's Actual ($140.4M) is higher than the checkbook total. 166 of the rows are credit/refund entries with negative amounts.

Source files in the repo. checkbook_FY26_2026-06-02.csv – raw FY26 checkbook export. budget_actual_FY26.json – budget vs. actual rollups by Fund, Department, Category, Division, and Object, built from the budget portal's API and CSV exports.
