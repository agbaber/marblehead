    Did You Know?

    On Transparency

    DC publishes budget data, but it's buried in 1,000+ page documents most residents never see. Rini believes you shouldn't need a law degree to understand where your taxes go.

    On Accountability

    DC collects performance data but doesn't use it to manage the city.
    Rini's plan: review every open DC Auditor report on Day One and deploy a team to translate findings into action.

    On the Basics

    Fill the potholes. Stop the wastewater spills. Improve 911 wait times. No new programs until we fix the basics.

    On Debt & Financing

    Nearly 15 cents of every local tax dollar goes to debt service and financing costs — not direct services for you.

    On Education

    DC invests more per-student than any other city in America, but we are ranked in the bottom half nationally. The question isn't how much we spend — it's whether that spending reaches classrooms.

    On the RFK Deal

    The FY2026 budget includes $681 million in public funds for the RFK stadium redevelopment. That's your money, but how will you benefit?
