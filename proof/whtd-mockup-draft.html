Cuts came through attrition, not layoffs. Police lost the School Resource Officer to patrol duty. DPW lost a heavy equipment operator, working foreman, and general laborer. The Community Development Director position was eliminated outright in the FY27 budget. Fire has held an unfilled vacancy for over a year, forcing 96-hour shifts.
