Schools budget history

Two numbers get called "the schools budget." Audited actuals from the town's ACFRs go back to fiscal year 2001. The superintendent's proposed budget, by category, goes back to fiscal year 2018, from School Committee meeting packets. Both are below.

What the schools proposed, by category (fiscal year 2026 and fiscal year 2027)

These amounts are the superintendent's level-funded proposed budget for each fiscal year, taken from the February 2026 School Committee meeting packet. They are before the end-of-cycle reductions the packet lists on pages 14 through 30 (roughly $1.6 million cut from fiscal year 2027 to land on the level-funded target), so the totals here run higher than the packet's adopted-budget grand total. Fixed Charges (health insurance and retirement) are not shown because Marblehead budgets these at the town level, not the school side.
