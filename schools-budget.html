Schools budget history

Two numbers get called "the schools budget." Audited actuals from the town's ACFRs go back to fiscal year 2001. The superintendent's proposed budget, by category, goes back to fiscal year 2018, from School Committee meeting packets. Both are below.

Sources and notes

Panel 1 comes from education_expenditure_FY01-24.csv (each year's Education line from that fiscal year's ACFR). FY22 and FY23 are one-time reporting anomalies (GASB liability adjustment and prior-year adjustments); excluding them the underlying trend 2015 to 2024 is broadly linear.

Panel 2 comes from mps_proposed_budget_by_category.csv, extracted by extract_mps_proposed_budget.mjs from the February 5, 2026 packet. Bucket sums total $48.6M in FY26 and $50.0M in FY27; the packet's headline of $49.1M for both years reflects town-side Fixed Charges (about $883K, not shown here) plus roughly $1.6M in entity-level reductions taken to hit the level-funded target. FY18 through FY25 land in follow-up updates.

Peer block totals from peer_schedule_a_expenditures.csv (DOR Schedule A). Population from dor_income_eqv_pop_FY27.csv. Enrollment from dese_peer_enrollment_FY24.csv (DESE E2C Hub). Sorted by per-capita because sorting by raw dollars just ranks by town size.
