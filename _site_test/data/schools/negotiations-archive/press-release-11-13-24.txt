                                          FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

                                               November 13, 2024

The Marblehead School Committee Bargaining Sub-committee (Sub-committee) met with the Marblehead
Education Association (Union) for its fourth mediation session totaling 34.5 hours of mediation since
Saturday morning.

The Sub-committee offered again to provide the Unions core bargaining team (up to 15 members) paid
release time to attend mediation until contracts are reached if the Union would cancel its strike as has been
ordered.

The Sub-committee presented comprehensive package proposals for four of the five units this afternoon. The
Union presented one package proposal for Custodians after 2:30 pm that was a take it or leave it proposal that
expired at 5 pm.

Following the Union having left mediation to attend a rally at Abbot Hall which started at 5:30 and the Union
returned in time to hold a press conference outside the Marblehead High School at 7:30 while the Mediators
and Sub-committee awaited replies from proposals inside. The Sub-Committee left at 9:45 after realizing the
Mediator and Union both left Mediation without informing the Sub-Committee.

The Sub-committee and Union were directed to reconvene tomorrow, Thursday November 14th.
