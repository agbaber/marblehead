                               Joint Committee on School Safety
   The Committee and the MEA agree to establish a joint committee on school safety with the following
                                              members:
                  Appointed by                                   Members
                  MEA                          Up to and including 10 members which will
                                              include one employee from each of the MEA
                                                    units and the MEA Co-presidents.

                Superintendent                   Up to and including 10 members which will
                                                    include the superintendent/designee.


  The Joint Committee on School Safety shall be co-chaired by the Superintendent/designee and one of the
MEA Co-presidents. The Joint Committee on School Safety will meet two times per academic year outside of
 the student school day but may meet more or less frequently by agreement. The Joint Committee on School
                Safety may make recommendations to the School Committee and the MEA.
