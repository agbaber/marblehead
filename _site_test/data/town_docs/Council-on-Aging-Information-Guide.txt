PROGRAMS & SERVICES

• Transportation: 4 COA vans; rides to medical, grocery, pharmacy, library & all programming

• Outreach: 1,839 calls; 85 active case management clients; 411 home & 622 office visits; 46
  benefit applications; monthly support groups offered

• Nutrition: Grab-n-Go (3x/wk), Tuesday lunches, Pop-Up Café, St. Patrick's & Thanksgiving
  luncheons

• Health/Wellness: 48 blood pressure clinics, flu clinic (92 seniors), foot care (117 appts), free
  Parkinson's fitness classes

• Fitness: 12 class types with certified trainers & PTs; upgraded fitness center; 726 members
  participated in 18,834 classes

• Recreation: 929 participants in 6,779 events — Bocce (86 members), Curling, Bridge, Mah Jong,
  Golf, Cribbage, Quilting & Knitting

• Community Education: Medicare, legal, cybersecurity, MBTA, history & monthly Rep. Armini
  sessions

• Veterans Coffee Hour: Monthly; 12–26 vets/session; bi-annual breakfasts serving 136 veterans



COMMUNITY HIGHLIGHTS

• Bibbo Park dedicated Aug. 2025 – 180+ attendees; state citations awarded; $250,000 raised
  from Shattuck Fund, FCOA & private donations

• Opening Minds Through Art: Intergenerational dementia program pairing seniors with MHS
  students; featured in local media; continuing in 2026

• MHS Film Club & Sports: Chair Volleyball, Indoor Curling & Zumba with high schoolers; 9th
  Grade Volunteer Day (6,000 envelopes labeled)

• Police TRIAD: Formal MPD–COA partnership for senior wellness & engagement; offers Citizens
  Police Academy program

• Home Safety: 35+ evaluations; smoke/CO detectors with MFD; funded by MFHS ($3,000)

• Social Events: Thanksgiving with the Masons, Veterans Breakfast with the Masons, St. Patrick's
  Day, Holiday Open House, Volunteer Luncheon & recreational tournaments

• Anti-isolation trips: Woodman's of Essex, Boston Ferry & Trolley Tour; monthly movies & day
  trips

✓ INVEST in Marblehead
Full Funding — Override Passes

✓Commercial kitchen fully operational (Tier 1) — weekly meal planning, grocery shopping, monthly Traveling
  Chef & pop-up home-cooked lunch programs all continue

✓Nutrition/Laborer position retained (Tier 1) — staff sets up/breaks down every class, manages all inventory,
  clears bocce court, cleans fitness equipment & gym floor, opens the Center each morning

✓Volunteer coordinator half-position maintained (Tier 1) — schedules 100+ volunteers, runs Senior Tax
  Work-Off Program & provides office support for transportation coordinator

✓Tuesday lunch & Café program restored & expanded (Tier 1) — ability to grow to a second lunch day and
  increase café lunches (space permitting); over 1,400 already served (Jan.–Apr.)

✓Transportation needs fully met (Tier 1) — all 4 vans running M–F 8am–3pm; additional out-of-town drives for
  medical appointments; vehicle maintenance coordinated with Highway Dept & outside vendors

✓Full AV & technology support continues (Tier 1) — senior tech assistance, online class support, sound
  system & audiovisual setup for every program maintained by nutrition/laborer staff

✓Part-Time Social Worker hired (Tier 2) — expanded outreach capacity to support growing senior caseload,
  behavioral health needs & housing referrals

✓Funding for program supplies & equipment maintenance (Tier 2) — supports arts, recreation, educational
  & wellness programming materials across all COA programs


  ✓ Tier 1 provides level funding, the COA operates at full capacity — delivering the complete range of
  services, programs & staffing that Marblehead's seniors depend on.




✗ No Override
Budget Cuts Take Effect

✗Tuesday lunch & Café program eliminated — loss of the nutrition coordinator position means no meal
  planning, no grocery shopping, no weekly lunch for seniors who rely on it

✗Commercial kitchen becomes unusable — pop-up lunches & monthly Traveling Chef program cancelled; a
  town asset sits idle with no one to operate or maintain it

✗Transportation requests cancelled — loss of nutrition position also eliminates the backup MassDOT-certified
  driver; rides must be turned away

✗Program setup & maintenance eliminated — no one to set up/break down classes, manage inventory, clean
  gym floor & equipment, clear bocce court, or open the Center

✗Volunteer coordination collapses — 100+ volunteers go unscheduled; Senior Tax Work-Off Program at risk;
 office support for transportation coordinator lost

✗Technology & AV support gone — no staff to assist seniors with technology, run online classes, or support
  sound systems; programs dependent on AV cannot run
