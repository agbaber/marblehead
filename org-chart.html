Your role

The voters of Marblehead elect the boards, fund the budget, and have the last word on overrides and bylaws. Here are the levers, plainly.

Vote

  • Annual town election (early May) – picks Select Board, School Committee, Town Moderator, Light Commission, and the other elected seats.
  • Town Meeting – every registered voter is a member; you vote directly on the budget, overrides, and bylaws.
  • Ballot questions – overrides, debt exclusions, and charter changes appear on the May ballot.

Show up

  • Public comment – most boards take comment at the start of the meeting.
  • Town Meeting attendance – quorum matters; this is the only body that can approve the budget.
  • Run for a seat – most boards have at least one seat up every May. Nomination papers open in late winter through the Town Clerk.

Ask

  • Public records request – MGL c.66 §10 gives you the right to request town and school records.
  • Email or call elected reps directly – Select Board and School Committee members publish email addresses on their respective official sites.
  • File a citizen petition – 10 registered voters can put an article on the Town Meeting warrant.
