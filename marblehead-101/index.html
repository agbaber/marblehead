Why this exists

marbleheaddata.org publishes deep pages on each piece of town government and the FY27 override debate. A first-time resident has no single starting path through them. Marblehead 101 is that path: a guided ~25-minute read with exit ramps to the detailed pages. The site does not take a position on the override; this primer does not either.
