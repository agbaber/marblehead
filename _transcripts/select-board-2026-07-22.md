    dek: "Finance Committee chair Molly Teets presented the means-tested senior property tax exemption program signed into law by Governor Healey in April 2025, and the board approved all four eligibility parameters."
    summary: |
      Finance Committee Chair Molly Teets presented the Marblehead Means-Tested Senior Citizen Property Tax Exemption, a program she described as built specifically for Marblehead after benchmarking 20 comparable communities and vetting by the state House, Senate, and Department of Revenue before being signed into law without amendment.
      
      **How it works:**
      The exemption fills the gap when the state Senior Circuit Breaker Income Tax Credit (up to $2,820) does not bring a senior's property taxes plus 50% of their water/sewer bill down to 10% of their income. The Marblehead exemption covers that remaining gap.
      
      **Example calculation:**

      | Item | Amount |
      |---|---|
      | Annual property taxes | $8,500 |
      | 50% of water/sewer bill | $1,200 |
      | Less 10% of income ($60,000) | −$6,000 |
      | Less state circuit breaker credit | −$2,820 |
      | **Marblehead exemption** | **$880** |
      
      **FY27 rollout timeline:**
      - Application window: November, due November 30
      - Applicants notified: Spring
      - Reduction applied to fourth-quarter property tax bill
      
      **Board votes (all unanimous):**
      1. Maximum exemption per participant: **$2,000**
      2. Total program funding cap: **$200,000** (already in tax overlay as approved at 2025 Town Meeting)
      3. Eligibility guidelines:
         - No second home ownership
         - Income limits aligned with Commonwealth Senior Circuit Breaker: $75,000 (single, not head of household), $94,000 (head of household), $112,000 (married filing jointly)
      
      The program is estimated to serve approximately 100 eligible seniors. If fewer apply or exemption amounts are lower, unused funds remain in the overlay as surplus. If demand exceeds $200,000, all exemptions are reduced pro rata per the legislation.
