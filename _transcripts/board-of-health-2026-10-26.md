      Key points raised:
      - The state standard requires hiring a professional sound firm at several thousand dollars to establish baseline readings before a complaint can be actionable.
      - Police currently have no enforceable standard and can only ask neighbors to turn down noise voluntarily.

      - The residents described noise running up to 12 hours a day as their primary concern, not incidental sounds like basketball or pool parties.
