      - Local boards of health derive authority primarily from Massachusetts General Law Chapter 111, including the nuisance statute (§122) and noisome trades statute (§143).
      - Board of health regulations carry the force of state statute and have never been overturned by the Supreme Judicial Court or Appeals Court when properly deliberated.

      **Open Meeting Law**
      - A "meeting" requires deliberation by a quorum of a public body; with five members, a quorum is three.
      - Social media posts by board members on matters likely to come before the board can constitute deliberation, especially if a quorum is likely to see them.
      - Site visits are permissible but board members must not deliberate with one another during them.
