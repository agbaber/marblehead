GROUP INSURANCE

11 years, $28M, one budget line

Group Insurance came in 19% under budget every year for eleven years running. Peer towns book 0–5%. The unspent money has a destination.

TOWN DEBT

$107.4M of debt. Voters approved every dollar.

51 debt-exclusion ballot questions since 1988. Voters said yes 50 times. As of FY25, $9.1M paid down net of new issuance.
RETIREMENT

One promise on track, one deferred

The pension is set to be fully funded by 2036. Retiree health is a $142M promise that is about 3% funded. Roughly 7 cents of every general-fund dollar goes to people who no longer work for the town.
