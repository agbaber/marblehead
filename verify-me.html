Verify yourself

Marbleheaddata.org lets verified residents back ideas and weigh in on the town's open questions. You have two ways to verify.

Sign in with Facebook and claim your address

We will check your name against the FY2025 Marblehead assessor record for the address you claim. If your name matches the named owner, you are verified on the spot. If not (renter, recent buyer, spouse not on the deed), we will route you to a neighbor who can confirm.
