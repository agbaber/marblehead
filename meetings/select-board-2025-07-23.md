## Cited section

**Ambulance Contract**

> This is a joint contract, approved by Swampscott the night before, which will provide enhanced competitiveness and include provisions for response times, performance oversight, and a 6-month notification period for municipalities to opt out. Beauport, which has experience serving multiple towns, will provide one fully staffed ALS ambulance and additional BLS coverage, with new co-branded vehicles.

**Catalog summary:** Approved a joint ambulance service contract between Marblehead, Swampscott, and Beauport Ambulance Services, Inc., providing one fully staffed ALS ambulance and additional BLS coverage with shared provisions for response times, performance oversight, and a 6-month opt-out notification period. Contract includes potential cost savings from a zero bid and supplier-handled medical supply replacements.

**Outcome:** Motion approved unanimously; contract begins August 15.
