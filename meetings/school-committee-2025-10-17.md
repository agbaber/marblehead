**Catalog summary:** Committee requested a zero-based budgeting approach examining staffing needs by building against enrollment projections, noting enrollment declined 800 students over eight years and that level funding with contractual increases would require $1.7 million in cuts or elimination of approximately 30 professional staff positions (10% of staff).
