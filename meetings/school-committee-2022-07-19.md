**Zero Based Budget Presentation by Michelle Cresta**

> Assistant Superintendent of Finance and Operations, Michelle Cresta gave an overview of what a zero based budgeting approach would entail. It was explained that the process was created to justify costs based on need in every salary line. Ms. Cresta also provided recommendations for planning for the FY24 budgeting season including beginning the process in October while incorporating an intensive overview of staffing levels, which was last completed some time ago.

**Catalog summary:** Director of Finance Michelle Cresta presented an overview of a zero-based budgeting approach for FY24, proposing the process be started in October with an intensive overview of staffing levels; she noted current staffing would not be adequate to fully implement zero-based budgeting.

**Outcome:** Presented and discussed; recommendation made to begin FY24 process in October with staffing review. No formal vote taken.
