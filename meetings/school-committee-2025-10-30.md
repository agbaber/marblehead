## Cited section

**Budget Subcommittee Report**

> Budget subcommittee met twice with finance committee liaison; all departments directed to prepare level service budgets and laid out a timeline (December preliminary information).

**Why this meeting matters in context:** The previous School Committee meeting on 2025-10-17 had ended with a committee request for a zero-based budgeting approach examining staffing against enrollment projections, with the Superintendent committing to provide school-by-school staffing breakdowns and methodology development scheduled for an Oct 28 meeting before budget packets distributed to principals Nov 3.

**What this meeting did instead:** The Budget Subcommittee directed all departments to prepare **level-service** budgets. The Oct 30 minutes contain no mention of zero-based budgeting, no methodology development, and none of the school-by-school staffing breakdowns the Superintendent had committed to two weeks earlier.

**Outcome:** Level-service direction adopted via the Budget Subcommittee timeline; no formal vote on the budget approach.
