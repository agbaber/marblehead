                                            MEMORANDUM

TO:           Marblehead School Committee
FROM:         Dr. John J. Buckey
C.C:          Michelle Cresta, Director of Finance
DATE:         12/2/2020
RE:           Acceptance of Donation of Memorial Bench



Earlier this fall, Mr. Dave Dumais reached out to school committee member Mr. David Harris sharing that
“In memory of my father-in-law, Bob Till, who recently passed away, our family would like to purchase and
donate a bench to MHS for placement at Piper Field. He was a member of the Marblehead Gridiron Club
since its formation and he was a volunteer on the sideline “chain gang” for over 50 years supporting MHS
football. Derek Norcross and Park & Rec. have agreed to help with the installation.”




Suggested Motion: Motion to approve acceptance of donation of a bench in memory of Mr. Bob Till from
his family for placement at Piper Field."
