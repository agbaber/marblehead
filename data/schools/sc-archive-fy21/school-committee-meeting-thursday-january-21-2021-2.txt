I first met with Beth Presson and her son, Jake Moore, in the summer of 2013. Jake and his
mother had concerns, questions, and worries about his transition to middle school. However,
over the next two years, not only did Jake grow to become comfortable here at MVMS, he truly
thrived. Nearly every morning as our students are entering the building, I remember greeting
Jake in the entry hallway as he excitedly headed to his first class. Unfortunately, after
transitioning to MHS in the Fall of 2015, Jake suffered a massive stroke and passed away on
October 19, 2015. His loss was very difficult for those of us who were so close with both him
and his family.

Jake was a great young man. An avid movie watcher and critic, he was a staple at Chet’s
Videos, and actually had his own section of recommended movies. He was also the cornerstone
of the MHTV program, “Marblehead Youth News.” He appeared in all 30 episodes, providing
movie reviews in his “Moore’s Movie Minutes” segment.

For the past five years, Beth Presson and I have been trying to get approval to install a small
memorial bench to honor Jake and his tremendous growth and success while at MVMS. If you
drive past the former location of Chet’s Videos, you can see a memorial bench dedicated to Jake
because of his love of movies.

This fall, we received an anonymous donation of a bench with a small memorial plaque in Jake’s
memory. I have included pictures below. Currently, the bench is placed outside of my office. It
is our hope to install it outside of the back door to MVMS for our school community to use. The
installation should be fairly easy for our maintenance crew, and will probably just include
bolting it into the concrete. If there are any costs, and the school district would rather not incur
them, I will pay for those costs myself.

I greatly appreciate your time and consideration of this memorial bench for Jake Moore.

January 19, 2021

Hello, Marblehead Public School Families,

We want to take a moment to introduce the Marblehead Special Education Parent Advisory
Council (SEPAC) to those of you who might not be familiar with our work and how we
might be of service to you.

We are a collective voice for families and students in the Marblehead School District. Our
main mission, and duty, is to help improve the education, well-being, and safety of
Marblehead's students with disabilities. We do this by working collaboratively with and
acting as advisory council to Marblehead Public School District officials and School
Committee members on matters of special education.

As a SEPAC, we also strive to:

   x   Facilitate communication between families and the District and School Committee.
   x   Encourage understanding, acceptance, and inclusion within the District of students
       with disabilities.
   x   Offer opportunities to advance learning and awareness for families, educators, and
       professionals of best practices for the education and safety of students with
       disabilities.
   x   Provide educational opportunities for the Marblehead community at-large on topics
       related to special education and the safety of students with disabilities.

We are a volunteer organization. Our members include parents and guardians of, and
individuals affiliated with, a Marblehead student with disabilities. Our professions are
wide-ranging, including education, the law, music, psychology, nursing, business, and more.

To get to know us, we welcome you and all community members to attend our programs
and meetings, which are all open to the public. To check meeting dates and times and for
more information about Marblehead SEPAC and its Board members, please visit our
website: https://www.mhdsepac.com/.

If you would like to receive SEPAC updates and news, we encourage you to join our email
list by submitting your name and email address to: marbleheadsepac@gmail.com.

If you have any questions, would like to get involved or simply hear about our current
initiatives, please contact mrschell.mhdsepac@gmail.com.

Sincerely,

The Board of the Marblehead Special Education Parent Advisory Council

                                              MEMORANDUM

TO:              Marblehead School Committee
FROM:            Michelle Cresta, Director of Finance
CC:              John J. Buckey, Superintendent
DATE:            January 13, 2021
RE:              FY21 Financial Reports as of December 31, 2020


Attached please find the year to date budget report as of December 31, 2020 along with a budget
transfer listing and a COVID Expenditure Report. The year to date budget report reflects that we have
spent a total of $14,936,104 or 36.86% of the FY21 budget.

There have not been any significant changes in spending during the month of December. While we
continue to monitor the special education out of district tuition costs, we have not seen any significant
changes. Our current projected short-fall in this area remains at $93,000.

Last month I reported that we continue to face a significant challenge with being able to secure substitute
teachers. This trend is also continuing. As of the end of December we have only spent $24,024 or 10%
of our substitute budget.

 Attached, please find a current list of the budget transfers and a COVID Expenditure Report.
In terms of COVID costs, we have spent $1,447,355 to date, we have encumbered $378,534 and we
anticipate additional costs of $87,597 for a total of $1,913,486. These figures remain consistent with the
past few months. We are anticipating some additional grant funds to cover COVID related costs. We
have not yet heard specifics on this funding, but expect to shortly.

We are projecting on-going COVID related costs for contracted cleaning services of $48,000 and storage
trailers of $4,500 for the remainder of the school year. A significant portion of these costs can be
covered with the remaining funds available within the ESSER Cares Act Grant and possibly the
additional funds that expected in the coming months.

If you should have any questions, please feel free to contact me at any time.
