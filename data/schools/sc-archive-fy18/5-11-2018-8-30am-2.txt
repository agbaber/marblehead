                                     Record of the Marblehead School Committee Meeting
                                                     Friday, May 11, 2018
                                                 School Administrative Offices


Members Present:                                Meredith Tedford, Kate Lipsitz, David Harris

Also:                                           Maryann Perry, Superintendent
                                                Dan Bauer, High School Principal
                                                Greg Ceglarski, Athletic Director

Members Absent:                                 Jennifer Schaeffner

I.   Initial Business

     A. Call to Order
        Ms. Tedford called the meeting to order at 8:30am.


II. Consent Agenda & Action Items
    1. Six boys will be going to the Maine Regatta. Mr. Ceglarski will be going on the trip with the boys and they
       will be staying on the Maine Maritime campus.

        Motion by Ms. Lipsitz to approve the overnight and out of state field trip for the boys Regatta. The motion was
        seconded by Mr. Harris and the Committee VOTED unanimously, 3-0, in favor.

VI. Closing Business
    A. Adjournment
        A motion to adjourn was made and seconded by Mr. Harris. The Committee unanimously VOTED to adjourn.
       The public session of the meeting ended at 8:35am.
