                                  Record of the Marblehead School Committee Meeting
                                                Monday, August 14, 2017
                                          Marblehead Schools Admin Building

Members Present:                            Meredith Tedford, Kate Lipsitz, Jennifer Schaeffner
                                            Susie Pratt, David Harris

Also:                                       Maryann Perry, Superintendent
                                            Ken Lord, Exec. Dir. Of Technology and Operations
                                            Amanda Maniaci, Director of Business and Finance


Members Absent:

I.     Initial Business

       A. Call to Order
          Ms. Tedford called the meeting to order at 8:00 am.

II. Consent Agenda & Action Items
    1. NRT Bus Inc. - METCO Transportation Contract
       Mr. Harris made motion to award the METCO Transportation Contract to NRT Bus Inc., inclusive of all terms and
       pricing within, for 180 school days at approximately $225,000.00. The motion was seconded by Ms. Lipsitz and
       the Committee VOTED unanimously, 5-0, in favor.

       2. Design Architect Contract
          Ms. Schaeffner made motion to accept the recommendation from the Gerry School Building committee and
          recommend to the Board of Selectmen, Raymond Design Associates for Designer Services for the Elbridge Gerry
          Feasibility Study for total value of $529,000 which is comprised of $425,000 for basic services and $104,000 for
          estimated allowance/reimbursable services. Said reimbursable services will be assigned and drawn down upon on a
          case by case basis as the feasibility study progresses and approved by the Gerry Building Committee. The motion
          was seconded by Ms. Pratt and the Committee VOTED unanimously, 5-0, in favor.

       3. Declare broken and old technology and other misc. equipment as recycling
         Ms. Schaeffner made motion to declare the list of broken or old technology and other miscellaneous equipment as
         recycling. The motion was seconded by Ms. Pratt and the Committee VOTED unanimously, 5-0, in favor.
