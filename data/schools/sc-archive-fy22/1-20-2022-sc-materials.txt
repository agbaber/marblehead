To:            Marblehead School Committee

From:          John J Buckey

Re:            Director of Student Services - Permanent Appointment

Date:          January 18, 2022

I am pleased to recommend that the School Committee appoint Dr. Paula Donnelly to the position of
Director of Student Services on a permanent basis. My recommendation is based on Paula’s performance
while serving in the Interim role for the past four months. Dr. Donnelly’s professional and leadership
skills, in my opinion, are excellent matches for the Marblehead Public Schools.
Given her tenure in the district, Paula has developed positive working relationships with all the key
stakeholders that the administrator in this role works with on a regular basis. This includes: central office
administrators and central office support staff; school principals and assistant principals; team chairs;
special education and student services staff; related therapies staff; the Special Education Advisory
Council, its leadership and members; and parents.
Paula assumed a department in distress and has worked tirelessly to attend to myriad issues across the
district and with the DESE. In that work, Paula has developed an excellent understanding of the entire
Student Services program, and she has a vision for its improvement for the future. She works well with our
Assistant Superintendent for Finance and Operation, Michelle Cresta and understands the need to regularly
monitor the financial aspects of the programs both from the revenue and expense sides. Under Paual’s
continued leadership I believe that services for students will become more focused and teachers/staff will
be provided the support they need. Professional development for staff will be timely and relevant.
I have had the opportunity to meet regularly with Paula throughout the fall and I have observed her in
our Leadership and Administrative Team Meetings. As a result, I recommend, without reservation,
her appointment as the permanent Director of Student Services.
If the Committee so votes, my plan will be to have Paula finish the current school year working under
the salary parameters of the current Interim contract which runs until June 30, 2022. Once appointed, we
will work on negotiations for the new contract which will take effect on July 1, 2022.
Suggested Motion:

Move to appoint Dr. Paula Donnelly as the Director of Student Services on a permanent basis.

To:             Marblehead School Committee

From:           John J Buckey

Re:             Sabbatical Leave Requests

Date:           January 18, 2022




Two requests for a sabbatical leave for the 2022-2023 school year have been submitted to my office. In accordance
with Article XXIII: Sabactical Leave of the Unit A contract, a response must be sent to the applicant by no later than
February 1st of the school year preceeding the school year for which the sabbatical leave is requested.

At this time, it would be my recommendation not to approve any sabbatical leave requests for the upcoming 2022-
20223 school year. MPS has not granted a sabbatical leave to anyone in recent memory, going back at least ten (10)
years. By Contract, to be eligible for sabbatical leave, a teacher must have been employed for at least (6) years. Losing
veteran staff for a year not knowing who we could attract for a single year appointment further substantiates my
hesitation in granting said requests. These teachers do have an option. Alternately, and in accordance with the Unit A
contract, applicants may consider submitting a formal request for an unpiad full school year leave.

I would recommend that we review the sabbatical leave provision of the Collective Bargaining Agreement – Article
XXIII and negotiate updated language and terms with the Marblehead Educators Association.

Hello Sarah,

I recently read in the Marblehead Reporter about the School Committee possibly
pursuing an override in order to secure the needed funding for the roof at the
High School and at Vets. According to the article, the two roof projects would
cost approximately $4 million.

Here at the Marblehead Community Charter Public School, we are also in need
of a roof replacement. The total cost for our project will likely be in the range of
$800,000 - $1 million.

I wonder if we may be able to partner on the pursuit of support for the override
and that MCCPS could be included to receive some funding for our roof project.

Currently there are 1321 students at MHS and Vets and a total of 2600 students
in the district. Our school serves 115 students from Marblehead currently.

The cost of the roof per student if you take the $4million and divide it by 2600
students it is $1565 per student.

For our 115 current students from Marblehead, that would be approximately
$180,000. While this is only a fraction of the cost of our project, it would be very
significant for us. Additionally, it is a wonderful way to partner together in support
of ALL students from Marblehead.

Thank you for your consideration. Please let me know if there is any additional
information you need from us.

-Peter Cohen
