         TO:             Marblehead School Committee
                         Marblehead Board of Selectmen

         FROM:           Michelle Cresta, Assistance Superintendent for Finance & Operations
         DATE:           March 16, 2022
         RE:             Submission of Statement of Interest to MSBA – School Roofs


         At the current time we are planning to submit a Statement of Interest (SOI) to the Massachusetts School
         Building Authority (MSBA) for the replacement of the roof on the Marblehead High School and a portion
         of the roof at the Veteran’s Middle School. The current estimates for the roofing projects are $3.9 million
         for the High School roof and $170,000 for the Veteran’s Middle School roof. This Statement of Interest
         submission would be under the Accelerated Repair program offered by the MSBA. The due date for the
         statement of interest is Friday, March 25, 2022.

         The submission of a statement of interest is to show the MSBA that we are in need of a building repair
         and that we hope to partner with them. This submission in no way guarantees us any funding, but this is
         just the first step in attempting to secure some funding for such a project. The timing and portion of
         funding is not known at this point in the process, but I can confidently state that this is not a particularly
         speedy process. If we are selected to move forward, our best guess is that the process to secure approval
         could take anywhere from 12 to 24 months or possibly longer. In speaking with the Project Manager
         from Leftfield who handled the Brown School Building project, a good estimate for percentage funding of
         the project is 36% for planning purposes.

         Given the fact that we are in need of roof replacements and the deadline for the Statement of Interest is
         pending, we respectfully request your approval to submit the Statement of Interest for both the
         Marblehead High School and the Veteran’s Middle School. The required motion of both the Board of
         Selectmen and the School Committee is listed below. A copy of the certified motion and vote will need
         to be filed with the statements.

         If you should have any questions, please feel free to reach out to me anytime. I can be reached by phone
         at 781-639-3140 x10108 or by email at cresta.michelle@marbleheadschools.org. Thank you for your
         consideration.

         Statement of Interest Form dated March 16, 2022 for the Marblehead High School and Veteran’s Middle
         School located at 2 Humphrey Street, Marblehead, MA and 217 Pleasant Street, Marblehead, MA

Re:            Glover School Principal - Permanent Appointment

Date:          March 16, 2022

I am pleased to announce that Hope Doran has accepted the position of Principal for the Glover
Elementary School. Hope is currently the Assistant Principal of Penn Brook Elementary School in
Georgetown, MA where she has served since 2014. Prior to that, Hope was the Interim Principal of North
Intermediate School with the Wilmington Public Schools. While in Wilmington, Hope served as an
Assistant Principal of Wilmington Middle School for three years and a sixth grade science teacher for eight
years.


Hope earned her Bachelor of Education degree at Framingham State in Elementary Education, a Master of
Education degree also from Framingham State University in Special Education and a Certificate of
Advanced Graduate Studies in school administration from American International College.


One of Hope’s references said “her greatest strength is caring about students.” This was clear throughout
the interview and forum processes. Her ability to be collaborative and inclusive with staff and families was
evident in her responses and the printed materials she shared.


We will be hosting Hope at the April Superintendent’s Coffee to be held at the Glover School. We are also
working on some transition days between now and her start date on July 1, 2022. We hope to include
another parent/community meet and greet with her.

                               MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") entered into this ________ day of ____________, 2022
by and between the Town of Marblehead ("Town") and the Marblehead School Department, by and
through the School Committee ("Schools").

WHEREAS, in an effort to save the Town and the Schools money and provide more efficient and
effective services and in an attempt to eliminate the duplication of services the parties are entering this
MOU; and

WHEREAS, in furtherance thereof the Town and the Schools have determined that the Town may use
the Eveleth School on a temporary basis to house the Abbot Public Library; and

WHEREAS, the parties understand that the MOU may be amended from time to time in order to fully
realize the benefit of this arrangement and assure that the costs are properly accounted for against the
responsible parties’ budgets.

NOW THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is
hereby acknowledged and for the mutual promises set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

      Use of Facilities.

      The Parties agree that the Town may use the Eveleth School located at 3 Brook Road, Marblehead,
      at no cost to the Town to house the Abbot Public Library on a temporary basis while the Town
      renovates the current Abbot Public Library Building.

      Any permanent structural improvement or modification to the Eveleth School or its grounds by
      the Town must have prior approval of the Schools.

      Services Performed.

      The parties hereto agree that the Town shall be responsible for the following:

             Maintain appropriate and comprehensive building and liability insurance coverage for the
              Eveleth School location while the Town is using the location for the Abbot Library. The
              Town will provide the Schools with proof of insurance prior to taking occupancy.

             Cover all maintenance, general repairs, and building system repairs of the Eveleth School
              while the Town is using the location for the Abbot Library.

             Cover any and all costs associated with any modifications or improvements of the Eveleth
              School that is necessary for the Town to use the location for the Abbot Library.

              Obtain any building permits that are necessary for any repairs, modifications,
              improvements, and or upgrades to the Eveleth School during the term of the Agreement.
              Pay for the costs of all building utilities/services for the duration of the Agreement.
            Provide all regular upkeep of the Eveleth School , including, but not limited to the
             following:

                   o Maintaining the grass and the field

                   o Snow Removal from parking lots and walkways

                   o Trash removal


      Term and Termination.

      Each party agrees that this MOU shall be effective from the date of execution through the
      remainder of FY23. In the event the parties desire to renew this agreement, they may do so
      with a mutually signed written agreement. No party may terminate this MOU without first
      providing the other with 90 clays written notice of termination. Upon said notice, at the end of
      the 90 period, this agreement shall be terminated and both parties shall be responsible for any
      costs incurred until the elate of termination.
