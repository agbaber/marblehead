                                        FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

                                                July 16, 2024




The Marblehead School Committee Bargaining Sub-committee (Sub-committee) had a lengthy meeting with
the Marblehead Education Association (Union) during our seventh bargaining session. The discussion was
devoted solely to the Custodians’ contract which expired June 30th 2024.

The Sub-Committee was hopeful both parties would be able to reach a consensus after presenting a package
proposal however the six hour meeting did not result in an agreement. The parties remain far apart on a
number of areas including wages and paid leaves.

Bargaining sessions are scheduled for August.
