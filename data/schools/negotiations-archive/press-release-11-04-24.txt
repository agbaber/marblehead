                                        FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

                                              November 4, 2024

The Marblehead School Committee Bargaining Sub-committee (Sub-committee) met with the Marblehead
Education Association (Union) in a bargaining session today, November 4th, 2024. The session was
dedicated to Unit A (the teachers). The MEA’s revised off the record proposal, which included a 24-hour
expiration date, provided no movement on their wages totaling an increased cost of $9,691,000 over the four-
year period. The MEA’s take it or leave it proposal remains more than $5,000,000 over the Sub-committee’s
current proposal. For perspective, the additional revenue increases projected for the schools are only
$1,000,000 per year therefore, the Sub-committee’s proposal would currently require either budget reductions
or an override to fund.

The Sub-committee asked the Union to jointly file for mediation with the Massachusetts Department of Labor
Relations (DLR) to help the parties reach an agreement. The DLR has a near perfect record for successfully
mediating agreements. The Sub-committee will proceed with filing a request for mediation with the DLR.
The Union bargaining team rejected the Sub-committee’s request for a mediator to help work towards settling
a new contract that meets the needs of the Union and takes into account the fiscal reality of our town.
