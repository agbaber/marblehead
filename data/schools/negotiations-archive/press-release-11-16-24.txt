                                        FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

                                             November 16, 2024

The Marblehead School Committee Bargaining Sub-committee (Sub-committee) is proud to announce a
tentative agreement has been reached with the MEA (Union) to establish a Joint Committee on School Safety.

This has been an important topic to the Union and the Sub-committee hopes today’s momentum will continue
and we will get to agreement soon.

The Sub-committee is committed to continuing mediation tonight in an attempt to get more tentative
agreements.
