                                                                       November 23, 2024

Good Evening Marblehead Families.

Although negotiations are continuing tonight, we wanted to call your attention to today’s
developments.

The Marblehead School Committee Bargaining Subcommittee attended mediation for
the 14th day. The Subcommittee has committed more than 150 hours over the last two
weeks to negotiating contracts with the Marblehead Education Association (MEA) and
getting our students back into school. Today, the Subcommittee proposed beginning the
students' day 5 minutes earlier, reducing the time teachers are required to report to work
prior to students and keeping the start of their work day the same. This increases time
on learning to allow for a full morning recess for elementary students. At this time, the
Union has pushed back on this request, and has not accepted this cost-free path to
getting elementary students a second recess.

The Subcommittee also increased the offer for paid parental leave to include 15 days
paid entirely by the employer with the balance of the leave to come from the employee’s
accrued time. This offer was not accepted. The Subcommittee continues its work tonight
to get our students back to school on Monday.

We will continue to update you as things develop. Please take the time to review the
information on our “Negotiations Updates” webpage.

Best regards,
