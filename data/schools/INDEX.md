# Marblehead Public Schools: Document Index

Index of public documents downloaded from
[marbleheadschools.org](https://www.marbleheadschools.org/) and converted to plain
text for grep-ability. Original PDFs are NOT committed to the repo; only text
extracts and this index are. The scraper at
[`scripts/scrape_schools_site.mjs`](../../scripts/scrape_schools_site.mjs) is re-runnable.

**Last scraped:** 2026-06-25. **830 documents** (517 native text, 313 OCR'd via Tesseract). Total committed text: 14997 KB.

## Citation discipline

Every text extract maps to a source URL in [`manifest.json`](manifest.json). When
citing a number, include the source URL and document date. OCR'd text in
particular has artifacts and is **not authoritative**; the original PDF is. To
verify a quote, refetch the source URL and read the original.

## One-sided sources flagged

The `negotiations-archive/` folder contains School Committee press releases and
statements from the fall-2024 Unit A contract dispute. **These are the SC's public
position, not neutral reporting.** Any citation drawn from that folder must be paired
with the MEA's contemporaneous statements or framed explicitly as the SC's position.

## Known gaps

- **MPS Policy Manual**: District policies (classifications A&ndash;L plus Procedures)
  live in [Google Drive folders](https://www.marbleheadschools.org/school-committee/mps-policy-manual)
  rather than directly on the school site. Drive folder contents are not scrape-able
  without authentication, so policy-text citations have to be fetched manually for now.
- **DESE Report Cards & MCAS**: External to the district site, at
  [reportcards.doe.mass.edu](https://reportcards.doe.mass.edu/2025/DistrictReportcard/01680000)
  and [profiles.doe.mass.edu](https://profiles.doe.mass.edu/general/general.aspx?orgcode=01680000&orgtypecode=5).
  These are state DESE artifacts, not district publications &mdash; a separate scrape project.
- **Budget Documents page**: The `/administration/business-and-finance-office/budget-documents`
  landing page is currently empty. The FY27 Superintendent's Proposed Budget is captured
  via the 2.5.2026 SC meeting packet inside `sc-meetings-fy26/`. Historical budget books
  for FY18&ndash;FY25 will be captured if they're embedded in their respective year's SC packets.
