Memorandum

TO: Marblehead School Committee

FROM: Julia Ferreira

DATE: August 30, 2024

RE: Social-Emotional Learning Curriculum Adoption Request

Suggested Motion: Motion to approve the Wayfinder program as the Social-Emotional Learning
curriculum for Marblehead Public Schools, grades PreK-12.

The Marblehead Public School Social-Emotional Learning Curriculum Committee, composed of
teachers, mental health staff, and administrators from all schools, participated in a
comprehensive review. After careful consideration, the Committee recommends to the
Superintendent and School Committee the adoption of Wayfinder to serve as the
social-emotional (SEL) curriculum for students grades PreK through 12. Wayfinder would
replace “Second Step,” an SEL curriculum previously utilized in Kindergarten through fifth
grade. The adoption of Wayfinder would be funded through use of ESSER funds.

Wayfinder was founded at Stanford University’s d.school with the vision of making school more
meaningful for students. Wayfinder started first with high school students, designing a
purpose-development curriculum under the advice of Stanford researcher, Dr. Bill Damon, who
hypothesizes that the student mental health crisis today is rooted in a lack of purpose. Wayfinder
is on a mission to help students connect to their experiences at school and develop a sense of
belonging, meaning and purpose.

Wayfinder designs everything using a human centered design process, conducting empathy based
interviews with thousands of students and teachers annually, co-creating curriculum content with
students and teachers in real classrooms, and testing the content across multiple classrooms
before it’s ever added to their platform of resources. This is critically important because content
focused on character development, SEL, and life-skills is only as effective as it resonates for
students' real lives.

Wayfinder meets our SEL Curriculum Committee’s vision because it includes engaging lessons
for students at all levels, professional development opportunities for educators, has easy to

implement and accessible materials for teachers, includes materials for family connections, and
includes an assessment tool. Additionally, our Student Opportunities Act Plan, approved by the
School Committee on March 21, 2024, outlines key initiatives the District will work to achieve
to meet students’ needs. One key initiative identified by key stakeholders was to implement a
multi-tiered system of support (MTSS) that helps all students progress both academically and in
their social, emotional, and behavioral development. The SOA plan outlines the goal of
providing a comprehensive set of Tier | social-emotional learning (SEL) and academic support
as part of the MTSS framework for grades K-12. Furthermore, in review of the data collected
from Marblehead High School, students indicated that there is more support needed for their
well-being and sense of agency. Additionally, data from our 2023-2024 professional
development staff survey indicated that educators need and want additional training and support
in regards to improving student behavior, emotional health, and belonging.

Wayfinder conducts 3rd party research every year showing statistical gains in all of CASEL 5
competency areas and in additional areas such as Purpose and Belonging. CASEL (Collaborative
for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning) is the trusted leader of SEL.

e Wayfinder is part of CASEL’s SELect programming, which includes a rigorous research
evaluation process to be included.

e Wayfinder’s Core Curriculum, Activity Library, and Waypoints assessment tool all guide
young people to access the skills and tools they need to thrive. Our curricular themes
support students to meaningfully connect with their inner selves, their local communities,
and the wider world. Wayfinder’s research-backed 6 Core Skills go beyond typical SEL
competencies to incorporate meaning-making, life readiness, and critical thinking skills
in order to connect classroom learning to the real world.

Wayfinder is a comprehensive MTSS solution, focused on Tier 1, 2, 3 resources for our
Marblehead students, and the vertically aligned curriculum will help educators meet the learning
standards outlined in DESE’s Comprehensive Health and Physical Education Standards. It also
has embedded accessibility accommodations for students who have disabilities, such as hard of
hearing and low vision, and includes bullying prevention lessons across grade levels. Wayfinder
is designed to support students in developing lives of meaning and purpose. Finally, being
life-ready and having the skills to make meaningful decisions that align with their sense of
purpose is essential for a student’s ability to thrive in school and in their lives.
