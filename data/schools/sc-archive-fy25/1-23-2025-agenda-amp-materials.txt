DECA State Career Development Conference and Competition

Participating in the state career development conference and competition offers invaluable
educational benefits for our students. It provides a platform for them to apply classroom knowledge
in real-world scenarios, enhancing their critical thinking and problem-solving skills. Furthermore,
these events encourage teamwork, leadership, and communication, which are essential for their
future careers.

DECA is a career and technical student organization that prepares emerging leaders and
entrepreneurs in marketing, finance, hospitality, and management. Founded in 1946, DECA has a
rich history of fostering business acumen and leadership skills among students across the globe. We
believe this experience will significantly contribute to their personal and academic growth.

FROM: Michael Pfifferling, Assistant Superintendent of Finance and Operations
DATE: January 21, 2025
RE: Retirement of Coffin School

Per School Committee Policy FCB, the School Committee must complete a “comprehensive closing
study” when any school facility is retired. As such, below is the closing study to the best of my

knowle

l.

dge. Attached is School Committee Policy FCB for your reference.

Age and current physical condition of the facilities, its operating systems, and program facilities

a. The Coffin School property, located at | Turner Road in Marblehead, sits on
approximately three acres of land. The school was originally built in 1948, with an annex
added in 1962, and it was most recently used as an elementary school serving students in
second and third grade. The school was closed in October 2021 when its operations were
shifted to the new Brown School (Lederman, 2023). The condition of the school is
considered poor due to its age and over 3 years of vacancy.

Adequacy of site, location, access, surrounding development, traffic patterns, and other

environmental conditions
a. The Coffin School is located in a residential neighborhood on the North side of

Marblehead. The school can be accessed via either West Side Drive or Green St.

Reassignment of children, including alternative plans according to Committee policy

a. Students who attended Coffin School were relocated to either Brown School or Glover
School in October of 2021 when construction at Brown School was completed.

Transportation factors, including numbers of children bussed, time, distance, and safety

a. No students are currently transported to or from Coffin School

Alternative uses of the building for school district purposes

a. At this time, the School District has declared the property surplus and as such, it will be

turned over to the Town to determine disposition.

Cost/Savings

a. Personnel
i. There are no personnel currently assigned to the Coffin School. All personnel
were relocated to other schools in late 2021.
b. Plant Operation
i. All cost savings related to the Coffin School have already been realized in prior
years budgets. With the approval of Marblehead Public Safety Officials, all
utilities and services have been disconnected from the building.
c. Transportation
i. Transportation operations ceased in October of 2021 when the facility to closed to
teaching and learning.
d. Capital Investment
i. N/A
e. Alternative Use
i. The School Committee and District Administration have not identified any
alternative use for the Coffin School.

7. Continuity of instructional and community programs

a. There is no expectation that the School Department will utilize the Coffin School to
service students of Marblehead at any time in the future. Nor it is expected that

community programs will benefit from use of these facilities.

Respectfully submitted,

Michael Pfifferling
