Overview

The MCAS assessment is crucial for evaluating the
academic performance of high needs students. It
provides educators with data to tailor instruction,
ensuring that individual needs are met. By
identifying strengths and weaknesses, the MCAS
helps schools implement strategies that foster
inclusive learning environments, ultimately
enhancing educational outcomes. Understanding
these results is essential for improving
educational equity and ensuring every student
has the opportunity to succeed.

SGP = Student Growth Percentiles

A student growth percentile measures a student's progress on MCAS compared to the
progress of other students with similar MCAS performance histories. SGPs range from

1 to 99, where higher numbers represent higher relative growth and lower numbers
represent lower growth.
