Call to Order and Roll Call (00:00:00–00:02:00)

Robidoux, and Assistant Superintendent Julia Ferreira.

Competency Determination Policy Discussion (00:02:00–00:42:00)

a. Superintendent John Robidoux presented draft competency determination policy (File
IKFE) required by state to replace MCAS as graduation competency measure.
b. Proposed policy requires students demonstrate competency by passing specific courses
with grade of 60 or higher:
   • English 9 and English 10
   • Algebra 1 and Geometry
   • Biology and one additional lab science
   • US History (beginning with class of 2027)
   • Students failing required courses placed on proficiency plans using portfolio
   assessments
c. Member Jennifer Schaeffner requested consideration of:
   • Extending competency requirements through 11th grade courses
   • Defining passing grade higher than 60
   • Reviewing student performance data
   • Considering MCAS scores as competency determination option
d. Chair Kate Schmeckpeper made motion to have administration prepare second draft and
bring policy to full school committee on December 4th; Schaeffner declined to second the
motion.
e. Subcommittee scheduled follow-up meeting for December 4th at 9:00 a.m. to review
revised draft; Robidoux noted district would submit current competency determination
process to DESE by December 31st deadline if policy not finalized.

Academic Achievement and Student Work Return Policy (00:42:00–00:53:00)

a. Jennifer Schaeffner requested review of Policy IK (Academic Achievement)
   • Parents reported concerns about tests and student work not being returned
   consistently as described in handbook
   • Handbook states all testing materials should be returned for corrective feedback, with
   exemptions only for summative assessments covering four or more weeks when
   formative assessments were previously returned
b. Superintendent John Robidoux stated test return is operational rather than policy matter;
teachers do not allow students to keep summative assessments to preserve test integrity
   • Committed to investigating whether handbook procedures are being followed
   consistently at high school, including adequate feedback and formative assessment
   return
c. Subcommittee agreed to table Policy IK for future review
   • Policy not updated since 2018, does not follow standard format, may need deletion or
   complete revamp

Facilities Naming Policy Discussion (00:54:00–01:03:00)

a. Jennifer Schaeffner raised concerns about facilities naming policy (File FF) following two
recent naming requests within less than a year.
   • Policy is unclear as it addresses both new and existing facilities
   • Suggested reviewing policy to establish clearer parameters before naming requests
   become more frequent
b. Chair Kate Schmeckpeper expressed preference to review policy in January when no
pending naming request exists; Superintendent John Robidoux cautioned that changing
policy while a naming request is pending could appear problematic to the public.
c. Schaeffner referenced Swansea Public Schools' 2021 naming policy as potential model,
which includes set time periods of five or nine years for naming designations, specific
eligible spaces, requirements that nominees meet at least six specified standards, and
language emphasizing naming should be done sparingly.
d. Schaeffner indicated she would prepare materials on the policy for the subcommittee's
December 4th meeting.

MASC Policies Review (01:03:00–01:30:00)

a. Five new MASC policies reviewed and recommended by Superintendent John Robidoux:
   • Policy ECAB (Access to Buildings and Grounds)
   • Policy EFBA (School Food and Nutrition Meal Modifications)
   • Policy EFE (Civil Rights Complaint Policy for Child Nutrition Programs)
   • Policy EHAA (District Security Relating to Technology)
   • Policy EHB (Data and Records Retention)
b. Jennifer Schaeffner made a motion to approve the five new policies for first reading; Kate
Schmeckpeper seconded the motion; the motion passed 2–0, with votes in favor: Jennifer
Schaeffner, Kate Schmeckpeper.
c. MASC revision recommendations for existing policies:
   • Policies EB, EBB, EBC, EEAA, EEAAA, EEAE, and EEAG approved for consent agenda
   • Policy ECAF (Security Cameras in Schools) held for further review due to concerns
   about public records and law enforcement access provisions
   • Three food service policies (EFC, EFD, EFBA) held until December 18th meeting to
   discuss meal-related policies together
d. Policy FF (Facilities Naming) review scheduled for December 18th meeting.
e. Kate Schmeckpeper made a motion to adjourn; Jennifer Schaeffner seconded the motion;
the motion passed 2–0, with votes in favor: Jennifer Schaeffner, Kate Schmeckpeper; the
meeting adjourned at 12:31pm.
