RE: User Fees for the 2024-2025 School Year

As one of the budgetary components to arrive a reduced services budget, we will have to increase all of
our user fees.

Our CURRENT user fees structure for the 2023-2024 school year is a flat fee structure. The rates are as
follows:

CURRENT YEAR: Athletics - Flat Fee

Athletics Per Student Fee
HS Athletics (unlimited) Ss 495
MS Athletics Fee (unlimited) S 220
Non-Athletics Per Student Fee
HS Clubs (Unlimited) § 140
Flag Football S 66

Elementary Activities (unlimited) S 140
Family Cap $ 800

The current structure also provides for a full fee waiver for students that qualify for free or reduced school
lunches.

After considerable review and discussions regarding our current fee structure we would like to present
you three user fee options for the 2024-2025 school year. All of these scenarios are expected to produce
the similar amounts of revenue.
All of the proposed options include a change in the fee waiver for free and reduced qualifying students
to be 75% of the fee with the student paying 25% of any fee. In addition, all options include a limited
additional financial aid opportunity for students that do not qualify for free or reduced lunch, but find
themselves in need of assistance. This additional option provides a waiver of 50% of the user fee.

Option 1 maintains the same structure and increases the fees and family cap.

MARBLEHEAD, MA PUBLIC SCHOOL DISTRICT

The Marblehead School Committee invites dynamic education leaders to apply for the position of Interim
Superintendent of Schools.

The Town of Marblehead is located on the North Shore of Massachusetts along Massachusetts Bay and Salem
Harbor. This picturesque seacoast suburban community of approximately 20,000 residents is rich in Revolutionary
War history. Ideally located between Boston and Cape Ann, Marblehead is a popular destination for sailing,
fishing and tourism.

Marblehead is a high-performing, top-tier district, serving approximately 2,800 students in five schools and
centering on academic achievement, social-emotional learning and personal growth, with a focus on inclusion for
special education. Rigorous academics and assessment are priorities, along with meeting the needs of each child
through specialized programs including ELL and a wide variety of special needs programs offered for our diverse
populations. There is a deep commitment by all faculty and staff to supporting students in achieving their
greatest potential. The Marblehead district has been a proud participating district in the METCO program for over
50 years. Marblehead’s FY 2024 budget is $ 45 million.

Marblehead is proud to embrace:

Tradition of being high-performance, high functioning school district on Boston’s North Shore
Strong parent and community engagement

Supporting our staff in implementation of current initiatives

Opportunities to aid in development of strategic plan and needs based budget.

Growing our current Student Services to meet the needs of all students.

Applicants must meet the following requirements:
» Experience leading a PreK-12 Public School district in Massachusetts
« Demonstrated leadership in budgets, curriculum, administration and education
= Massachusetts certification as Superintendent
