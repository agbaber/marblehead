                                     FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
                                         September 28, 2023
The Interim Superintendent Screening Committee, after reviewing several applications and performing
interviews, has selected Dr Theresa McGuinnes and Dr Jannell D. Pearson- Campbell as finalists to present
to the Marblehead School Committee.

The School Committee has scheduled public interviews of both candidates for Monday October 2, 2023 to
take place at the Marblehead High School Library.
