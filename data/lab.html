Data Lab

Every dataset behind marbleheaddata.org – town financials, statewide DOR records, the meeting-minutes catalog – in one SQLite database, queryable from your browser. No login, no signup, no rate limit. Results stay in your browser.

Type SQL into the box below and click Run. Click a table on the left to drop a starter SELECT into the editor. The full database is downloadable below for use in Python, R, DB Browser, or anywhere else SQLite is supported.
