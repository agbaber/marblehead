# FY26 actual road spending (from the town checkbook)

Source: `data/checkbook_FY26_2026-06-30.csv` (Town of Marblehead vendor
checkbook, full fiscal year FY2026, 2025-07-01 through 2026-06-30, ~17,900
rows). Cash-basis: these are vendor payments that cleared in FY26, not work
performed or dollars committed.

There is NO single "roads" line in the town's books. Road money is spread across
several funds and the operating budget, and many capital payments carry no
functional tag (the Division field on the Article 11 roadway payments is
literally "UNDEFINED"). The figures below are pulled together across funds.

## Defensible road/sidewalk spending, FY26

| Source (fund / division) | FY26 paid |
|---|---|
| Article 11 road & sidewalk capital (A11 2022 fund; descriptions: Roadway and Sidewalk, Sidewalk and ADA Transition, Pavement) | $1,873,129 |
| Chapter 90 (state aid fund; line painting, sidewalk/ADA) | $106,555 |
| ARPA sidewalk & ADA transition (COVID-19 ARPA fund) | $96,222 |
| PW Maintain Streets & Sidewalk (general-fund operating; town crews) | $50,213 |
| Street Opening Revolving Fund | $14,695 |
| Road Safety Grant | $1,783 |
| General-fund road maintenance (cold patch, EXPENSES) | $1,374 |
| **Core total** | **$2,143,971** |

Rounded: **about $2.1 million.**

## Key checks / caveats
- **The Article 11 fund is mostly NOT roads.** The "A11 2022 Capital Improvement"
  fund paid out ~$9.1M in FY26, but ~$7.2M of that is buildings (chiefly the high
  school roof/HVAC). Only ~$1.87M is roadway/sidewalk. Summing the fund would
  overstate road spend roughly fivefold. The $1.87M is a reproducible keyword
  match (roadway, sidewalk, pavement, ADA transition, reclamation), not a hand-pick.
- **Classification-dependent range: roughly $2.0M-$2.3M.** Items deliberately
  EXCLUDED because they are a different category or belong to another project:
  street lighting (~$61K), road restoration billed inside sewer/water capital
  projects (~$150K; correct project costing, not "roads"), a waste revolving-fund
  line (~$72K), and an unverified earmark (~$50K, no road description found).
- **Cash-basis, not work-basis.** Capital paid from bond proceeds can lag the
  paving it funds.
- **Comparison point:** ~$2.1M actual is below the ~$3M/yr the road program is
  budgeted for (Article 11 $12.475M / 5 yr + Chapter 90), consistent with the
  borrowing lag ($6.98M of the $12.475M still unissued at the FY25 audit).
