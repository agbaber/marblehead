# Chapter 128: Public Ways, Use of

## § 128-1 Reflectors required during certain hours.

No person shall use a public way within the Town, within the period from 1/2 hour after sunset to 1/2 hour before sunrise, while jogging, roller skating or skateboarding, without having a reflector or reflector tape attached to the front and back of such person, visible for a distance of 200 feet under normal atmospheric conditions.

## § 128-2 Violations and penalties.

Whoever violates any provision of this By-Law shall be liable to penalty of $10 for a first offense and $25 for a second or subsequent offense within a calendar year.
