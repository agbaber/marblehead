# Chapter 177: Town Property

## § 177-1 Reward for information.

Twenty-five dollars reward will be paid to any person for information leading to the conviction of anyone damaging Town property.

## § 177-2 Power to rent.

The Select Board shall have the power to rent Abbot Hall, the Old Town Hall, and other property of the Town under their jurisdiction, for any lawful purpose, provided, however, that at all times such renting shall be subject to the use by the Town for Town purposes.

## § 177-3 Insurance.

It shall be the duty of the Select Board as agents of the Town to keep insured against loss or damage or fire all insurable property of the Town, both real and personal. Each department of the Town, except the Board of Assessors, shall annually, on or before the first day of June, render a complete list of all Town property, both real and personal, under its control, to the Select Board, together with the description of said property and its estimate of the replacement value thereof. Each department shall further, forthwith upon the purchase or acquisition of any property, render a statement in writing to the Select Board, fully describing the same, with the replacement value thereof.

## § 177-4 Disposition of used equipment.

The Select Board and any Town board or officer having charge of any equipment used for Town purposes may, in purchasing new equipment authorized by the Town, sell or trade in used equipment which the new equipment replaces, and apply the proceeds of such sale or trade as a part of the purchase price of the new equipment.

## § 177-5 Authority.

The Select Board is hereby authorized to sell at public auction to the highest bidder any of the property which the Town may have acquired or may hereafter acquire through proceedings based upon nonpayment of taxes or under proceedings for the sale of lands of low value, to impose upon the property so sold such restrictions, reservations or conditions as shall be deemed expedient, and to execute and deliver in the name and under the seal of the Town a deed without covenant or other instruments therefor. Any bid deemed inadequate may be rejected.

## § 177-6 Notice.

Prior to any sale as heretofore authorized, the Select Board shall give prior notice of the time and place of said sale, including a description of each parcel to be sold or a statement that (as shall be the fact) said description is available during business hours in the office of the Town Treasurer, to be examined and copied at the copier's expense. Said notice shall be by publication at least weekly for the three consecutive weeks ending at least seven days prior to the date on which the sale is to occur, in a newspaper of general circulation in the Town. The sale as so publicized may, after being convened, be adjourned to a later time or different place, or both, provided notice of said adjournment and the time and place of the reconvened sale are published in such a newspaper at least seven days prior to the date the sale reconvenes.

## § 177-7 Repeal.

All by-laws or votes of the Town, or portions thereof, heretofore in force inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed.

## § 177-8 Violations and penalties.

A.
Violations of the Public Shade Tree Regulations of the Town of Marblehead which are approved by the Select Board, may be penalized by a noncriminal disposition in the manner provided in Chapter 40, Section 21D of the General Laws. For the purpose of this section, the specific penalty which is to apply for violation of said regulations shall be listed below and the Town officer or employee whose title(s) are listed shall be deemed the enforcing officer(s) for the regulations.
B.
Public shade tree regulations: Penalty for violation of the Public Shade Tree Regulations shall be as follows:
(1)
Three hundred dollars for each offense and on each day a violation exists may be deemed to be a separate offense. An offense shall be deemed to occur on each day from the day the offense(s) occurs through the date upon which a public hearing resulting in the resolution of the offense(s) is closed as determined by the Tree Warden; and
(2)
Said penalty shall be paid in accordance with MGL c. 40 § 21D. This section shall be enforced by the Tree Warden.

## § 177-9 Setback tree planting.

A.
The Town may plant trees within 20 feet of the public right-of-way, provided that written permission from the adjoining property owner is obtained first consistent with G.L.c. 87, sec. 7.
B.
The trees planted under this provision are defined as "public shade trees" and thus protected by all sections of G.L. c. 87.
