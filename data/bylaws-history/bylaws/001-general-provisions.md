# Chapter 1: General Provisions

## § 1-1 Title.

These By-Laws shall be designated as "By-Laws of the Town of Marblehead."

## § 1-2 Repeal of conflicting provisions.

So far as the provisions of these By-Laws are the same in effect as those of previously existing By-Laws, they shall be construed as a continuation of such By-Laws, but all By-Laws or portions thereof, heretofore in force inconsistent herewith, are hereby repealed.

## § 1-3 Rules of construction.

In all the By-Laws of the Town, the words and expressions shall, unless inconsistent with the manifest intent, be severally construed as follows:
A.
The words "public way" shall include any highway, town way, road, bridge, street, avenue, boulevard, roadway, parkway, lane, sidewalk or square.
B.
Words purporting to give joint authority to a board or committee shall give authority to a majority of such board or committee; the word "person" shall include corporations; words importing the singular number shall apply to the plural number; and words importing the masculine gender shall apply to the feminine gender.

## § 1-4 General penalty.

A.
Whoever violates a provision of any By-Law of the Town shall, unless other provision is expressly made, be liable to a penalty not exceeding $50 for each offense.
B.
Violation of the following portions of this By-Law may be penalized by a noncriminal disposition in the manner provided in Chapter 40, Section 21D of the General Laws. For the purpose of this section, the specific penalty which is to apply for violation of each such chapter shall be as listed below and the Town officers or employees whose titles are listed under each chapter shall be deemed to be enforcing officers for each such chapter. Each day on which a violation exists may be deemed to be a separate offense.
(1)
Chapter 190, Waterways.
(a)
Penalty for violation of any provision contained in Chapter 190 shall be as follows:
First offense: $50.
Second offense: $100.
Third and subsequent offenses: $150.
(b)
Enforcing persons: Harbormaster and Assistant Harbormasters.
(2)
Chapter 225, Landing Places.
(a)
Penalty for violation of any provision contained in Chapter 225 shall be $20 for each offense.
(b)
Enforcing persons: Harbormaster and Assistant Harbormasters.

## § 1-5 Authority to issue licenses and permits.

When in any By-Law anything is prohibited from being done without the license or permission of a certain officer or officers, such officer or officers shall have the power to license or permit such thing to be done.

## § 1-6 Amendments.

These By-Laws may be amended at any Town Meeting, an article or articles for that purpose having been inserted in the warrant for such meeting.
