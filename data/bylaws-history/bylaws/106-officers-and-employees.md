# Chapter 106: Officers and Employees

## § 106-1 Physical examination required.

Any person for any of the following positions, before becoming an employee of the Town, shall submit to a physical examination by a reputable doctor to be designated by the Select Board. Those position are:
Fire fighters
EMTs
Police officers
Any employee that works under the water department, sewer department, drains or department of public works, tree department, highway department
Any employee that works under the water department, sewer department, drains or department of public works, tree department, highway department.
Recreation and Parks employees
Board of Health employees
Inspectors
Custodial staff
Animal Control employees
Cemetery employees
Harbor Master employees
And any other position which the Human Resources Director or, if there is none, the Town Administrator determines to be in the best interest of the Town to require a physical examination prior to employment with the Town.

## § 106-2 Town moneys.

All money received by any Town officers or committees, or by any person acting for or on behalf of the Town, shall, upon the receipt of the same, be paid into the Town treasury, except as otherwise provided by the statutes of the commonwealth.

## § 106-3 Office hours.

All Town offices staffed by two or more full-time employees, including elected officials, shall remain open for business to service the public through the hours of 12:00 noon to 1:00 p.m.

## § 106-4 Town documents.

All the officers of the Town shall turn over to their successors in office at the expiration of their term of office all books, papers, documents, or other properties in their custody belonging to the Town, and all departments shall turn over to the Town Clerk all books, papers, and documents belonging to the Town and not necessary for the immediate use of the respective departments, and these shall be deposited in the Town vaults.

## § 106-5 Town Seal.

A.
The Town Clerk shall have the custody of the Town Seal.
B.
All conveyances under seal which may hereafter be executed by the Town, pursuant to a vote of the Town or otherwise, shall be sealed with such Seal and subscribed by a majority of the Select Board for the time being.

## § 106-6 Copies of deeds and other instruments.

The Town Clerk shall keep a true copy (in a book to be kept for such purpose alone) of all deeds or other instruments executed by the Select Board in accordance with the provisions of these By-Laws.

## § 106-7 Book of records; notification of Town Meeting votes.

The Town Clerk shall keep a book of records for the sole purpose of recording the location of all highways and Town ways within the Town, with an index thereto; shall notify immediately in writing all committees that may be elected or appointed at any Town Meeting, and the nature of the business upon which they are expected to act; shall notify the chair of departments and committees and officers of votes of the Town pertaining to their respective departments.

## § 106-8 Registry of Deeds.

It shall be the duty of the Town Clerk to see that every conveyance to the Town of any interest in land is properly recorded in the Registry of Deeds.

## § 106-9 Safekeeping of documents.

The Town Clerk shall be responsible for the safekeeping of all books, papers and plans, and all records which may be deposited in the vaults, and shall not allow anything to be taken from the Clerk's custody. The Clerk shall furnish all boards, committees and officers with a copy of all votes affecting them.

## § 106-10 Forms to be furnished.

The Clerk shall furnish blanks of the ordinary letter size, with appropriate headings, upon which petitions, reports, and other papers in the ordinary course of Town proceedings may be prepared.

## § 106-11 Endorsing and filing of documents.

The Clerk shall fold uniformly all papers and documents within the Clerk's department, and shall endorse them in a manner to indicate the contents of each paper, and shall file and arrange them in a manner convenient for reference and examinations and shall index all such papers and documents and all the records of the Town.

## § 106-12 Filing of materials related to Town Meetings.

All petitions for the insertion of articles in the warrant for a Town Meeting, and all papers and reports, whether printed or written, relating to such a meeting, shall be kept in separate files, with the words, "Regular Town Meeting" or "Special Town Meeting," as the case may be, with the current year plainly marked thereon.

## § 106-13 Book of reports.

Whenever any report of any board or committee is presented to the Town, and its recommendations are adopted, the Clerk shall cause to be entered upon the regular record of the meeting such portions of the report only, if any, as may be necessary for the understanding of the action taken by the Town, and the Clerk shall cause the report to be entered in full, in a book provided for the purpose, which shall be called the "Book of Reports." But if the report is in print it need not be so entered. The book of reports shall be duly indexed. The reports shall be numbered for each municipal year in the order of their presentation, and a marginal reference shall be made to the date of the meeting at which the report was presented. And the regular record of the meeting shall also make reference to the number of the report as entered in the book of reports.

## § 106-14 Annual report.

The Town Clerk shall publish in the annual printed report of the Town a summary of the proceedings of all Town Meetings that have been held during the preceding year.

## § 106-15 Appointment; duties.

The Select Board shall annually appoint an attorney-at-law to act as Town Counsel. It shall be the duty of the Town Counsel, when required by any Town officer, board of Town officers, or any committee of the Town, to furnish a written opinion on any legal question that may be submitted to Town Counsel in regard to any matter which concerns the Town officer, board of Town officers, or committee requesting the opinion, and Town Counsel shall at all times furnish legal advice to any officer of the Town who may require Town Counsel's opinion upon [a] subject concerning the duties incumbent upon said officer by virtue of their office.

## § 106-16 Annual report.

In the Treasurer's annual report the Treasurer shall state specifically the objects for which the debt of the Town was increased during the year preceding, and recite the votes under which the money was borrowed, and shall render a classified statement of all expenditures and receipts of the Town in such detail as to give a fair and full exhibit of the objects and methods of all expenditures.

## § 106-17 Salary; disposition of fees.

The Sealer of Weights and Measures shall be paid such annual salary as the Town from time to time may vote. The Sealer of Weights and Measures shall account for and pay into the treasury of the Town all fees received by the Sealer by virtue of office, but no fees shall be charged for services rendered under the provisions of Section 32 of Chapter 98 of the General Laws.

## § 106-18 Appointment; powers and duties; annual report.

The Select Board shall annually appoint a Town Engineer at a fixed salary, who shall have authority to appoint such assistants as the Town engineering work requires, subject as to number and compensation only to the approval of the Select Board. The Town Engineer shall have charge of all Town engineering, and no person connected with the Engineering Department shall engage in engineering work except for the Town of Marblehead, without prior permission in writing in each instance of the Select Board. The Town Engineer shall have charge of, and shall preserve, arrange and index, so as to be readily accessible to the public, all plans, surveys, field notes, records, documents, and inventories connected with the Engineering Department of the Town. The Town Engineer shall keep an accurate set of books showing in detail the cost of the Engineer's department and of all work done under the Engineer's direction, all materials purchased and of whom, and all vouchers, orders, and letters received and sent. The Town Engineer shall each year submit to the Select Board, in time for publication in the Annual Town Report, a statement showing in detail the work of the Engineering Department for the year, the names of all persons employed and a complete inventory of all Town property in that Department. The Select Board shall have the power at any time to remove the Town Engineer.

## § 106-19 Annual report.

The annual report of the Collector of Taxes shall contain a statement of the amount of taxes and assessments committed to the Collector for collection, the amount collected thereon together with the interest, the amount of abatements and discounts, the amount of uncollected taxes, and the amount paid over to the Treasurer.

## § 106-20 Disposition of moneys.

The Collector shall pay to the Town Treasurer all money received on account of taxes at least on or before the fifth of each month.

## § 106-21 Office hours.

The Collector shall have stated daily office hours for the transaction of business.

## § 106-22 Appointment; removal; records.

There shall be appointed annually by the Board of Health an Inspector of Gas Piping and Gas Appliances. Such appointment shall be made in March of each year and the Inspector shall hold office from the first day of April following for a term of one year or until a successor is appointed in the Inspector's place. The Board of Health may at any time remove the Inspector. The Inspector of Gas Piping and Gas Appliances shall keep a record of inspections.

## § 106-23 Duties.

A.
The Inspector's duties shall be the enforcement of the rules and regulations adopted by the board established under Section 12H of Chapter 25, General Laws.[1]
Editor's Note: Section 12H established the Gas Fitting Board and was repealed by St. 1977, c. 843.
B.
The Inspector shall be paid such annual salary as the Town from time to time may determine. The Inspector of Gas Piping and Gas Appliances shall account for all fees received by the Inspector by virtue of office, which shall, upon receipt, be paid into the Town treasury.

## § 106-24 Appointment.

The Select Board shall appoint a Director of Human Resources for a period not to exceed three years. The Director shall have the authority to appoint such employees as the Director's work requires, subject as to number and compensation only to the approval of the Select Board.

## § 106-25 Duties.

The Human Resources Department shall manage the personnel and benefits functions of the Town, including but not limited to: formulating and implementing personnel policies, employee relations, support for collective bargaining, wages and salaries administration, recruitment, monitoring workers' compensation, police/fire medical administration, benefits administration for Town employees and retirees, overseeing unemployment benefits, and managing personnel records.

## § 106-26 Appointment.

The Select Board shall appoint a Director of Community Development and Planning for a period of three years. The Director shall have the authority to appoint such employees as the Director's work requires, subject to number and compensation only to the approval of the Select Board.

## § 106-27 Duties.

The Director of Community Development and Planning shall manage the operations of and be responsible for a wide range of activities and duties, including but not limited to land use planning, housing policy, transportation projects, historic preservation, community development, sustainability efforts, open space conservation programs, administration of regulatory boards and neighborhood improvement efforts.
