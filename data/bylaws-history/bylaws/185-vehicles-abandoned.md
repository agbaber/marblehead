# Chapter 185: Vehicles, Abandoned

## § 185-1 Prohibited acts.

No person in charge or control of any real estate within the Town, whether as owner, tenant, occupant, lessee or otherwise, shall park, store, leave or allow any registered or unregistered motor vehicle of any kind which is in a wrecked, junked, partially dismantled or abandoned condition, whether attended or not, on such property longer than 60 days, unless a permit has been obtained from the Select Board; except this By-Law shall not apply with regard to a vehicle in an enclosed building. In granting such permit the Select Board shall consider the effect on the neighborhood and any nuisances or serious hazards.

## § 185-2 Penalty.

Any person violating any of the provisions of this By-Law shall be punished by a fine of $100 upon the first conviction thereof, by a fine of $200 upon the second conviction thereof, and by a fine of $300 upon the third and subsequent convictions thereof. Each day that such a violation is permitted to exist shall constitute a separate offense.
