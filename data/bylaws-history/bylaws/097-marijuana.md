# Chapter 97: Marijuana

## § 97-1 Limitation on number of licenses issued.

The number of marijuana retailers as defined by MGL c. 94G, §1, in the Town of Marblehead shall be limited to 20% of licenses issued in the Town of Marblehead for retail sale of alcoholic beverages not to be drunk on the premises where sold, which shall be calculated by rounding up to the next whole number.
