# Chapter 37: Buildings, Numbering of

## § 37-1 Enforcement.

The Select Board or their designee(s) shall enforce the provisions of this chapter.

## § 37-2 Responsibility to place numbers.

It shall be the responsibility of every owner and/or occupant of each house, building, and/or structure within the Town of Marblehead to place thereon the number of said house, building, or structure.

## § 37-3 Size, color and location of numbers.

The size, color, location, and visibility of said numbers shall be as follows:
A.
The minimum height of said numbers shall be three inches in height.
B.
In order to be visible from the road, street, or way, the number shall be of contrasting color and shall be reasonably visible to persons or vehicles approaching from either direction upon said road, street, or way.
C.
The number shall be affixed to the front door or as close to the front door as possible.
D.
Where there are multiple houses, buildings, or structures located off the road on private ways, private roads, or private lanes, the numbers shall be placed at the following locations:
(1)
The numbers shall be affixed to a post at the entrance to said private way, private road, or private lane.
(2)
If the house, building, or structure is so far off the private way causing the numbers to be unidentifiable from the private way, then a post with the numbers affixed thereto shall be placed at the entrance of the driveway leading to the house, building, or structure.

## § 37-4 Issuance of certificate of use and occupancy.

Each new building shall be identified in accordance with this chapter before a certificate of use and occupancy (is) issued therefor.

## § 37-5 Violations and penalties.

The penalty for any violation of this chapter shall be $50. Upon notice of violation, an owner shall correct such violation within 30 days. Each thirty-day period of noncompliance shall be deemed a separate offense.
