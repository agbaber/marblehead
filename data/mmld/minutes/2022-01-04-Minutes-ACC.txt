Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:30 PM

Minutes:

The Commission will vote on the November 30, 2021 minutes at their February meeting.


Bad Debt:
At the request of Finance Manager Barrett, Chairman Hull recommended to charge off
the 2021 bad debt schedule totaling $45,378.27 and noted that the department will
continue collection efforts on the accounts listed on the report. The list includes closed
accounts from 11/01/2019 – 10/31/2020.
Vote #2022-01
It was moved by Commissioner Frechette and seconded by Johnson to charge off the
department’s 2021 schedule of bad debts in the amount of $45,378.27. All in favor

Rate Presentation by UFS:
Dawn Lund, Vice-President of Utility Financial Solutions, LLC, made a presentation on
the cost-of-service study including financial projections and targets, cost of service
results, monthly customer charge and a sample rate design.
Chris Lund, Business and Technology Manager of Utility Financial Solutions, LLC gave
a presentation on rate design with an emphasis on time of use and a residential demand
charge. The Commissioners will forward General Manager Kowalik any questions in
regards to the presentations, then he will follow up with UFS and have them attend a
future meeting to discuss any questions.


General Manager Report:
General Manager Kowalik updated the Commissioner’s on any Covid developments.
MMLD is following Marblehead Department of Health’s guidance to where a mask
indoors, unless in the office alone, PCR tests are available to all employees and the lobby
continues to be open.
