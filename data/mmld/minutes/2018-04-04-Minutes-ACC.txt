Contract Negotiations:


Vote: #2018 - 19
Motion to adjourn to Executive Session under M.G.L. c. 30A, Sec. 21(a)(2), to conduct

General Manager Contracts:


Vote: #2018 – 20
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
contract with the new General Manager, Joseph Kowalik effective April 9, 2018 with an
annual salary of $160,000.00; the employment agreement will exist until a formal
contract can be drawn up by the Commission’s counsel, Rick Holland. All in favor


Vote: #2018 – 21
It was moved by Commissioner Homan and seconded by Commissioner Tumulty to
extend General Manager Andy Hadden to a 6 month’s contract extension with an annual
salary of $130,000.00, effective April 9, 2018. All in favor
