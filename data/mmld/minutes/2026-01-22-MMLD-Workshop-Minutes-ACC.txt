A quorum being present, Chair Yarmoff brought the meeting to order at 1:05 pm. The meeting was held

     Commissioners:             Commissioners Frechette, Harrington, Smith, and Yarmoff all participated in

                        Commissioner Hull was excused.
     Light Department:      All staff members of the Light Department were invited.

Strategic Plan / Priority List for MMLD
The board discussed previously the necessity to put in place a long-term strategic plan to guide the
actions of the Light Department. This process is expected to take several months to complete. For the
short term, it is important to have clear priorities for the department. The board decided to organize a
series of meetings, including this workshop, to create a priority list of actions for the next two years. The
process and the list will be reviewed at the January 27 board meeting, at a second workshop to take
place on February 10, and during the board meeting on February 24.

Workshop organization
The General Manager Jon Blair reviewed the objectives of this meeting, which was organized to gather
input and ideas from the staff of MMLD as the priority list is being developed. Five roundtables were
organized, with cross-functional MMLD team and board members discussing critical themes:
         - Safety and wellbeing of employees and customers;
         - Quality service to our customers and community;
         - Reliability through operations, infrastructure, and staffing;
         - Affordability and cost effectiveness;
         - Environmental responsibility and leadership.
A sixth roundtable was organized for participating members of the public, focusing on General threats
and opportunities to the Department.
Each participant was asked to generate ideas individually, then exchange with other members of their
roundtable. Each group then listed priority ideas and presented them to the whole group.

The meeting concluded at 2:53 pm.
