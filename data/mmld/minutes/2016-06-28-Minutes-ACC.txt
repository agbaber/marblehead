                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                               Tuesday, June 28, 2016
             40 Tioga Way, 3RD Floor, Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                         Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Homan, Kowalik,
Maccario and Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Manager Dugan, Finance
Assistant Barrett and Manager of Technical Operations Coleman

Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:20 P.M.

Minutes:
Vote: #2016 - 30
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
approve the minutes of the May 26, 2016 meeting. Hull, Kowalik and Tumulty in
favor; Maccario and Homan abstained

Minutes:
Vote: #2016 - 31
It was moved by Commissioner Kowalik and seconded by Commissioner Tumulty to
approve the minutes of the June 8, 2016 meeting. Hull, Kowalik, Maccario and
Tumulty in favor; Homan abstained

Financial Reports:
Finance Manager Dugan distributed to and discussed with the Commission the April
2016 operating statement and the May 2016 accrued operating statement.
The Purchased Power Adjustment for June 2016 remains at .14 cents per Kwh for all
customers.

General Manager Report:
General Manager Hadden updated the Commission on union contract negotiations and is
hopeful to have it finalized by the July meeting.
Hadden also informed the Commission that Wilkins passed the emissions test, but there
appears to be an issue with the sound as it is louder that it was before the upgrade. Peaker
will continue to diagnose this issue.

Business Office Hours:
Vote: #2016 - 32
It was moved by Commissioner Homan and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
adopt the following business hours effective July 1, 2016:
Monday, Tuesday and Thursday: 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM
Wednesday: 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM
Friday: 7:30 AM to 12:30 PM
Unanimous
