                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                          Tuesday, April 26, 2016
                    80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA

                                        Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioner’s Homan, Kowalik,
Maccario and Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Manager Dugan, Finance
Assistant Barrett and Manager of Technical Operations, Coleman


Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:00 P.M.

Commission Signatures:
Vote: #2016/18
It was moved by Commissioner Kowalik and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
authorize Commissioner Tumulty to sign payroll and accounts payable warrants while
Commissioner Hull is on vacation. Unanimous

Minutes:
Vote: #2016/19
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
approve the minutes of March 29, 2016 meeting. Hull, Kowalik, Maccario and
Tumulty in favor; Homan abstained


Financial Reports:
Finance Manager Dugan distributed to and discussed with the Commission the February
actual operating statement and the March accrued operating statement. In addition he
distributed the 2015 DPU report.
The Purchased Power Adjustment for April 2016 remains at .14 cents per Kwh for all
customers.

Bankruptcy:
Manager of Finance Dugan reviewed and discussed with the Commission a recent U.S.
Bankruptcy Court Order Discharge of Debtor for customer account #22-40844 for
$1,272.00 and has requested the commission to vote to charge off this amount as required
by law.

Vote #2016/20
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
charge off from accounts receivable for account #22-40844 for $1,272.00, as required by
U.S. Bankruptcy Case#15-13762 dated 01/04/2016.
Unanimous

The Purchased Power Adjustment for March 2016 remains at .14 cents per Kwh for all
customers.

General Manager’s Report:
General Manager Hadden updated the Commissioners that the staff would be moving to
40 Tioga Way on April 29 and the office at 80 Commercial Street will be officially closed
until the renovation is complete. The office will switch from Verizon to Comcast for
phone service as Verizon is on strike and not taking any orders. Peaker will be starting the
Wilkins foundation the first week of May. Hadden will meet with Matthew Ide of
MMWEC on May 9thth to discuss financing for the renovation.

Adjourn:
Chairman Hull requested a motion to adjourn.

Vote #2016/21
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
adjourn at 4:45. Unanimous
