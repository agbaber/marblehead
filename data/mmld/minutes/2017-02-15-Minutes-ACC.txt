           MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                      Wednesday, February 15, 2017
        40 Tioga Way, 3RD Floor, Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                      Minutes

Present for the meeting were: Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Kowalik, Maccario and
Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Assistant Barrett


Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:00 P.M.
Building Renovation:
The Commission discussed building permit fees being assessed by the Town for the
building renovation. GVW is scheduled to start by mid-February.


Wilkins:
The Commission discussed the Wilkins sound and emissions upgrade. Peaker has
wrapped one stack and the second scheduled for mid-February. Once completed, a sound
study will be scheduled, subject to DEP review. Emissions have already been approved.
General Manager Hadden will contact MMWEC to inquire about loss of revenue due to
the delay.


Vote #2017 - 10
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
adjourn at 6:20. Unanimous
