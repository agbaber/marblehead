           MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                     Wednesday, September 14, 2016
        40 Tioga Way, 3RD Floor, Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                        Minutes

Present for the meeting were: Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Kowalik, Maccario and
Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Manager Dugan, Finance Assistant Barrett,
Manager of Technical Operations Coleman


Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:15P.M.


Collective Bargaining Agreement:
Vote: #2016 - 46
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
approve the three year Collective Bargaining Agreement (07/01/2016 – 06/30/2019)
between the Marblehead Municipal Light Department and the IUE – CWA Local 81214
effective July 1, 2016, as presented by General Manager Hadden. Unanimous


Manager’s Report:
As a follow up to the August meeting, Hadden will inform Peaker Services that the
Commission is not willing to pay any percent, until Peaker provides a detailed plan and
timeframe to meet 100% of the contract’s specifications.


Hadden informed the Commission that he filed the ZBA and Planning Board’s decisions
regarding the building renovation with the Registry of Deeds. Dana Weeder will work on
the bid process with Becky Curran, Town Planner, and Rick Holland.


Adjourn:
Chairman Hull requested a motion to adjourn.
Vote #2016 - 47
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
adjourn at 5:13. Unanimous
