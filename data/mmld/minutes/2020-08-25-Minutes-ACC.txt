Financial Report:
Finance Manager Barrett forwarded to the Commission via electronic files of the June
2020 operating statement, the July 2020 accrued operating statement and the June 2020
power costs.


Through the end of June, power costs are $781K less than budget, while kWh sales are
1.1 M less than budget, sales revenue is 235k less than budget and operations expense is
291K less than budget through the end of July.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for August 2020 is 3.1 cents per Kwh for all
customers.
