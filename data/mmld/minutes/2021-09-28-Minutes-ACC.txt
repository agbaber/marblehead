Matt Ide, Executive Director, Energy & Financial Markets:
Matt Ide joined the meeting to discuss the proposed federal legislation, CEEP, Clean
Electricity Performance Program. The House Energy and Commerce Committee adopted
the proposal on September 15, 2021 but may hit some obstacles in the House and Senate.
The proposal increases clean electricity by 4% annually from 2023 to 2030. A penalty is
assessed at $40 per MWh for any short fall on nay clean electricity from the prior year.
Once 85% clean electricity is achieved no penalties will be assessed so long as the
percentage does not decrease from prior year.
Other bill provisions are the residential energy efficiency & electrification which includes
$9 billion for whole home energy retrofits, $5.5 billion for electrification for heat pumps
and $3.5 billion for weatherization.
$3.2 billion for critical facility modernization for educational buildings, public safety,
hospitals, municipal and state buildings and waste water treatment plants.
Zero- emission vehicle infrastructure includes $2 billion for charging equipment rebates,
$1 billion for a vehicle charging equity program, $500 million for state energy
transportation, $4 billion for DOE Plug-in electric drive vehicle program and $6 billion
for near term transportation electrification program.
Over $8 billion for transmission grants and loans
$2.8 billion for environmental reviews and solar.

Strategic Committee:
Commissioner Wolf requests that the Commission revise the Strategic Committee to

Commissioner Wolf suggests that we at least have a plan looking out about 8 years.
