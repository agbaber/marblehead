                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                               Tuesday, July 26, 2016
             40 Tioga Way, 3RD Floor, Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                         Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Homan, Kowalik and
Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Assistant Barrett and Manager of Technical
Operations Coleman

Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:08 P.M.

Minutes:
Vote: #2016 - 34
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
approve the minutes of the June 28, 2016 meeting. Unanimous

Solar:
John Arata, President of Solar Finance Strategies LLC, was present to update the
Commission on the feasibility of solar power more specifically, technical, regulatory and
financial for public land and buildings.
Brian Herr, Vice President of Solect, made a detailed solar presentation. He is currently
looking at the feasibility of putting an array on the rooftop at 21 Tioga Way. He
distributed an Annual Production Report from one of his recent projects located in
Norwood which has its own Municipal Light Department.

Financial Reports:
Finance Assistant Manager Barrett distributed to the Commission the May 2016 operating
statement and the June 2016 accrued operating statement.
The Purchased Power Adjustment for July 2016 remains at .14 cents per Kwh for all
customers.

Vote: #2016 - 35
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
transfer $30,000.00 from the Depreciation Cash Account to the Construction Cash
Account to cover recent expenditures as allowed by the DPU. Unanimous

Sick and Vacation:
Vote: #2016 - 36
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
prorate all sick and vacation balances by a factor of .9375. Unanimous

Meter Assessment:
Vote: #2016 - 37

Adjourn:
Chairman Hull requested a motion to adjourn.
Vote #2016 - 38
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Homan to
adjourn at 6:12. Unanimous
