General Manager’s Report:
Peaker will be completing the Wilkins upgrade tomorrow with a sound test scheduled for
this Friday. General Manager Hadden will follow up for DEP approval as soon as the
results of the sound study are available. He will also be scheduling winter capacity test as
soon as the temperature falls below 32 degrees.


Temp power has been set up for the building renovation. Garage has its own temp

building renovation. We will be billed $1,700.00 monthly for three rooms located on first
floor.


General Manager Hadden is hoping to have the Manager of Distribution position filled by
the end of March.
