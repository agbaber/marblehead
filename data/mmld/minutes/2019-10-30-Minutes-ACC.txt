Underground Electrical Service Policy:
1. Several residential areas serviced by older underground service have are now less reliable,
   and in need of replacement
2. The cost of new underground service in Marblehead is a multiple of overhead service.
   Replacing old underground service with new underground service should not be a given.
3. MMLD will meet with residents in effected areas to discuss overhead and underground
   options.
4. (Ocean Ave model) Residents can agree to pay for the incremental cost of providing new
   underground service, over the cost of new overhead service.
5. MMLD will work with local contractors to identify ways to lower costs for underground
   service e.g. annual contract agreements, xx linear feet/year.


MMLD 100% Renewable Energy Opt in Program
1. Today MMLD sells all Berkshire Wind RECs (Mass Class 1) on the open market. 2012-
   2018 average price is $43/MWH
2. MMLD proposes to open these REC purchases to customers.
3. Average residential meter is 700 kWh/m (0.7 MWH)
4. 2020 Berkshire Wind projected REC price $45/MWH
5. Average MMLD residential customer willing to pay an additional $45 x 0.7=
    $32/month will be certified by MMLD that their electricity is from a 100%
    renewable energy power source.
6. Simplify for our billing system and unknown number of participants
7. Specify an initial enrollment period. Dec 1- Jan 30.
8. Customers commit for 1 year; we will establish enrollment estimates based on avg
monthly usage: 600-700; 701-800; 801-900
9. Max participation for Berkshire Wind, based on avg annual RECs generated, is 275
residents…
10. REC pricing will also fluctuate during 2020. Final pricing will be in 2020 tbd…consider a
y-e true-up
