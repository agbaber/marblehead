       MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                        Thursday March 5, 2020
80 Commercial Street, Commissioner’s Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                       Minutes

Present for the meeting were: Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Homan, Maccario,
Johnson and Tumulty. General Manager Kowalik, Finance Assistant Barrett and Manager
of Technical Operations Coleman.


Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:00 P.M.


Minutes:
Vote #2020-5
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
approve the minutes of the January 29, 2020 meeting. Unanimous


Financial Report:
Finance Assistant Barrett distributed to the Commission the December 2019 Operating
Statement and the 2019 kWh sales and revenue and 2019 cost of power.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for March 2020 is 3.1 cents per Kwh for all customers.


General Managers Report:


Village 13 Substation:
Michael Barrett of PLM Electric Power Engineering reviewed three bids for the
procurement for two (2) 18/24/30 MVA 22.9 – 13.2kV power transformers to replace the
existing transformers at the Village 13 Substation. The following bids were received:
Virginia        $891,754
Delta Star      $1,151,940
ABB             $1,204,100
All three bidders were considered qualified vendors with Delta Star requesting 90%
payment at time of award, ABB requesting 30% progress payment during manufacturing
while Virginia Transformer had no payment stipulation.
Vote #2020-6
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
award the transformer contract to Virginia Transformer for $891,754 to be paid out of the
plant and equipment depreciation cash fund. Unanimous
Michael Barrett of PLM Electric Power Engineering reviewed three bids for the
procurement for one (1) 15kV switchgear assembly to replace existing switchgear at the
Village 13 substation. The following bids were received:
Eaton          $1,174,787
AZZ            $1,174,236
Myers          $1,232,500
All three bidders were considered qualified vendors. Eaton had an acceptable delivery
timeframe, AZZ had a delivery timeframe 10 weeks too late and Myers would not commit
to a timeframe.
Vote #2020-7
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
award the switchgear contract to Eaton for $1,174,787 to be paid out of the plant and
equipment depreciation cash fund. Unanimous


Marblehead Town Bootcamp:
The Town of Marblehead is running a program where various town departments are
meeting with interested residents to provide them with a better insight of the day to day
operations of their department. MMLD will attend the Bootcamp on Thursday, March
19.


NREL:
MMLD will schedule a meeting this Spring with MMWEC and NREL to discuss NREL’s
preliminary results which utilized NREL’s tool called REopt to evaluate the techno-
economic report for the potential of adding PV, wind, and battery storage to offset
MMLD’s energy costs.

Bell School:
MMLD will coordinate with the new Bell School architects to include plans for MMLD
Community Solar PV on the roof and EV charging in the parking lot.

Power Lines Tree Trimming Project:
General Manager Kowalik informed the Commission that Arborist Ben Staples of Cicoria
Tree Service Company will begin assessing trees around MMLD’s equipment on Friday,
March 6.


ISO-NE Tariff:
General Manager informed the Commissioners that ISO-NE may propose a fuel security
tariff that could cost MMLD 100K annually. MMWEC and ENE Services will continue
to monitor and update MMLD for any new developments.
Flood Mitigation:
General Manager Kowalik informed the Commissioners that he attended a
2030/2050/2070 flood forecast presentation by the Salem Sound Conservancy. Kowalik
will invite them to attend a Spring meeting.
