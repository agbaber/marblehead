Financial Report:


Finance Manager Barrett forwarded to the Commission via electronic files of the August
2020 operating statement, the September 2020 accrued operating statement and the
August 2020 power costs.


Through the end of August, power costs are $682K less than budget, while kWh sales are
300K less than budget, sales revenue is $79k less than budget and operations expense is
$452K less than budget through the end of September.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for October 2020 is 3.1 cents per Kwh for all
customers.
