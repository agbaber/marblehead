               MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                        Monday, November 2, 2015
                   80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA

                                       Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioners Homan, Kowalik,
Maccario and Tumulty, General Manager Hadden and Financial Assistant Barrett.

       1) Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:03 P.M.

          General Manager Hadden informed the Commission that Financial Assistant
          Barrett spoke to Matthew Ide from MMWEC regarding their financing option
          for the Building Renovations Project. We are in the pipeline for financing for
          2016. We just need to make a decision by this Wednesday on how much we
          are going to request.

          It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty, second by Commissioner Kowalik
          and unanimously.

              Vote #2015-50
              Voted: To go forward with and reserve 6 million of funding with
                     MMWEC for the Building Renovations Project.

       2) Chairman Hull proceeded with agenda item two – Motion to go to Executive
          Session to discuss Contracts. Not to return to Open Session.

          Chairman Hull took a poll vote of the Commission to go to Executive Session
          to discuss Contracts. Not to return to Open Session. Commissioner Tumulty
          – yes; Commissioner Kowalik – yes; Commissioner Homan – yes;
          Commissioner Maccario – yes; Chairman Hull – yes.

              Vote #2015-51
              Voted: Motion to go to Executive Session at 4:06 P.M. to discuss
                     Contracts. Not to return to Open Session.
