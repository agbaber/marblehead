Safety Minute – Slips, Trips, & Falls
                       • #2 common cause of workplace injury
                       • ~25% of workplace falls result in a
                         moderate or severe injury like deep
                         bruising, broken bones, or concussions
                       • Nearly 700 workplace fatalities per year
                         (15% of all workplace deaths)
                       • Seasonal hazards include:
                          • Slips due to ice, wet leaves, or spilled
                            gravy
                          • Trips on debris hidden by leaves, snow, or
                            poor visibility
                          • Falls while working aloft on holiday
                            decorations
