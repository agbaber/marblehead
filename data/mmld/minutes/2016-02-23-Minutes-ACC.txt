                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                         Tuesday, February 23, 2016
                    80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA

                                        Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioners Homan, Kowalik,
Maccario, Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Assistant Barrett


     Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:00 P.M.

     Minutes:
     Vote #2016-3
     It was moved by Commissioner Kowalik and seconded by Commissioner Homan to
     approve the minutes of January 26, 2016. Hull, Homan and Kowalik in favor;
     Maccario and Tumulty abstained.

     Vote #2016-4
     It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Homan to
     approve the minutes of December 22, 2015. Hull, Homan and Tumulty in favor;
     Kowalik and Maccario abstained.

     Solar Presentaion:
     John Arata, President of Solar Finance Strategies LLC and Charles Jenkins, CEO of
     Synergyenergy, and David Morrelli of Synergyenergy, attended the meeting to
     discuss the process of evaluating the feasibility of bringing solar power to the Town
     of Marblehead.
     Conversations will continue between Commissioner’s Tumulty and Kowalik, John
     Arata and Charles Jenkins regarding the feasibility more specifically, technical,
     regulatory and financial, across all Town assets.

     Financial Report:
     Finance Assistant Barrett did not distribute the December 2015 Operating Statement
     as the MMLD is currently in the middle of closing the books for 2015.

     The Purchased Power Adjustment for February 2016 remains at .14 cents per Kwh
     for all customers.


     Vote #2016-5
     Moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
     adopt M.G.L. Chapter 32B, Section 20, as amended by Chapter 68, Section 57 of
     the Acts of 2011, thereby establishing an Other Post-Employment Benefits Liability
     Trust Fund and to appropriate amounts to be credited to such OPEB Trust Fund,
     subject to the approval of the State Retirement Board Trust Fund Board of Trustees.
     Unanimous

    Vote #2016-6
    Moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
    make an initial investment of $1,400,000.00 to the State Retirement Board Trust
    Fund, the current balance of MMLD’s OPEB liability account subject to the
    approval of the State Retirement Board Trust Fund Board of Trustees. Unanimous


    General Manager’s Report:
    The MMLD received four bids for the removal of the generator located at 80
    Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA. One bid was disqualified due to an invalid
    submission specifically the bidder did not acquire the appropriate bonding.

    Vote #2016-7
    Moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Homan to award
    the bid to Byors & Sons, the lowest qualified bidder, for $59,875.00. Unanimous

    Wilkins Substation: Peaker completed the sound test last week and will begin work
    in two to three weeks.

    5 year budget: A meeting was held between the mangers to discuss the 5 year
    budget for projects. Another meeting will be held shortly to discuss any new
    construction and the operations budget. A five year budget work plan will be
    presented to the Commissioners at the conclusion of the 5 year budget process.

    Adjourn:
    Chairman Hull requested a motion to adjourn.

    Vote #2016-8
    It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario
    to adjourn at 6:15. Unanimous
