                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                          Wednesday, December 28, 2016
             40 Tioga Way, 3rd Floor, Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                        Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioners Homan, Maccario and
Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Manager of Finance Dugan and Manager of
Technical Operations Coleman.

     Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:05 P.M.

     Minutes:
     Vote #2016-58
     It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario
     to approve the minutes of November 29, 2016. Unanimous


     Financial Report:
     Manager of Finance Dugan distributed to and discussed with the Commission the
     October 2016 Operating Statement and the November 2016 Accrued Operating
     Statement.

     The Purchased Power Adjustment for December 2016 remains at .14 cents per Kwh
     for all customers.

     Bad Debt:
     Manager of Finance Dugan discussed the 2016 schedule of bad debts to be charged
     off this year. He recommended to charge off the entire schedule totaling $27,691.83
     and noted that the department will continue collection efforts on the accounts listed
     on the report.

     Vote #2016-59
     It was moved by Commissioner Homan and seconded by Tumulty to charge off the
     department’s 2016 schedule of bad debts in the amount of $27,691.83.

     General Manager’s Report:
     General Manager Hadden updated the Commission on the Wilkins Substation.
     Peaker and Stoddard have installed new pipes and the engines are running quieter
     and they are waiting to encase the horizontal pipes before conducting the sound test.

     General Manager Hadden updated the Commission on the building renovation.
     Dana Weeder and Becky Curran have reviewed the bids and met with GVW, who is
     the low bidder for the project.

     Vote #2016-60

    General Manager Hadden informed the Commission that thru MMWEC, we have
    been awarded a grant from the Department of Energy Resources for fifty percent
    reimbursement for the purchase of LED street lighting.

    is still pursuing going underground and could hire their own contractor to do the
    work.

    General Manager Hadden informed the Commission that he has been recently
    attending meetings regarding participating in the Peabody Project 2015A and will
    be looking for a vote to do so by February 3rd.

    Adjourn:
    Chairman Hull requested a motion to adjourn.

    Vote #2016-61
    It was moved by Commissioner Maccario and seconded by Commissioner Tumulty
    to adjourn at 5:15 P.M. Unanimous
