Present for the meeting were:
MMLD Commissioners – Hull, Homan, Johnson and Wolf
Selectmen: Belf-Becker, Nye, Grader and Dupree


Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:30 P.M.
Interviews were conducted with the following applicants to replace former MMLD
Commissioner Maccario who resigned in November 2020: Nathaniel Burke, Minora
Collins, Simon Frechette.
Each applicant was asked a question from both the Board of Selectmen and the Municipal
Light Board. After interviews a roll call vote was conducted for the appointment for
Commissioner Maccario’s remaining term which expires in June 2021.


Vote #2020-28
MMLD:
Homan – Burke
Johnson – Burke
Wolf – Frechette
Hull – Burke
Board of Selectmen:
Grader – Burke
Nye – Burke
Belf-Becker – Burke
Dupree – Collins


A majority of the total votes was necessary for the appointment; Nathaniel Burke is
therefore appointed as he received 75% of the vote. Mr. Burke will contact the Town
Clerk to be sworn in to serve as a Marblehead Municipal Light Commissioner and
complete the state ethics course which is required for all public employees and officials.


Vote #2020-29
Motion made by Commissioner Homan and seconded by Commissioner Wolf to adjourn
at 5:30 PM: Unanimous
