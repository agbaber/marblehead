Subcommittee:
Commissioner Wolf and Commissioner Burke will serve on the grid resiliency
subcommittee with an emphasis on renewable energy.


Lucretia and Joseph Brown Elementary School:
MMLD will facilitate the underground service from the school’s rear entrance on
Dartmouth to existing pole locations on Cornell with the intent to avoid setting 5 new
poles. The project will start on March 8 with a 2–3 week time frame weather dependent.


Hydro Quebec:
MMLD took a position with a 20-year transmission line support agreement between
MMWEC, National Grid, Vermont Electric and Eversource. Through the agreement,
MMLD will receive .75 MW of firm hydroelectric power or approximately 6,570 MWH
annually.


MMWEC Project 2020A:
MMLD and several other municipals engaged into a Power Sales Agreement with
MMWEC to build a 6.9 MW solar array on 30 acres in Ludlow, MA with a project
completion date of April 2022. MMLD portion of the project will be 10.9% or
approximately 1,450 MWH annually.
