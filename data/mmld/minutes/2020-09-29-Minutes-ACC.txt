Financial Report:


Finance Manager Barrett forwarded to the Commission via electronic files of the July
2020 operating statement, the August 2020 accrued operating statement and the July
2020 power costs.


Through the end of July, power costs are $798K less than budget, while kWh sales are
200K more than budget, sales revenue is 5k more than budget and operations expense is
336K less than budget through the end of August.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for September 2020 is 3.1 cents per Kwh for all
customers.


Mission Statement:

Vote: #2020 - 24
Motion moved by Commoner Homan and seconded by Commissioner Wolf to change
MMLD’s current mission statement to the following: The mission of Marblehead
Municipal Light Department (MMLD) is to provide reliable power as we work towards a
goal of a carbon free portfolio while maintaining competitive rates in a safe working
environment for MMLD employees and its customers. Unanimous
Commissioner Wolf’s Presentation “Promoting Energy Efficiency, Opportunities
and Cost Benefits”:
Commissioner Wolf gave a detailed presentation emphasizing the following points:


   •   Promoting Efficiency is economically advantageous for both MLPs and
       customers:
               1) lowers MLP net operating costs from load shifting
               2) lowers customer energy use.
   •   Efficiency helps reduce GHGs.
   •   Flattening the Curve is a form of Energy Efficiency
   •   EVs, batteries
   •   ToU rates can all help flatten the curve
