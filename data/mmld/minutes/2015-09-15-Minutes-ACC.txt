               MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                       Tuesday, September 15, 2015
                   80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA

                                       Minutes

Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioners Kowalik and Maccario,
General Manager Hadden and Business Assistant Stone.

      1) Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:07 P.M. and proceeded with agenda
         item one – Approve minutes of the August 27, 2015 meeting.

          There were no minutes to approve.

          General Manager Hadden informed the Commission that we have a contract
          with Alan Dennis’s Structural Engineering Company for services to design a
          new roof over the Commercial Street Generator at a cost of $7,700.00

          General Manager Hadden informed the Commission that we have a contract
          with Geotechnical to survey the building for hazardous materials inside and
          outside at a cost of $9,000.00 and $8,700.00 respectively.

          General Manger Hadden informed the Commission that he purchased a new
          SUV which was delivered today that he will use and Assistant General
          Manager Snow will get his old vehicle as the older one was traded in.

      2) Chairman Hull proceeded with agenda item two – Motion to go to Executive
         Session to discuss personnel issues. Not to return to Open Session.

          Chairman Hull took a poll vote of the Commission to go to Executive Session
          to discuss personnel issues. Not to return to Open Session. Commissioner
          Maccario – yes; Commissioner Kowalik – yes; Chairman Hull - yes.

             Vote #2015-43
             Voted: Motion to go to Executive Session at 4:12 P.M. to discuss
                    personnel issues. Not to return to Open Session.
