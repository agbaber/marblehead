# MMLD Light Commission minutes, 2015-2026

Text extractions (`pdftotext -layout`) of all 186 board-minutes and
board-presentation PDFs posted on
https://marbleheadelectric.com/meetings-annual-reports.html as of
2026-07-07. Coverage: January 2015 through March 2026. Pre-2015 minutes
are not posted by the department.

Each `.txt` corresponds to a source PDF at
`https://marbleheadelectric.com/pdfs/minutes/<YYYY>/<filename>.pdf`
where `<filename>` is the `.txt` basename. Three source files contain
literal spaces in their names on the server (2020-08-25, 2023-03-28,
and the 2026-01-22 strategic-planning workshop); their local names have
the spaces stripped.

PDFs are committed only for documents cited directly on
`/light-department.html` (the Wilkins mitigation award and resume
letter, and the 2025 general-manager transition votes); everything else
is text-only to keep the repo small, matching the `data/schools/`
convention. Re-fetch any PDF from the URL pattern above.

Counts by year: 2015 (18), 2016 (14), 2017 (17), 2018 (17), 2019 (10),
2020 (11), 2021 (15), 2022 (18), 2023 (14), 2024 (14), 2025 (26),
2026 (12 through March).
