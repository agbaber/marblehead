Maccario Resignation:
Commissioner Maccario submitted a letter of resignation to Chairman Hull as he is going
to retire and move away from the area. The Commissioners thanked Commissioner
Maccario for his years of service as a Commissioner and wish him well with his
retirement. General Manager Kowalik will send him a letter of appreciation for his
service. There will be a joint meeting scheduled in December between the Board of
Selectmen and the MMLD Commissioners for the sole purpose of appointing a new
Commissioner who will serve out the remainder of Commissioner Maccario’s term which
expires in May 2021.


Financial Report:


Finance Manager Barrett forwarded to the Commission via electronic files of the
September 2020 operating statement and power costs and the October 2020 accrued
operating statement.


Through the end of September, power costs are $687K less than budget, while kWh sales
are 400K less than budget, sales revenue is $109k less than budget and operations
expense is $489K less than budget through the end of October.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for November 2020 is 3.1 cents per Kwh for all
customers.


Hydro Quebec:
Matthew Ide, MMWEC Executive Director, Energy & Financial Markets informed the
Commissioners that on October 30, 2020, MMWEC signed the anticipated power
agreement with Hydro Quebec in which MMWEC reserves a portion of its contractual
transmission rights to purchase carbon-free power for its participating municipal utilities.
The five-year power purchase transaction will allow participating MMWEC member
municipal utilities to flow clean power down the transmission line. MMLD will receive
6,570 MWH of carbon free energy approximately 6% of MMLD’s power purchases.
