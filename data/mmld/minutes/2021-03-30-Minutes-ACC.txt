80 Commercial St Plant:
Dennis Dale of Dale Designs attended the meeting to discuss upgrades to the 80
Commercial Street Plant including site access, egress, drainage, fencing and gates.
The use of the driveway is dangerous to pedestrians with utility trucks coming in and out,
and because of the slope of the driveway and access to walls and curbing, kids are using
the property for biking, rollerblading and skateboarding. Dale Designs will provide a
quote to MMLD to address these issues.


General Manager’s Report:


Mass Climate Bill:
General Manager Kowalik discussed the new Mass Climate Bill signed by Governor
Baker. All generation must be 50% non-carbon emitting by 2030, 75% non-carbon
emitting by 2040 and net zero emissions by 2050.


Brown Elementary School:
The linemen have finished the underground work for the new Brown Elementary School.


Public EV Charges:
Voltrek has completed the installation of two dual charging stations at the Mary Alley
Building and Roundhouse Road.
Wind Initiatives:
General Manager Kowalik informed the Commissioners that the Footprint Plant located
on Salem Harbor are considering maintaining their DPA (Designated Port Area) status to
potentially be used to build turbines for Atlantic wind farms.


80 Commercial Street Flood Mitigation:
General Manager Kowalik discussed the possibility of applying for coastal resilience
grants to fortify the seawall surrounding the 80 Commercial St Plant.


Village 13 Transformer Replacement:
General Manager Kowalik met with SESD engineer to discuss the delivery of the
transformers over Marblehead Sewer Department infrastructure.
