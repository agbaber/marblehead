Results of MEAM Customer Survey
    •   MEAM is the association of General Managers of Municipal Light Plants in Massachusetts
    •   Strong overall results for MMLD with high marks for reliability, trust, and rates, outperforming
        many peers and investor-owned utilities.
    •   Feedback highlighted need to expand conservation and innovation efforts.
    •   Survey skewed toward older, higher-income respondents.
                                                                                                      2/3
MMLD Financial Performance & 2026 Budget
   •   Favorable revenue performance was offset by higher power costs, which were driven by
       summer demand.
   •   Financial outlook remains stable with a focus on maintaining modest positive operating income.
   •   MMLD continues to deliver competitive, stable rates, despite broader industry cost pressures.
   •   Long-term challenge noted: flat sales and limited commercial load, alongside rising peak
       demand pressures which increase costs.

Capital Planning & Funding
   •   2026 capital plan reflects a disciplined, prioritized investment strategy.
   •   Focus on maintaining adequate depreciation fund levels to support infrastructure needs.
   •   Balance between operating reserves and capital funding remains a key consideration.

System Resiliency (Transmission Project)
   •   Multi-year effort is underway to strengthen and modernize transmission infrastructure.
   •   Project aims to improve reliability, reduce risk, and enhance community assets
   •   Specifically, the utility corridor lines on the Salem branch of the rail trail: the project will include
       phased upgrades and undergrounding, supported in part by external funding (DOE Grant).

Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)
   •   Strategic project will be located at Village 13 substation. Project in partnership with Lightshift
       and Massachusetts Municipal Wholesale Electric Company (MMWEC).
   •   Expected to provide operational and financial value, including demand management benefits.
   •   Key considerations include permitting and long-term decommissioning risk protections.
   •   Coordination with various stakeholders in the community has been actively ongoing: Fire Chief,
       Water Department, Conservation and Planning departments.
