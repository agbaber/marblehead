MMLD Sustainability Fund: This memo was presented for discussion, but no formal
policy has been adopted



Background:       •   MMLD currently sells a majority of its Class 1 RECs ($120,000 in 2021);
                      and thereby does not retain the environmental attributes of this energy;
                  •   Consequently, MMLD’s ‘clean energy’ portion of its energy supply is
                      <0.1%, as compared with a 16 % mandated requirement of IOU’s in 2021;
                  •   Previously MMLD rejected the Green Communities requirement of adding
                      a $0.0005/kWh surcharge on customer’s bills, thereby forfeiting
                      Marblehead’s access to $250,000 in grant funds designed to give assistance
                      for community efficiency and electrification initiatives
                  •   MMLD has cited concerns about ‘in perpetuity’ payments to the Green
                      Communities fund, and the regressive nature of the surcharge
                  •   The sum of the sale of RECs plus the avoided surcharge = $170,000

Proposal:
                  •   Establish a dedicated MMLD Sustainability Fund;
                  •   Deposited Funds will be equal to the amount from sold REC’s plus the
                      forfeited Green Communities grant monies
                  •   Amount will be approximately $370,000
                  •   Funds will be directed to sustainability initiatives, which will be prioritized
                      and selected by a Sustainability Working Group, composition to be
                      determined.
                  •   Possible initiatives:
                          o Community solar generation facilities;
                          o Power storage for peak electric use times;
                          o Infrastructure such as public charging stations for electric vehicles,
                               and other similar programs;
                          o Residential rebates/Incentives for electrification and efficiency.
Models:
                  •   Ipswich - $265,000/yr - “we treat our Sustainability Fund very similarly to
                      the Depreciation Fund. Each year we expense $265K against a specific
                      line in our operating budget and deposit the cash into a dedicated, restricted
                      account. We then create a standalone budget to forecast and track the
                      spending of that cash account.”
                  •   Wellesley – 4% of residential monthly bills – goes to “Wellesley's
                      WECARE program - a utility initiative that funds local renewable energy
                      projects to reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and preserve the
                      planet. Residents are automatically enrolled.”
                  •   Hingham - from sale of class 1 RECs minus what is spent on purchase of
                      class 2 RECs to cover the entire portfolio.
