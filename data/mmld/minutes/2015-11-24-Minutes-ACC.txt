                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                        Tuesday, November 24, 2015
                    80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA

                                       Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioners Homan, Kowalik,
Maccario, Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Assistant Barrett and Special
Assistant Jolly

     Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:00 P.M.

     Minutes:

     Vote #2015-52
     It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and second by Commissioner Maccario to
     approve the minutes of October 27, 2015. Unanimous

     Vote #2015-53
     It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and second by Commissioner Maccario to
     approve the minutes of November 2, 2015. Unanimous

     Financial Report:

     Finance Assistant Barrett distributed to and discussed with the Commission the
     September 2015 Operating Statement and the October 2015 Accrued Operating
     Statement.

     The Purchased Power Adjustment for November 2015 remains at .16 cents per Kwh
     for all customers.

     General Manager’s Report:

     General Manager Hadden updated the Commission on the building renovation and
     the Wilkins Substation.

     MMWEC Project 2015A:

     Vote #2015-54
     It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and second by Commissioner Kowalik
     that the Marblehead Municipal Light Commission, hereby agrees to participate in
     MMWEC’s Special Project 2015A, (Peabody Peaking Project.) Unanimous
