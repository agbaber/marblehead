           MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                        Tuesday, January 10, 2017
                       RD
        40 Tioga Way, 3 Floor, Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                        Minutes

Present for the meeting were: Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Homan, Kowalik and
Tumulty, General Manager Hadden, Finance Assistant Barrett, Manager of Technical
Operations Coleman


Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:05 P.M.


Vote: #2017 - 1
Motion to adjourn to Executive Session under M.G.L. c. 30A, Sec. 21(a)(10), votes may
be taken, and if they are votes will be released at a time deemed appropriate by counsel.
The Commission will reconvene in open session.
The Commission was polled: Tumulty – yes, Kowalik - yes, Homan – Yes, Hull - yes.
Executive session commenced at 4:06 PM.
The Commission reconvened to open session at 5:00 PM.

Building Renovation:
Dana Weeder of Winter Street Architects and Billy Grant of North Shore Consultancy
attended the meeting to update the Commission on the building renovation. Mr. Grant
will serve as the Project Manager. The building renovation is set to begin during
February and expected to be completed by the end of the year.


Vote #2017 - 2
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
adjourn at 6:15. Unanimous
