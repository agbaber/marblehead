Village 13:
General Manager Kowalik has been holding weekly meetings with PLM Engineer, Mike
Barrett, MMLD Engineer, Colin Coleman, MMLD Distribution Manager William
Jackson and former MMLD General Manager, Bob Jolly to discuss replacing Village 13
transformers and switchgear. Request for bids in the next two weeks. A delivery time
frame of about 50 – 60 weeks with the intent to be operational by end of 2023.


2050 Roadmap to Net Zero:
The Commissioners had a lengthy discussion on their roadmap to 2050. Commissioner
Frechette suggested that the Commission lead by example by establishing specific
ambitious goals to decarbonize by 2040. General Manager, Joe Kowalik, requested of
Matt Ide (MMWEC), who was attending the meeting remotely, to create a roadmap for
both 2040 and 2050 for the Board’s consideration. Commissioner Wolf requested that the
Roadmap take into account an increase in demand of at least doubling over that time
period. The Commission will continue the decarbonization dialog at their next meeting.
