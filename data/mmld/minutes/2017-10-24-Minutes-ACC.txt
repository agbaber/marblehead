Financial Report:
Finance Assistant Barrett distributed to the Commission the August 2017 Operating
Statement and the September 2017 Accrued Operating Statement.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for October 2017 remains at .16 cents per Kwh for all
customers.


Building Renovation Update:
Billy Grant and Yura Zazulin of North Shore Consultancy, Mike Fields of Winter Street
Architects updated the Commissioners on the building renovation. They presented
GVW’s target milestone schedule. The current target date of substantial completion and
certificate of occupancy is slated for mid-February 2018. They also distributed a
summary of delays most notably the generator and soil, ledge and brick removal for the
elevator slab.
John Livermore:
John Livermore, of Sustainable Marblehead, a centralized group of Marblehead citizens
who want to take actions on issues of energy and climate change came back with a
revised presentation on Sustainable Marblehead’s vision for the town. Their goal is to
turn Marblehead into becoming a Massachusetts Green Community and adoption of Mass
Power Forward’s target for the town to be 100% powered by renewable energy by 2035.
The commission has some reservations on supporting this resolution for 100%
renewables by 2035, specifically the inclusion of a target date and the target percent. The
commission will work with NREL and MMWEC to craft their own position with regard
to a renewable energy policy.
