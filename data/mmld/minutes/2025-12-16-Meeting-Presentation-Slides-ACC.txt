Customer Survey Results

 In October 2025, MMLD conducted a customer survey in cooperation with the Municipal Electric
  Association of Massachusetts (MEAM) and Great Blue Research
 Results are overwhelmingly favorable and align well with expectations
 Responding customers’ demographics suggest reporting bias favoring established homeowners with
  relatively high income
 Curious reporting on customers’ intent to adopt EVs and/or rooftop solar arrays, may suggest that these
  are polarizing feelings or that early adopters have already purchased EVs and/or solar arrays
 MMLD could improve its outreach and programming and incentives, especially those related to helping
  customers conserve electricity
 Useful insights were gained regarding customers’ preferred methods of payment and communication;
  this feedback will enable MMLD to serve our customers more effectively

Key Financial Takeaways

 The operating budget of MMLD is very lean; net income has risk due to influence of below-the-line
  influences
 Rates do not need to be increased to support operating budget; we do not anticipate making changes to
  the power purchase adjustment, but that is subject to our (limited) exposure to energy market volatility
 Maintaining a depreciation rate of 3% with augments from operating reserves is sufficient to fund
  MMLD’s ambitious capital plan

Transmission Resiliency Project Summary

Fortification of Marblehead’s power supply will be conducted in three distinct project phases:
 Phase 1: Converting several of MMLD’s wooden poles to galvanized steel near the National
  Grid substation in Salem
 Phase 2: Undergrounding 1,800 feet of the sub-transmission lines along the most rural span of
  the utility corridor in Marblehead
 Phase 3: Undergrounding the remaining 3,900 feet of lines along the utility corridor

Phase 3               $1,982,467            100%                August 2026 – TBD

Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Proposal

 Developer: Lightshift (via MMWEC)
 Size: 5MW/20MWh
 Location: Village Substation
 Anticipated value for MMLD customers: ~$500k/year (~45% of total savings
  from avoided transmission and capacity costs)
 Governed by three main documents:
  • Land use agreement (site terms)
  • Interconnection agreement (electrical terms)
  • Energy storage service agreement (financial terms)
