Present for the meeting were:

Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:30 PM
Minutes:
Approval of minutes for meeting of 7/27/2021 and 8/3/2021 were deferred to the
September meeting.

2021 Financials:
General Manager Kowalik and Finance Manager Barret updated the Commissioners on
the YTD financials. The YTD revenue through June exceeded the budget by $585,000
while power costs were under budgeted by $316,000. The higher revenue and power cost
are attributed to June having well above normal temperatures.


Rate Study:

Vote #2021-27
Commissioner Homan moved and seconded by Commissioner Johnson, to engage in a
contract with UFS, Utility Financial Solutions, LLC, 185 Sun Meadow Court, Holland,
MI, to provide a cost of service and rate design study for $49,900.00 which will include
the original scope of $26,900 plus $6,000 for new renewable energy rate classes for
REC’s and solar, $9,000 for time of use analysis, $4,000 for power cost adjustment and
an additional $4,000 for any participation in public meeting. Unanimous


Building Renovations:


Vote #2021-28
Commissioner Wolf moved and Commissioner Johnson seconded to engage in a contract
with GRLA, Gorman, Richardson and Lewis Architects for construction documents and
construction administration services for the repair to the existing building envelope of the
switchgear room located at 80 Commercial St for a fee limit of $29,900 including
reimbursable expenses. Unanimous Estimated cost of remediation will be
approximately $153,400 to $188,000. MMLD is actively pursuing their legal options to
recover costs of remediation


Project 2015A Update:
DPU issued a favorable ruling. If the DPU decision is not appealed, MMWEC will move
forward with bond financing.


NEPPA Conference:
General Manager Kowalik and Commissioner Wolf attended the annual NEPPA
conference held in Portland Maine on August 22 – 25. They report that the line-up of
speakers was excellent including Paul Hibbard, former DPU Commissioner, Gordon Van
Weile, ISO New England President & CEO, Joy Ditto, APPA President & CEO, Meguire
Whitney, NEPPA Lobbyist, Brad Bradshaw President of the Massachusetts Hydrogen
Coalition and Velerity Management Consulting, and Arshad Mansoor, Electric Power
Research Institute, President & CEO
