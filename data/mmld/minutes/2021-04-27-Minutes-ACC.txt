2015A Peaking Plant:
Nick Scobbo, MMWEC Attorney, Ronald DeCurzio, MMWEC Chief Executive Officer,
Matt Ide , MMWEC Executive Director, Energy & Financial Markets, Jason Viadero,
MMWEC ,,, attended the meeting to discuss the importance of the 2015A Project, a
natural gas and oil hybrid peaking generator, specifically as it relates to MMLD’s
current capacity supply obligation (CSO) portfolio in the Northeast Massachusetts and
Boston Load Zone (NEMA). The 2015A project would satisfy 7-8% of MMLD’s current
open capacity position or exposure of 50%.
Eileen Matheau and Jean-Jaques Yarmoff presented Sustainable Marblehead’s position
to use renewable electric generation as an alternative to the proposed generator with an
emphasis on battery storage charged by renewable assets. Sustainable Marblehead also
took issue with the lack of transparency on the proposed project. Sustainable requested
the following from MMLD: 1) The minutes from the board meetings where Project
2015A was discussed; 2) Details of the NPV of Project 2015A, specifically, what is
Marblehead’s obligation annually, and how much will be reimbursed by the capacity
payments; and 3) The costs of an alternative battery energy storage system for peak
demand, based on current costs per kWh, calculated from 2020 data from EIA (US
Energy Information Administration) or from 2020 NREL data.
General Manager’s Report:
General Manager Kowalik updated the Commissioners that the new ChargePoint EV
charging stations at the Mary Alley parking lot and on Roundhouse Road will be
energized as soon as the line markings are complete. He also informed the
Commissioner’s that their will be 16 parking spaces designated for EV charging at the
new Brown Elementary School and that the roof is designed to support a photovoltaic
array.
General Manager informed that MMLD will be sponsoring Article #45 on the 2021 Town
Warrant as follows: To see if the Town will approve the change of use from school
purposes to the electric light purposes and then transfer the care and custody and control
of town-owned land, parcel 146-33-0, listed in the Assessors Department records as 21
Lincoln Avenue, to the Marblehead Municipal Light Department. The unimproved
parcel is bordered by Tioga Way and Hoods Lane.
