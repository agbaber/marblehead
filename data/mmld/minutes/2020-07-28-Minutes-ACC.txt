Financial Report:
Finance Manager Barrett forwarded to the Commission via electronic files of the May
2020 operating statement, the June 2020 accrued operating statement and the May 2020
power costs.


Through the end of May, power costs are $734K less than budget, while kWh sales are
1.7 M less than budget, sales revenue is 325k less than budget and operations expense is
245K less than budget through the end of June.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for July 2020 is 3.1 cents per Kwh for all customers.


ENE Strategies:
Vincent Ragucci, III, Senior Executive Vice President of Energy New England, LLC,
provided the Commissioners with a update of pending bills at the State House that impact
municipal light plant operations, specifically bills regarding the percent of power that
must be generated from clean energy sources.


The Commission will request MMWEC to present a summary of potential new renewable
energy projects and projects now underway at their August meeting.
MMLD Mission Statement:
The Commissioners discussed MMLD’s current mission statement. The current mission
statement on the MMLD website reads, “The mission of the Marblehead Municipal Light
Department (MMLD) is to provide, subject to applicable laws and regulations, reliable
and efficient electrical power at fair, reasonable and competitive rates to its ratepayers in
a safe working environment for its employees and the town of Marblehead.:
After a brief discussion, the Commission decided to work on the language and will take
up the issue at its August meeting.
