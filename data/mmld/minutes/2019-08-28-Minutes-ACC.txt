General Manager’s Report:
NREL Renewable Energy Optimization:
Andy Walker and Douglas Gagne from National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)
made their much anticipated field visit from August 26 -28 to explore tax-exempt parcels
in Marblehead and a couple of parcels in Salem that are owned by the Marblehead Water
and Sewer Department for potential renewable energy generation with an emphasis on
solar, not to rule out other renewable initiatives. MMLD looks forward to receiving
NREL’s analysis and recommendations sometime in the Fall .
