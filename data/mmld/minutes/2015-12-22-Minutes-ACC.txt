                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                         Tuesday, December 22, 2015
                    80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA

                                        Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioners Homan, Tumulty, General
Manager Hadden, Manager of Finance Mark Dugan, Finance Assistant Barrett

     Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:00 P.M.

     Minutes:
     Vote #2015-57
     It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and second by Commissioner Homan to
     approve the minutes of November 24, 2015. Unanimous


     Financial Report:
     Manager of Finance Dugan distributed to and discussed with the Commission the
     October 2015 Operating Statement, the November 2015 Accrued Operating
     Statement and the 2014 Audit performed by Goulet, Salvidio and Associates, P.C..

     The Purchased Power Adjustment for December 2015 remains at .16 cents per Kwh
     for all customers.

     Bad Debt:
     Manager of Finance Dugan discussed the 2015 schedule of bad debts to be charged
     of this year. He recommended to charge off the entire schedule totaling $38,664.65
     and noted that the department will continue collection efforts on the accounts listed
     on the report.

     Vote #2015-58
     It was moved by Commissioner Homan and seconded by Tumulty to charge off the
     department’s 2015 schedule of bad debts in the amount of $38,664.65.

     General Manager’s Report:
     General Manager Hadden updated the Commission on the building renovation and
     the Wilkins Substation. There will be a pre-bid meeting on the Wilkins Substation’s
     noise abatement and pollution control at the Wilkins Substation on Wednesday,
     December 30 at 10:00 AM, 7 Woodfin Terrace, Marblehead, MA 01945. The
     Commission will open bids on Friday, January 8 at 1:00 PM, Commissioner’s
     Meeting Room, 80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA 01945.

     General Manager Hadden informed that he has been in contact with Maryann Perry,
     Superintendent of Marblehead School District, about using space at the Eveleth
     School during the building renovation period. They will meet in a couple of weeks
     to discuss further.
