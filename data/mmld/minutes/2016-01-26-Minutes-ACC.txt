                MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                         Tuesday, January 26, 2016
                    80 Commercial Street, Marblehead, MA

                                         Minutes


Present for the meeting were: Chairman Hull, Commissioners Homan and Kowalik,
General Manager Hadden, Finance Assistant Barrett

     Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:03 P.M.

     Winter Street Architects:
     Dana Weeder presented an update of the building renovation project. We should
     have an estimator within 2 to 3 weeks. There is a consensus to move forward with
     the project while we wait for the estimator. Actual construction slated to begin late
     April to early May.

     Minutes:
     Minutes of the December 22, 2015 meeting will be approved at the February
     meeting as Commissioner Kowalik was not present for that meeting leaving only
     two Commissioners to vote.


     Financial Report:
     Finance Assistant Barrett distributed to and discussed with the Commission the
     November 2015 Operating Statement, the December 2015 Accrued Operating
     Statement. Barrett informed that the December profit of $1,512,000 will be offset
     by OPEB and the additional 2% depreciation.

     The Purchased Power Adjustment for January 2016 remains at .14 cents per Kwh
     for all customers.

     General Manager’s Report:
     General Manager Hadden updated the Commission on the Wilkins Substation. We
     received one bid from Peaker Services Inc. totaling $1,198,886.00 for the Wilkins
     Substation’s noise abatement and pollution control project.
     Vote #2016-1
     It was moved by Commissioner Homan and seconded by Commissioner Kowalik to
     award the Wilkins Substation’s noise abatement and pollution control project to
     Peaker Services Inc. for $1,198,866.00. Unanimous

     General Manager Hadden informed that he met with Maryann Perry, Superintendent
     of Marblehead School District, about using space at the Eveleth School during the
     building renovation period. Hadden stated that the property is not suitable for many
     reasons, ie.. lack of space, bathrooms and air conditioning.
