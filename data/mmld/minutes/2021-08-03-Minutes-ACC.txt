            MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                      Tuesday August 3, 2021
                   9 Maple Street, Marblehead MA

                                        Minutes
Present for the meeting were:
MMLD Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Frechette and Wolf, General Manager Kowalik


Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 9:00 A.M.

Town Meeting Article 45 School Property Transfer:
The Municipal Light Commission and the Marblehead School Board held a joint meeting
where the Marblehead School Board unanimously voted to transfer the care, custody and
control of their property located at 21 Lincoln Avenue to the Town of Marblehead. The
original Article 45 read as follows: To see if the Town of Marblehead will approve the
change of use from school purposes to electric light purposes, then transfer care and
custody and control of Town owned land, parcel 146-33-0, listed in the Assessing
Department records as 21 Lincoln Avenue, to the Marblehead Municipal Light
Department. The unimproved parcel is bordered by Tioga way and Hoods Lane.
