       MARBLEHEAD MUNICIPAL LIGHT COMMISSION
                     Wednesday December 18, 2019
80 Commercial Street, Commissioner’s Conference Room, Marblehead, MA

                                        Minutes

Present for the meeting were: Commissioners – Chairman Hull, Homan, Johnson and
Tumulty, Finance Assistant Barrett, Manager of Technical Operations Coleman



Chairman Hull opened the meeting at 4:00 P.M.


Minutes:
Vote #2019-30
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Johnson to
approve the minutes of the November 25, 2019 meeting. Unanimous.


Financial Report:
Finance Assistant Barrett distributed to the Commission the October 2019 Operating
Statement and the November 2019 Accrued Operating Statement.


The Purchased Power Adjustment for December 2019 remains at .27 cents per Kwh for
all customers.

Bad Debt:
Finance Assistant Barrett discussed the 2019 schedule of bad debts to be charged off this
year. He recommended to charge off the entire schedule totaling $22,827.68 and noted
that the department will continue collection efforts on the accounts listed on the report.

Vote #2019-31
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Johnson to charge off the
department’s 2019 schedule of bad debts in the amount of $22,827.68.

Sustainable Marblehead:
John Livermore of Sustainable Marblehead presented their Town of Marblehead Climate
Action Plan, a strategy for a carbon-free Marblehead by 2040. We thank Sustainable
Marblehead for their presentation and their determination to combat climate change.
Representation from MMLD, Green Marblehead Committee and Sustainable Marblehead
will meet after January 1, 2020 to begin the process of creating a vision and plan to guide
the Town in its carbon emission reduction mission.
