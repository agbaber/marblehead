Thoughts for MMLB meeting Jan 27, 2026

       Submitted by Eileen Mathieu, 44 Longview Drive, Marblehead

       Sustainable Marblehead’s Clean Energy and Public Policy working group



Thank you for the invitation to the public to join the goal setting retreat at the library on Jan
22, to focus on your 2 yr goals.


I realized that perhaps we didn’t affirm the following points

       1) Great job that MMLD does in any outage and with the professionalism of its
          employees when dealing with the public
       2) Great job that MMLB does in guiding the decisions and policies of the dept so
          that we achieve our Town adopted goals of Net Zero by 2040. The Board is
          responsive to the significant public support for Net Zero and for cleaner
          electricity
             This takes the form of purchasing decisions for MMLD ‘s energy portfolio to
             move away from fossil fuels whenever possible, (that focus and effort needs
             to continue for the next 2 years as well as the next 14 years.)
       1) Your support of electrification by the following steps you have taken;
             a. Hiring Michael Hall and his encouraging teamwork between Sustainable
                  Marblehead and MMLD on the Green Homes Tour
             b. His efforts on public education and his newsletter
             c. Cosponsoring events, such as the March 11 joint event on “the future of
                  renewable energy” ;other sponsors include, Sustainable Marblehead,
                  MMLD, League of Women’s Voters, Abbot Public Library
             d. Support of energy coaching program started by Sustainable Marblehead

       2) Final point: we didn’t make this point at the retreat:

               MMLD has a big role in helping Marblehead become a Green Community,
               which will result in many grants to the Town of MHD. We need MMLB to vote
               to levy the Renewable Energy Charge, once the Town becomes compliant
               with 3A zoning, in order to meet the final criterion to become a Green
               Community.
