Bringing MMLD’s near-term vision into focus

inspire and guide the decisions and actions we take in the future

What, how, and why…

                                           Mission: What do we do?

                  What                       • Our stated mission is to “provide reliable power as we work
                                               toward a goal of a carbon-free portfolio, while maintaining
                                               competitive rates in a safe working environment for MMLD

                  How                          employees and its customers.”

                                           Core Values: How do we achieve our mission?
                                             • Safety and wellbeing of employees and customers
                   Why                       • Reliability through operational efficiency and effectiveness
                                             • Environmental responsibility and leadership
                                             • Quality service to our customers and community
                                             • Affordability and cost effectiveness
                                           Vision: Why?
                                             • In contrast to mission, a vision is intended to establish a
                                               common direction, inspire people, and guide decisions --
                                               Where are we going and why should we go there?
