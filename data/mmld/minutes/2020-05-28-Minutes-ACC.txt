Marblehead Neighbors Helping Neighbors:
This program would be targeted for Marblehead residents, who are experiencing
economic hardship due to Covid-19 job loss or prior economic hardship. The program
will make some level of payment toward a past due electric bill for a resident suffering
from economic hardship. MMLD will partner up with North Shore Community action
Program (NSCAP) who will administer the Marblehead Neighbors Helping Neighbors
Hardship Partnership Fund on behalf of MMLD.
Vote #2020-13
It was moved by Commissioner Tumulty and seconded by Commissioner Maccario to
make a seed funding of $5,000.00 to the North Shore Community Action Program
(NSCAP) subject to a program design that does not violate any Chapter 164 rate setting
provisions. Unanimous
NREL:
GM Kowalik made a detailed presentation with the remote assistance of NREL
employees Doug Gagne and Andy Walker and MMWEC Employees Matthew Ide and
Stephen Smith to discuss NREL’s results which utilized NREL’s tool called REopt to
evaluate the techno-economic report for the potential of adding PV, wind, and battery
storage to offset MMLD’s energy costs and to increase renewables in MMLD’s energy
portfolio. MMLD will continue their discussion with NREL and utilize their findings
with assessing any potential renewable investments. A summary of the NREL report will
be made available on MMLD’s website.
