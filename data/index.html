Debt & taxes

How has Marblehead managed its debt?

$116.5M outstanding, 50 of 51 voter-approved exclusions since 1988, AAA credit rating. Where the debt came from, how an exclusion works, and the forward payoff schedule through FY2044.

What does Marblehead spend on retirement?

The pension, on a fixed schedule to be fully funded by 2036, and retiree health, a $142M promise that is about 3% funded. How much of every dollar goes to former employees, and how both compare to other Massachusetts towns.

What relief can seniors claim?

Circuit Breaker credit by income and home value. Only 418 of an estimated 1,158 eligible seniors claimed the credit in 2022.

How do I apply for the senior exemption?

Up to $2,000 off a fiscal 2027 tax bill, from a $200,000 pool. Eligibility, documents, the November window, and what the town has not published yet.
