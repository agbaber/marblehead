## Why bucket sums do not equal $49,120,285

Two separate effects explain the gap:

**Effect 1 — Fixed Charges are included in the $49,120,285 grand total but excluded from the 6 buckets.**

The packet's grand total includes District 5000 Fixed Charges ($875,015 FY26 / $892,626 FY27)
and Athletics 5000 Insurance ($8,500 / $8,670). These are excluded from the 6 buckets per the
task definition (health insurance, retirement, Medicare are budgeted at town level, not school-side).
Combined excluded amount: $883,515 FY26 / $901,296 FY27.

**Effect 2 — The $49,120,285 uses post-reduction entity totals, not pre-reduction section subtotals.**

Each school's budget page shows a level-funded gross, then lists ADDITIONS/REDUCTIONS, then a
final post-reduction total. The BUDGET TOTALS summary (line 1290-1299) uses those post-reduction
figures. Total per-entity reductions across all entities: approximately -$1,614,943 (FY27) plus
a Retirements offset of -$182,000.

**Reconciliation for FY27:**

- Pre-reduction section subtotals (all, incl Fixed Charges): $50,917,231
- Per-entity reductions: -$1,614,943
- Retirements offset: -$182,000
- Grand total (post-reduction): $49,120,288 ≈ $49,120,285 (rounding, $3 gap)

**For FY26:** The per-entity FY26 totals sum to $49,494,718 but the BUDGET TOTALS line shows
$49,120,285. The difference of $374,433 is not explained by an explicit line in the text extract
for FY26 (the Retirements line is blank for FY26 in the packet). Most likely the FY26 column
in the BUDGET TOTALS block represents the adopted FY26 budget after mid-year amendments or
transfers not captured in the per-school level-funded FY26 columns shown in the detailed pages.
Treat the BUDGET TOTALS grand total ($49,120,285) as authoritative for FY26; treat the
per-section FY26 figures as the extractor's row-level inputs.

## Mapping decisions and ambiguities

**School-level 2000 bundles regular + SPED instruction.** Each school's
`2000: INSTRUCTIONAL SERVICES TOTAL` includes both regular-ed teachers and SPED teachers at
that building. The packet does NOT provide a school-level DESE 2000-regular vs 2000-SPED
subtotal; those would require summing individual line items (SPED salary lines have OBJ codes
510100/510302 with PROJ 500). For Panel 2's purposes, the school-level 2000 sections are mapped
entirely to the "Regular instruction" bucket, with the rationale that the dominant cost at each
building is general education. The SPED specialists, coordinators, and OOD tuitions that are
distinctly district-managed appear in the District's 2000 and 9000 sections, mapped to "Special
education."

**District 2000 is mapped to Special education, not Regular instruction.** The District's
`2000: INSTRUCTIONAL SERVICES TOTAL` ($3,020,548 FY26 / $3,118,082 FY27) consists of:
instructional coaches, summer programs, K-12 SPED faculty and OT/PT/SLP/BCBA staff, IDEA-offset
lines, instructional assistants, professional development, and curriculum supplies. Although it
also contains lane-change reserves and curriculum coaches, the dominant cost and DESE intent is
SPED service delivery. Mapping to "Special education" is defensible. Alternative: split it with
the instructional coaches going to "Regular instruction."

**District 3000 includes SPED transportation.** The District's `3000: STUDENT SERVICE TOTAL`
($1,474,762 FY26 / $1,575,971 FY27) is mostly SPED OOD transportation (bus drivers, OOD transport
contracts, $1,237,480 OOD transport FY26), plus homeless/DCF transport, attendance, health, and
security. This is mapped to "Student services" (DESE 3000) rather than split into Operations.
The task definition places transportation under "Operations (DESE 4000)" but the packet codes
transportation inside 3000. Flag this for the extractor author: if Panel 2 is meant to isolate
transportation under Operations, District 3000 needs to be re-coded manually — it cannot be
extracted at the section-total level.

**MHS 9000 (Recovery High School tuition, $22,000) is mapped to Special education.** This is a
tiny amount for an alternative placement. Could reasonably go to Regular instruction.

**Athletics 5000 Insurance ($8,500 / $8,670) is EXCLUDED.** Fixed Charges category. The
remaining athletics budget ($637,895 / $654,767) is entirely Student services (3000).

**District 4000 includes technology staff and equipment.** The District's
`4000: OPERATION & MAINT TOTAL` ($3,937,229 FY26 / $4,039,901 FY27) contains custodians,
maintenance, utilities, vehicle/fuel costs AND the technology helpdesk salaries, technology
contracted services, equipment, and copiers. The technology lines total approximately $637,000
FY26. These are coded under 4000 in the packet. Mapped to "Operations" as-is.

**Capital (District 7000) is small.** $50,900 FY26 / $51,918 FY27, just two line items
(DISTRICTWIDE EQUIPMENT and DISTRICTWIDE EQUIPMENT/REPL). This is the only 7000 section
in the packet; no per-school capital appears at the section level.

**Two passes of content in the packet.** Lines 1-1299 are the summary/first pass;
lines 2260+ repeat the same content (likely the detailed appendix). Section totals appear twice
with the same values. The extractor should use the first-pass figures (lines 513-1258 range).
One exception: Village and MHS second-pass TOTAL lines show the pre-reduction figure, not the
post-reduction, so the second pass does NOT replace the first-pass data for those.
