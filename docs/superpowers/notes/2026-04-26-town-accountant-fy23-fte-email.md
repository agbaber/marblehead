Hello,

I'm a Marblehead resident working on a civic data project at marbleheaddata.org that compiles and explains primary-source town financial data. I have a specific question about the ACFR's statistical-section schedule titled "Full-time Equivalent Town Employees by Function" (FY23 ACFR page 138, FY24 ACFR page 128).

Between the FY22 and FY23 reports, the Education line on this schedule went from 482.69 to 537.00 FTE, a one-year increase of about 54 positions. The same year, three other things changed in the schedule:

1. Reported FTE values switched from decimals (e.g., 482.69, 94.58, 20.97) to whole or half FTE (537.00, 91.00, 19.00) for FY23 forward.
2. The "Public safety" and "Fire" rows in earlier ACFRs were merged into a single "Public safety" row, with prior years restated.
3. General government, public works, human services, and culture/recreation each declined 1–4 FTE in the same FY23 column. Total town FTE rose from 668.50 to 709.00.

The FY22-to-FY23 change in education FTE doesn't appear to reflect 54 new hires: General Fund education salaries grew only 2.6% the same year (about $34.4M to $35.3M), well below what 54 net new positions would have cost. That suggests a counting or data-source change rather than real hiring.

I haven't found a methodology footnote on this schedule explaining what changed. I'd like to describe it accurately on the public site rather than guess.

Two questions, whichever you're best positioned to answer:

1. What changed between FY22 and FY23 in how the Education line on this schedule is compiled? Did the data source change (e.g., from departmental headcount reports to an HR/payroll system extract)? Were positions previously reported under other functions (or not reported at all) added to Education?
2. Does the schedule include positions paid out of revolving funds (Circuit Breaker, PK/K Tuition Revenue, Building Rental Revenue, Educatius), federal grants (IDEA, ESSA), and the Local Education Account, or only one of these? The FY27 budget packet shows positions being moved between these funding sources during budget construction, so the question of which funds are counted matters.

If a paragraph or two of explanation works better than email, I'm happy to take a phone call or attribute a quote on the site. The goal is a clear, accurate description that residents can read alongside the underlying ACFR.

Happy to narrow this further if helpful, and thank you for taking time on a question that probably has an obvious answer once you see it.
